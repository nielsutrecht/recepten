# Lemon cheesecake oats

Servings: 1 | Calories: 481kcal | Protein: 45g | Carbs: 50g | Fat: 11g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**CHeesecake filling**

- 40g rolled oats
- 160g 0% fat Greek yoghurt
- 20g light cream cheese

**(brand: Philadelphia lighter)**

- 65ml almond milk juice of ½ a lemon
- 1 tbsp 0-cal sweetener of choice (I used stevia)
- 1 tsp lemon extract
- (optional - but takes the lemon flavour to another
- level! - sub with a little more lemon juice)
- 25g vanilla protein powder (I use Protein Works)

**topping**

- 1 digestive light (brand:
- McVities - sub with graham crackers) lemon zest

## Instructions

1. In a mixing jug, combine the oats, Greek yogurt, almond milk, 20g of cream cheese, a tablespoon of zero-calorie sweetener, lemon juice, lemon extract and vanilla protein powder. Stir until well mixed. In a mason jar or container, layer half of the oat mixture followed by a layer of crushed digestive biscuit. Repeat this layering once more. Seal the container and refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight. Enjoy from the fridge when you're ready!

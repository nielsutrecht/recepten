# cinnamon Cereal oats

Servings: 1 | Calories: 395kcal | Protein: 45g | Carbs: 37g | Fat: 10g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**cinnamon filling**

- 40g rolled oats
- 130g 0% fat Greek yoghurt (brand: Fage)
- 30g light cream cheese (brand:

**Philadelphia lightest)**

- 110 ml low-fat milk (I used skimmed milk)
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 25g vanilla protein powder (I use Protein Works French vanilla)
- 1 tbsp brown stevia (brand:
- pure via) pinch of salt (enhances flavours)

**toppings**

- dusting of cinnamon between each layer (mainly for aesthetics lol)
- 5g cinnamon cereal of choice (I

**used cinnamon coated wheat**

- puffs as a low calorie option)

## Instructions

1. In a mixing jug, combine oats, Greek yogurt, milk, cream cheese, cinnamon, brown stevia, a pinch of salt and vanilla protein powder. Stir until everything is well mixed. Pour the oat mixture into a mason jar or container, and top with your cinnamon cereal of choice and a sprinkle of cinnamon. Seal the jar and store it in the fridge for at least 2 hours, or overnight. Enjoy!

# Crispy Beef & Cheese Tacos

Servings: 12 | Calories: 219kcal | Protein: 26g | Carbs: 6g | Fat: 9g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**taco beef**

- 800g lean ground beef (5% fat or less)

**taco seasoning (home-made recipe below)**

- 1 medium diced white onion
- 80g enchilada sauce (brand: Old El Paso)
- Splash of water (around 60ml)
- Cooking spray (I used avocado oil)

**taco seasoning**

- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp cayenne
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp oregano
- 2 tsp sea salt
- 1 tsp black pepper

**Creamy enchilada sauce**

- 60g enchilada sauce (or salsa)
- 150g 0% fat Greek yoghurt
- 1.5 Tsp honey
- 1/2 tsp cayenne Salt to taste

**Creamy enchilada sauce**

- 60g enchilada sauce (or salsa)
- 150g 0% fat Greek yoghurt
- 1.5 Tsp honey
- 1/2 tsp cayenne Salt to taste

## Instructions

1. Add the lean ground beef to a hot pan with a little cooking spray and cook over medium-high heat until fully browned. Once cooked through, add the taco seasoning and diced white onion. Stir well and cook for another 2–3 minutes until the onion softens and everything is coated in the spices. Pour in the enchilada sauce along with a splash of water (about 60ml) and stir to combine. Let it simmer for a minute, then remove from the heat. To make the creamy enchilada sauce, simply mix together all the ingredients in a bowl until smooth. Adjust seasoning to taste and set aside. To assemble, start with warm mini tortillas. Spoon 1/12th of the taco beef (weigh the full mixture and divide evenly) onto one half of each tortilla, then top with 25g of low-fat grated cheese. Fold each tortilla in half and place on a baking tray. Repeat for all 12 tacos. Lightly spray the tops with cooking oil and sprinkle with a touch of smoked paprika for colour. Bake at 200ºC/400ºF for 8–10 minutes or air-fry at 180ºC/355ºF for 8–10 minutes until crispy and golden. Serve hot with the creamy enchilada sauce on the side. To store, allow to cool completely and transfer to a paper towel-lined container. To reheat, airfry or pan-toast until heated through and crispy again. Enjoy!

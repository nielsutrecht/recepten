# sticky Beijing beef rice bowls

Servings: 4 | Calories: 526kcal | Protein: 56g | Carbs: 53g | Fat: 10g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Crispy beef strips**

- 800g lean steak sliced into thin
- strips against the grain! (top sirloin or flank)
- 20ml soy sauce
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 20g cornflour
- 5g baking soda (tenderises the beef) cooking spray

**beijing beef glaze**

- 60ml soy sauce
- 120ml water
- 2 tbsp sriracha
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp hoisin
- 1 tbsp sweetener ( I used granulated sweetener)
- 5g cornflour mixed with a tbsp of water

**other ingredients**

- 200g white rice (uncooked weight)
- 2 garlic cloves (diced)
- 1 medium red bell pepper (sliced into 1” pieces)
- 1 medium white onion (halve and slice into 1/4’s)

## Instructions

1. flavors the beef but also tenderizes it. Ensure the beef is well-coated, then leave it to marinate briefly. Before you start cooking prepare the Beijing beef glaze. In a separate bowl, whisk together soy sauce, water, sriracha, oyster sauce, hoisin, your preferred sweetener, and a slurry of cornflour with water. Heat a pan over a high heat and coat with cooking spray. Add the beef strips, frying them until they’re golden and crisp. Remove them from the pan and keep them aside. In the same pan, sauté the onions and bell peppers until they soften. Add the garlic and continue to stir for two more minutes, allowing the aroma to develop. Pour in the glaze and let it simmer gently until it starts to bubble lightly. Reduce the heat, and as the glaze thickens, reintroduce the crispy beef. Stir to coat the beef evenly with the glaze. Portion the glazed beef and cooked rice into four meal-prep containers. These can be stored in the refrigerator for up to five days. When you're ready to eat, add a teaspoon of water over the top and microwave partially covered for 3-4 minutes, Enjoy!

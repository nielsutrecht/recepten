# Huge fluffy Garlic butter naan

Servings: 1 | Calories: 332kcal | Protein: 14g | Carbs: 52g | Fat: 7g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**Fluffy naan**

- 65g 0% greek yoghurt
- 70g self-raising flour sub with plain flour and
- ½ tsp baking powder
- 5g light butter

**garlic butter glaze**

- 10g light butter
- ½ tsp garlic paste
- ½ tsp dried parsley

**Notes You can sprinkle some**

- reduced fat grated cheese into the dough for a cheesy garlic naan and
- this will boost the protein too! Just remember to

**track this accordingly :)**


## Instructions

1. In a bowl, add the greek yoghurt and self-raising flour, then mix together with a fork until you get doughy clumps, at which point you can knead it into a dough with your hands. Avoid over-handling the dough with your palms to avoid it getting sticky - you can add a little more flour if needed (but try not to as this will add calories) Once you have a dough, use any leftover flour in the bowl to sprinkle on your kitchen worktop or a large flat surface. Rub a little flour on your rolling pin too, then roll out the naan to about 4"-5" across and 7"-8" long. In a small microwave safe bowl, add the butter and microwave for 30 seconds to melt. Once melted, add the garlic paste and dried parsley. Mix to combine. Now heat up a pan to high heat, melt in some butter and cook the naan for 2-3 minutes on each side. Once cooked, using a brush, coat both sides of the naan in the garlic butter glaze. Serve hot and Enjoy!

# air fried Apple crisp dessert

Servings: 1 | Calories: 280kcal | Protein: 18g | Carbs: 52g | Fat: 0g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**Cinnamon apples**

- 1 large apple
- 3 tsp honey sprinkle cinnamon vanilla protein ice cream (I made mine in the Creamy but you can use the store bought)

## Instructions

1. TOppings sprinkle of granola (I actually used soy crispies for extra protein - they are from Amazon)

2. Slice a large apple in half and use a spoon to scoop out the core from each side. Place the halves cut-side up in your air fryer basket and drizzle with honey. Sprinkle generously with cinnamon. Air-fry at 200ºC/400ºF for 15 minutes until soft and caramelised around the edges. Once cooked, transfer to a bowl and top with two scoops of protein ice cream — either homemade (like a Ninja Creami blend) or your favourite store-bought option. Finish with a sprinkle of granola or soy crispies for added crunch and a little extra protein. Serve warm and enjoy immediately!

# Chicken & bacon caesar wrap

Servings: 1 | Calories: 488kcal | Protein: 52g | Carbs: 29g | Fat: 14g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**Main ingredients**

- 150g (1) chicken breast
- 45g smoked back bacon
- 1 low calorie tortillas (brand: Weight Watchers)
- 50g shredded lettuce
- 25g sliced shredded tomato juice of ½ a lemon
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp salt and pepper low cal cooking spray

**Caesar sauce**

- 25g light mayo
- 15g Parmesan
- ½ tsp Worcestershire sauce
- ½ tsp garlic salt
- 1 tsp black pepper dash of lemon juice
- 5-10ml of water (for consistency)

## Instructions

1. Slice the chicken breast into thin strips and season with garlic powder, salt, black pepper and squeeze of lemon juice. Before cooking make the caesar sauce by combining light mayo, parmesan, Worcestershire sauce, garlic salt, black pepper a dash of lemon juice, water for consistency and mix well with a spoon. To a medium high heat add some cooking spray and cook the chicken for 6-8 minutes till you get a golden sear. Set aside and in the same pan (with no added oil) fry the back bacon on a high heat till crispy. Once the bacon is done, set it aside and place the low calorie tortilla in the same pan allowing to toast for 30 seconds in the bacon juices (this does not add calories since it’s already included with the bacon - but it does add flavour!) assemble the wrap with the shredded lettuce, sliced tomatoes, cooked chicken and the crispy bacon. Drizzle over the caesar sauce and fold into a wrap, Enjoy!

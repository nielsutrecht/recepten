# Chicken & Halloumi gyros

Servings: 1 | Calories: 496kcal | Protein: 58g | Carbs: 39g | Fat: 9g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**Main ingredients**

- 1 greek style flat bread (brand: Tesco)
- 150g chicken breast
- 50g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 30g reduced fat halloumi (brand: Aldi)
- 50g shredded lettuce Half a red onion
- ½ juice of lemon

**tzatziki sauce**

- 75g 0% fat Greek yoghurt
- ¼ medium cucumber
- 1 tsp garlic paste
- 1 tsp fresh dill (chopped) juice of half a lemon salt to taste

## Instructions

1. Cut chicken into thin slices and add to a bowl with 45g 0% fat Greek yoghurt juice of 1/2 a lemon, oregano, Tsp salt and garlic powder. Then mix till fully coated and marinate for 30 minutes in the fridge Thinly slice red onion and shred the lettuce. Add to a bowl and wash both with cold water (this helps add crunch and takes the edge off of the pungent onion flavour). Set aside. Slice the cucumber in half, remove the seeds and finely dice up. Then wrap in paper towel and squeeze to remove any excess water. Add the diced cucumber to a bowl with Greek yoghurt, garlic paste, fresh dill, lemon juice and salt to taste Then mix well to form your high protein Tzatziki! Heat a pan to medium-high heat with some low cal cooking spray, add the marinated chicken and cook for 6-8 minutes till you get a golden sear. Set the chicken aside, add the sliced halloumi, frying 1-2 minutes till golden on each side, then slice the cooked halloumi slices into sticks. Assemble the Gyros with the shredded lettuce, sliced red onions, chicken, halloumi and a generous dollop of tzatziki, wrap in foil or parchment paper to hold together and and enjoy your high protein gyros!

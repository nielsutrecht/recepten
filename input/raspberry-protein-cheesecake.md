# raspberry protein cheesecake

Servings: 8 | Calories: 170kcal | Protein: 10g | Carbs: 18g | Fat: 7g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**raspberry cheesecake**

- 1 (12g) sugar free raspberry jelly sachet (brand: Hartleys)
- 1 cup boiling water
- 3 cups 0% fat cottage cheese (blended)
- ½ cup. 0 cal sweetener (I used stevia)

**biscuit base**

- 9 light digestive biscuits sub with 135g of graham

**crackers (adjust tracking accordingly)**

- 3 tbsp melted butter

- Begin by dissolving the sugar-free raspberry jelly sachet in a cup of boiling
- water, ensuring it's completely mixed. Let this cool to room temperature.

## Instructions

1. In a blender, add 3 cups of 0% fat cottage cheese with 1/2 cup zerocalorie sweetener. Blend for at least 2 minutes until completely smooth. Once the jelly mixture has cooled, add it into the blender with the cottage cheese and blend once more to combined. For the biscuit base, crush 9 light digestive biscuits (or 135g graham crackers) into a fine consistency. Mix the crushed biscuits with 3 tablespoons of melted butter and press firmly into the bottom of your cheesecake pan to form the base. Pour the cheesecake mixture over the biscuit base in the pan and smooth the top with a spatula. Chill in the refrigerator (making sure it’s level) for at least 6 hours, or overnight, to allow it to set. Once set, your low calorie and high protein raspberry cheesecake is ready. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days and enjoy!

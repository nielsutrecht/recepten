## Instructions

1. add cooking spray and cook the beef until it's golden brown and thoroughly cooked. For the low-fat sour cream, mix Greek yogurt, light cream cheese and lemon juice in a small bowl season with salt to taste. Then spread the sour cream over the centre of a low calorie tortilla, followed by the cooked ground beef, two slices of light cheese, shredded lettuce and diced tomatoes. Place a toasted mini tortilla on top of the lettuce, then fold the edges of the larger tortilla towards the center, to form a pentagon/hexagon shape. Carefully place the crunch wrap in a hot pan, mini tortilla side down, and toast for 1-2 minutes to seal it, then flip and toast for another minute till crispy. Serve it hot and enjoy!

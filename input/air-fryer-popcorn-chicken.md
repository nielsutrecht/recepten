# air fryer popcorn chicken

Servings: 1 | Calories: 461kcal | Protein: 37g | Carbs: 25g | Fat: 24g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 200g boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 25g cornflour
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp olive. oil
- Notes Chicken thighs are an absolute cheat code for great tasting and juicy chicken! well worth the few extra calories.

## Instructions

1. Dice the chicken thighs into small, popcorn-sized pieces and place them in a small bowl. Add olive oil, cornflour, paprika, onion powder, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Feel free to add any additional seasonings you prefer. Mix well until the chicken is fully coated. Arrange the coated chicken pieces in an air fryer or on a baking tray. Cook at 200°C (400°F) for 10 minutes in the airfryer or 12 minutes in the oven, flipping halfway through to ensure even crispiness. Serve immediately with your favourite condiments. Enjoy your lower-calorie crispy popcorn chicken!

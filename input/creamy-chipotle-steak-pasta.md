# Creamy chipotle steak pasta

Servings: 4 | Calories: 583kcal | Protein: 55g | Carbs: 68g | Fat: 10g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**chipotle steak bites**

- 650g lean steak diced (I used rump steaks with the fat
- trimmed - top sirloin or flank are also great)
- 20g chipotle paste (brand:

**Waitrose)**

- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp dried coriander/cilantro
- 2 tsp salt juice of 1 lime

**Creamy chipotle pasta**

- 300g penne pasta (uncooked weight)
- 100ml reserved pasta water
- 260ml low fat milk (I used 1%)
- 150g light cream cheese (brand:

**Philadelphia lightest)**

- 25g grated parmesan
- 2 garlic cloves
- 40g chipotle paste
- 20g honey
- 1 tsp salt
- other ingredients Fresh chopped cilantro (garnish)

**cherry tomatoes (optional)**


## Instructions

1. Dice the lean steak into cubes, then season with garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, oregano, dried coriander/cilantro, salt, chipotle paste, and the juice of 1 lime. Mix until the steak is fully coated, then add it to a pan with cooking spray and sear on high heat for 3-4 minutes until golden brown and tender. Set aside and keep warm. Next, set your pasta on to boil in some salted water. While that's cooking, in a blender cup, add low-fat milk, light cream cheese, parmesan, 2 garlic cloves, chipotle paste, honey, and salt to taste. Blend until smooth and creamy. Reserve 100ml of pasta water before draining, then add the creamy chipotle sauce to a pan along with the cooked pasta and pasta water. Bring to a light bubble, then reduce the heat and stir until the sauce thickens and the pasta is fully coated. Distribute the chipotle pasta and steak bites into four equal servings in meal prep containers, and garnish with chopped cilantro. Optional: serve with a side of cherry tomatoes for extra veg! Store refrigerated for up to five days. To reheat, add a teaspoon of water and microwave partially covered for 3-4 minutes. Enjoy!

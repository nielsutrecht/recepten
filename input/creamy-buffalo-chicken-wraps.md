# Creamy buffalo Chicken wraps

Servings: 3 | Calories: 466kcal | Protein: 59g | Carbs: 27g | Fat: 14g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**Main ingredients**

- 600g chicken breast
- 3 low calorie wraps (brand: weight watchers)
- 1 cup fresh chopped spinach
- 1 tsp Paprika
- 1 tsp Garlic powder
- 1 tsp Onion powder
- 1 tsp Salt & pepper
- 15ml olive oil

**creamy buffalo sauce**

- 230g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 80g Light cream cheese (Brand: Philadelphia lighter)
- 90g Buffalo hot sauce

**(brand: franks red hot Tesco)**

- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder

## Instructions

1. Season the chicken breast with smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt and pepper and a drizzle of olive oil. rub till well coated then bake or air fry at 200°C (400°F) for 15 minutes Meanwhile make the creamy buffalo sauce by adding to. a blender Greek yoghurt, light cream cheese, buffalo sauce, onion powder and garlic powder. Blend into a smooth and creamy consistency. Once the chicken is cooked, dice it into cubes and add it to a bowl with the creamy buffalo sauce and washed freshly chopped spinach. Toss everything together till the chicken is well coated. Then assemble the wraps by adding 1/3 of the creamy buffalo chicken to each wrap and rolling up tightly. Wrap the burritos in foil and store in the freezer for up to two months. To reheat, remove the foil, wrap in a damp paper towel and microwave on a plate for 5-6 minutes, flipping over at halfway, unwrap carefully from the paper towel and enjoy! Optionally toast in the pan after reheating to get it crispy!

# Crispy chicken katsu fried rice

Servings: 4 | Calories: 565kcal | Protein: 55g | Carbs: 66g | Fat: 9g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Crispy chicken katsu**

- 700g (4) medium chicken breasts
- 100g breadcrumbs (80g for
- tracking 20g per breast) brand: paxo
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 egg
- 50ml egg white Cooking spray

**katsu sauce**

- 100g carrots (diced)
- 1 medium white onion (diced)
- 1 tsp garlic purée
- 1 tsp ginger purée
- 1 tbsp curry powder
- 1 tbsp brown stevia
- 400ml chicken stock

**easy fried rice**

- 600g day old cooked white rice (200g uncooked)
- 2 eggs
- 1 whole egg white
- 120g frozen mixed veg
- 25ml soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 10ml sesame oil

## Instructions

1. Start by flattening out the chicken breasts with a meat mallet or rolling pin. Then in one bowl, beat together one egg and egg whites. In a separate bowl, mix breadcrumbs with paprika, salt, and black pepper. Dunk the chicken breasts first in the egg mixture, then in the breadcrumbs, pressing down firmly for an even coating. Spray the coated chicken breasts with cooking spray. Then air fry at 200°C (400°F) for 14-16 minutes, or oven bake at 180°C (375°F) for 18-20 minutes. For the katsu sauce, to a pan on medium heat with cooking spray add diced carrots and onion in a pan and cook till soft. Once soft, add garlic and ginger puree, curry powder, brown stevia, and chicken stock. Bring that to a light bubble then simmer for 10 minutes. before adding the mixture to a blender and blitzing on high until completely smooth. For the fried rice, add frozen mixed vegetables to the pan and cook until soft. Push the vegetables to one side and pour in beaten eggs mixed with egg whites, scrambling until fluffy. Now add the day-old cooked rice along with soy sauce and oyster sauce. Mix everything until the rice changes color. Then add the green parts of spring onions and sesame oil, giving it one final mix. Divide the fried rice into four equal servings in meal prep boxes. Top with sliced crispy chicken katsu and a generous amount of katsu sauce. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, microwave for 2 minutes covered, then uncover and microwave for a further minute. Optionally, reheat the chicken katsu separately in the air fryer or oven for added crispiness. Enjoy!

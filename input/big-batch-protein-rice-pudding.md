# Big batch protein Rice pudding

Servings: 12 | Calories: 297kcal | Protein: 25g | Carbs: 46g | Fat: 2g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 300g of pudding rice (dry weight)
- 2L fat-free milk
- 50g sugar (or sweetener)
- 200g raisins (optional) - you can also use choc-chips!
- 240g unflavoured or vanilla whey protein powder (I use Protein Works)

## Instructions

1. Add the pudding rice, milk, sugar (or sweetener), and raisins (if using) to a slow cooker. Stir to combine. Cook on low for 3-4 hours, stirring occasionally, until the rice is soft and the mixture is creamy. To serve, weigh out a portion of 200g, then stir in 20g of whey protein powder - no need to add milk the whey will make it creamier! optionally add more sweetener to your taste. Serve warm and enjoy!

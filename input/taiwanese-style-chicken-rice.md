# Taiwanese Style Chicken & Rice

Servings: 4 | Calories: 564kcal | Protein: 50g | Carbs: 52g | Fat: 18g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients

- 350g chicken breast
- 350g chicken thighs (fat
- trimmed) - using a mix results in

**a best texture and flavour**

- 30ml soy sauce
- 30ml rice wine
- 15g (1 tbsp) oyster sauce
- 30g brown sugar alternative
- 20g chilli bean pasta

**(doubjajiang or gochujang)**

- 1 chicken stock cube (crumbled,

**or powdered)**

- 1 tsp black pepper
- 5 cloves garlic (diced)
- 1 tsp ginger
- 200g green beens (boil for 60 seconds before hand)
- 2 mild chilli peppers
- 600g cooked white rice (150g per prep)
- 4 fried eggs

**sesame seeds (to garnish)**

- Roughly chop the chicken breast and chicken thighs until you get a mince-like
- texture. Using a mix of breast and thigh gives the best balance of flavour and juiciness.

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, mix the soy sauce, rice wine, oyster sauce, chilli bean paste, brown sugar alternative, crumbled chicken stock cube, and black pepper. Stir well to combine and set aside. Heat a wok or large pan over high heat with some cooking spray. Add the chicken and let it get a strong sear before stirring. Cook until the chicken is fully cooked and most of the moisture has evaporated. Add the diced garlic, ginger, green beans, and mild chilli peppers. Stir-fry for a minute until fragrant, then pour in the prepared sauce. Cook for another 1–2 minutes, stirring continuously, until everything is evenly coated and glossy. Serve the chicken over cooked white rice and top each portion with a fried egg. Finish with a sprinkle of sesame seeds. Divide into four meal prep containers and store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, microwave covered with a damp paper towel for 3–4 minutes, mix well, and enjoy.

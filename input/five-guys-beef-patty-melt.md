# Five guys Beef patty melt

Servings: 3 | Calories: 575kcal | Protein: 57g | Carbs: 36g | Fat: 21g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**Double smash patties**

- 600g lean ground beef (5% or less)
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp black pepper
- 6 (20g) light cheese slices (brand Tesco)

**Caramelised onions**

- 1 large red or pink onion
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 15g brown stevia (brand Pure via) cooking oil spray

**Burger sauce**

- 50g reduced sugar ketchup (brand Heinz)
- 40g light mayo (lighter than light mayo Heinz)
- 5g American mustard
- 1 tsp honey chopped chives black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. cooking oil, then add the onions. Cook slowly, stirring occasionally, until softened. Add balsamic vinegar and brown stevia, then continue cooking (6-8 minutes) until caramelized. Set aside. Next, season the ground beef with salt and black pepper, and mix with your hands till well seasoned. Then divide into 6 equal portions. Heat a pan over high heat and spray with cooking oil. Place a portion of beef in the pan (multiple if you have space) and smash it down with a spatula or burger press until thin. Cook for about 1-2 minutes per side until crispy and browned. Add a cheese slice on top of each patty and let it melt. set aside once cooked. To make the burger sauce, in a bowl, mix ketchup, mayo, mustard, honey, chopped chives, and black pepper. Stir until well combined. To assemble, slice the brioche buns in half and flip them over so the sliced side faces outward. Place a cheese slice on the inside of the bun, then stack two smash patties on top. Add caramelized onions and a drizzle of burger sauce, then close the bun and press gently. Lightly grease a pan and heat over medium-low. Place the patty melt in the pan and toast, pressing down gently with a burger press or spatula until golden and crispy, about 1-2 minutes per side. Be careful when flipping—use a plate over the top to help if needed. Serve warm and enjoy with extra burger sauce on the side!

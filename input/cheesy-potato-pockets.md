# cheesy potato pockets

Servings: 8 | Calories: 296kcal | Protein: 24g | Carbs: 31g | Fat: 8g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**Beef filling**

- 500g lean ground beef (5% fat or less)
- 1 medium diced white onion
- 10g diced chilli (optional for heat sub with 1/2 tsp cayenne)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp mixed herbs Cooking spray

**potato dough**

- 900g peeled potatoes (chop and

## Instructions

1. boil in salted water starting from cold for an even cook) 125g (1 cup flour) 1 egg Salt to taste

2. other ingredients Pocket assembly 1/8 potato pocket dough 1/8 meat filling (weigh out total mixture and divide by 8 for gram amount) 20g low fat grated cheese

3. Start by heating a pan with a little cooking spray and cooking the diced onion on medium heat until soft. Add the diced chilli if using, then add the lean ground beef. Let it develop a crispy sear before adding the garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, black pepper, and mixed herbs. Cook until browned and fragrant, then set aside to cool slightly. Meanwhile, peel and chop the potatoes, then boil them in salted water (starting from cold for an even cook) for around 15 minutes, or until fork tender. Drain well and mash until completely smooth. Let the potatoes cool fully - this is important so the dough forms properly. Add the cooled mashed potatoes to a bowl with the flour, egg, and a pinch of salt. Mix until a dough forms, then divide it into 8 equal pieces. Lightly grease your hands to make handling easier. Flatten one piece of dough in your palm, add one-eighth of the beef filling along with 20g of low-fat cheese, then gently fold the dough around the filling to seal it. Shape into a ball, then flatten slightly into a pocket. Place the pockets in a pan over medium-low heat with some cooking spray. Cover with a lid and cook for 1–2 minutes on each side until golden and crispy. Repeat for all 8 pockets. Once cooled, wrap them individually in foil and store in the fridge or freezer. To reheat from the fridge, unwrap and air fry at 220°C (430°F) for 8–10 minutes until crispy. From frozen, air fry at 220°C for 13–15 minutes until hot and golden. Perfect as a high-protein snack or quick meal prep - cheesy, crispy, and unreal for only 296 calories each.

# High protein french crepes

Servings: 1 | Calories: 357kcal | Protein: 31g | Carbs: 26g | Fat: 14g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**Crepe mix**

- 1 large egg
- 120ml almond milk
- 20g plain flour
- 25g vanilla protein powder (I use Protein Works)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

**Toppings**

- 15g melted chocolate spread (brand: Protein
- Works brownie spread sub with any chocolate spread)
- 1-2 fresh strawberries (sliced)

## Instructions

1. To a blender add egg, almond milk, plain flour, vanilla protein powder and vanilla extract. Blend until smooth. To a pan on a medium-low heat add low-cal cooking spray. Pour in a third of the batter, tilting the pan to spread it out evenly. Cook for 2-3 minutes on each side, flipping carefully. Repeat to make three crepes. Fold them into quarters, then top with melted chocolate spread and sliced strawberries.

2. Notes Be careful handling the crepes when cooking in the pan, they can be delicate!

3. Enjoy!

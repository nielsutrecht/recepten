# Creamy chicken Alfredo

Servings: 4 | Calories: 496kcal | Protein: 52g | Carbs: 54g | Fat: 8g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Seasoned Chicken**

- 600g (4 medium) chicken breast
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1 tsp salt and pepper Cooking oil spray

**CREAMY Alfredo sAUCE**

- 300ml 2% milk
- ½ cup (180ml) reserved pasta water
- 180g light cream cheese
- 25g grated parmesan
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1 tsp salt and pepper

**OTHER INGREDIENTS**

- 240g fettuccine
- 1 red onion (diced)
- 2 cloves garlic (minced) parmesan (garnish) finely chopped parsley (garnish)

## Instructions

1. Bring a pot of water to a boil, add a pinch of salt, and cook the fettuccine noodles until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup of the pasta water for later use. Season the chicken breast with garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, oregano, salt, and pepper. Lightly spray with low-cal cooking spray and bake or air-fry at 200°C (400°F) for 10-15 minutes. In a blender, combine low-fat milk, the reserved pasta water, light cream cheese, grated parmesan, oregano, and salt and pepper to taste. Blend until smooth. In a pan over medium heat, sauté diced red onion and minced garlic until brown. Add the creamy Alfredo sauce and cooked pasta to the pan. Season with a bit more salt and pepper, if desired. Turn the heat to high and bring the sauce to a boil. Once it starts bubbling, reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer for 6-8 minutes until the sauce thickens. Divide the pasta and chicken into four equal servings. Place in meal-prep containers and refrigerate for up to 5 days. To reheat, add a teaspoon of water to the pasta. Partially cover and microwave for 2-3 minutes. Stir with a fork, then microwave uncovered for an additional minute until creamy. Enjoy!

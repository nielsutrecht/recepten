# Beef and broccoli Stir Fry

Servings: 4 | Calories: 575kcal | Protein: 53g | Carbs: 65g | Fat: 12g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Sauce ingredients**

- 1 cup low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 cup water
- 2 tsp ginger (minced)
- 8 cloves garlic (minced)
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes
- 70g brown stevia (brand pure via
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
- 40g cornstarch (mixed with a 2 tbsp water to make a slurry)

**OTHER INGREDIENTS**

- 800g lean flank steak
- 6-7 cups (500g) broccoli florets
- 200g white rice (dry) cooking spray sesame seeds (optional)

## Instructions

1. Rinse the white rice until the water runs clear, then cook according to packet instructions. Set aside once done. In a jug, mix the soy sauce, water, ginger, garlic, red pepper flakes, brown stevia, sesame oil, apple cider vinegar, and cornstarch slurry until smooth. Set aside. If time allows, freeze the steak for 30 minutes to make slicing easier. Slice the steak into thin strips against the grain and cut the broccoli into small florets. Spray a large pan or wok with cooking spray and place over medium-high heat. Add the broccoli and sauté for a few minutes until lightly browned and tender with a slight crunch. Remove and set aside. Add more cooking spray to the pan and spread the beef strips in a single layer. Cook for a few minutes without moving to allow them to crisp up, then stir and cook through. set aside. Return the broccoli to the pan, pour in the sauce, and Stir fry everything together until the sauce thickens and coats the beef and broccoli. Divide the rice and beef and broccoli evenly between 4 containers. Garnish with sesame seeds if using and store in the fridge for up to 5 days. When you’re ready to eat, microwave for 3–4 minutes until warmed through. Enjoy!

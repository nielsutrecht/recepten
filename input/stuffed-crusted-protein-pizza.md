# Stuffed crusted protein Pizza

Servings: 2 | Calories: 507kcal | Protein: 49g | Carbs: 53g | Fat: 11g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**high protein dough**

- 110g plain flour
- 130g 0% greek yoghurt
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp pinch of salt
- 3 cheese strings (brand: strings

customise this with any of your favourite toppings! Track calories and accordingly.

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 220°C (428°F). In a mixing bowl, combine 110g of plain flour, a teaspoon of baking powder, and a pinch of salt. Add 130g of 0% Greek yogurt, then use a fork to mix until the ingredients form doughy clumps. Once that consistency is reached, knead the mixture with your hands to form a dough. Place the dough on some baking paper. Lightly flour your rolling pin and roll out the dough into a 12-inch pizza base that's about 0.5cm thick. Cut three cheese strings into 2cm pieces and lay them along the edge of your rolled-out dough. Once you have cheese all around the perimeter, fold the dough over the cheese and pinch to seal the stuffed crust. Spread 15g of pizza sauce evenly over the base, leaving the stuffed crust exposed. Sprinkle 60g of low-fat grated cheddar over the sauce, followed by 160g of cooked, seasoned chicken breast slices (seasoned with paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper). Add 25g of sliced red onion and finish with a drizzle of 15g reduced-sugar BBQ sauce. Bake the pizza in the oven for 18-20 minutes, or until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted. Slice up and enjoy your high-protein stuffed crust pizza!

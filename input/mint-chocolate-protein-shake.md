# mint CHocolate Protein shake

Servings: 1 | Calories: 475kcal | Protein: 48g | Carbs: 40g | Fat: 13g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 200ml fat-free milk
- 150g 0% skyr (sub with
- 0% greek yogurt)
- 30g chocolate protein powder (I use Protein Works) tsp cocoa powder
- 1 Aero mint chocolate bar (36g) light squirty cream (optional topping)
- Into a blender add 2% milk, water, 0% Skyr, a scoop of
- chocolate protein powder, teaspoon cocoa powder and 1
- Aero mint chocolate bar (save a little bit to crush and add on top).

## Instructions

1. Blend everything until smooth. Ensure the Aero chocolate is well incorporated and the shake has a consistent texture. Then pour into a glass and enjoy your, protein-rich Aero min chocolate shake!

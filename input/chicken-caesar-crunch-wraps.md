# Chicken Caesar Crunch Wraps

Servings: 3 | Calories: 594kcal | Protein: 55g | Carbs: 59g | Fat: 17g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**crispy chicken**

- 450g chicken breast (1.5 chicken breasts in halves)
- salt pepper and garlic powder to taste
- 1-2 eggs (beaten)
- 80g plain flour (not all is used)
- 80-100g panko bread crumbs (no all is used)
- 15g grated Parmesan cooking spray

**Caesar Salad**

- 2 cups shredded lettuce
- 20g light mayo
- 1 tsp roasted garlic paste
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 15g grated Parmesan salt & pepper to taste
- 1 tsp Worcestershire

**garlic sauce**

- 120g 0% greek yoghurt
- 30g light mayo
- 1 tsp roasted garlic paste salt to taste lemon juice to taste

**Ingredients Per Crunchwrap**

- 1 protein tortilla (brand mission protein tortillas)
- 30g low fat cheese
- 1/3 Caesar salad
- 1 crispy chicken (chopped)
- 1/3 garlic sauce
- 1 toasted mini tortilla

## Instructions

1. Slice the chicken breasts in half lengthways to create thin fillets. Season both sides with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Set up three coating stations: plain flour in one bowl, beaten eggs in another, and panko breadcrumbs mixed with grated parmesan in a third. Coat each piece of chicken in the flour, then the egg, and finally the panko mixture, pressing gently to help it stick. Place the coated chicken on a rack, spray lightly with cooking oil, and cook until golden and crispy. Air fry at 180°C (355°F) for 12–15 minutes, or oven bake at 200°C (400°F) for 18–20 minutes, flipping halfway. Once cooked, chop the chicken into bite-sized pieces. For the Caesar salad, add the shredded lettuce to a bowl along with the light mayo, roasted garlic paste, Dijon mustard, parmesan, Worcestershire sauce, salt, and pepper. Mix well until the lettuce is evenly coated. Make the garlic sauce by mixing the Greek yoghurt, light mayo, roasted garlic paste, salt, and a squeeze of lemon juice. Add a splash of water if needed to loosen the consistency. To assemble, warm a protein tortilla and sprinkle 30g of low-fat cheese in the centre. Add one-third of the Caesar salad, the chopped crispy chicken, one-third of the garlic sauce, and a toasted mini tortilla on top to create the crunch layer. Fold the edges inwards to form a crunchwrap shape. Toast the crunchwrap in a pan over medium heat, folded side down first to seal, then flip and cook until golden and crispy on both sides. Serve hot and crispy. If storing, wrap in foil and keep in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, microwave for 30–60 seconds to remove the chill, then air fry or pan toast for a couple of minutes to bring back the crunch.

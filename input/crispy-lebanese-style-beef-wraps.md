# Crispy Lebanese Style Beef Wraps

Servings: 4 | Calories: 420kcal | Protein: 44g | Carbs: 37g | Fat: 10g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**beef kebab mix**

- 650g lean ground beef
- 1 medium white onion finely diced (drained)
- 4 cloves garlic finely diced (drained)
- 2 green chillies finely diced (drained)
- handful of parsley finely chopped (drained)
- 2 tbsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp salt

**crunchy salad**

- 1/2 red onion sliced thinly

**handful coriander roughly chopped**

- 1 tbsp sumac salt to taste

**garlic cucumber sauce (simple)**

- 250g 0% greek yoghurt
- 100g fresh grated cucumber (80g drained)
- 2 tsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp roasted garlic paste salt to taste
- Finely dice the onion, garlic, green chillies, and parsley, then squeeze out as much
- excess moisture as possible. Add everything to a bowl with the lean ground beef,
- smoked paprika, black pepper, and salt. Mix well until evenly combined and set aside.

## Instructions

1. For the crunchy salad, combine the sliced red onion, chopped coriander, sumac, and salt in a bowl. Toss well and set aside. To make the garlic cucumber sauce, mix the Greek yoghurt, drained grated cucumber, lemon juice, roasted garlic paste, and salt until smooth and creamy. Carefully separate each Lebanese wrap or pita into two thinner layers. Spread a thin, even layer of the beef mixture onto one half, then place the other half on top and press down firmly so it sticks.

2. Other ingredients 4 Lebanese wraps or pitta (80g each)carefully seperate into two layers sliced pickes cooking spray

3. Heat a pan over medium heat with some cooking spray and toast the wraps, flipping occasionally, until golden brown and crispy and the beef is cooked through. Once cooked, layer the wraps with garlic sauce, crunchy salad, sliced pickles, and hot sauce if you like. Fold into a wrap and serve immediately while hot and crispy. These are best enjoyed fresh, but you can store cooled wraps wrapped in foil in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, microwave briefly to remove the chill, then air fry or pan toast to bring the crisp back.

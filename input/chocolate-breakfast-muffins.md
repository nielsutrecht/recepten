# chocolate breakfast muffins

Servings: 6 | Calories: 295kcal | Protein: 18g | Carbs: 44g | Fat: 4g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**dry ingredients**

- 190g self-raising flour

**(sub with plain flour and**

- 2 tsp of baking powder)
- 75g chocolate protein powder (I use Protein Works chocolate silk)
- 20g cocoa powder
- ¼ cup 0-calorie baking sweetener (brand: pure via)
- 1 tsp baking soda pinch of salt

**Wet ingredients**

- 2 ripe medium bananas
- 225g 0% fat cottage cheese (blended)
- 120ml almond milk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 190°C (375°F). Line a jumbo muffin tin (you can find the silicone tray and cups I use here). In a mixing bowl, whisk together plain flour, 75g chocolate protein powder, cocoa powder, 0-cal baking sweetener, baking powder, baking soda, and a pinch of salt. Blend the 0% fat cottage cheese for at least 2-3 minutes till completely smooth and creamy. In a separate bowl, mash the bananas. Mix in the blended cottage cheese, almond milk, vanilla extract. Stir until the mixture is smooth. Combine the wet and dry ingredients, stirring just until mixed (don’t over mix - for moister and fluffier muffins!). Gently fold in the dark chocolate chips.

2. Mix ins 40g dark chocolate chips

3. Distribute the batter evenly among the jumbo muffin cups, filling each nearly to the top. Bake for 20-25 minutes, Remove the muffins from the oven, allow them to cool in the tray for a few minutes, then transfer them to a wire rack to cool completely. For storage, place the muffins in an airtight container. They'll stay fresh at room temperature for up to 2 days or in the fridge for up to a week.

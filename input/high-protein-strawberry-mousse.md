# high protein Strawberry mousse

Servings: 1 | Calories: 290kcal | Protein: 56g | Carbs: 14g | Fat: 1g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 250ml boiling water
- 1 (17g) strawberry sugar free

**jelly sachet (brand: Hartleys)**

- 290g 0% Greek yoghurt

**(make sure it’s authentic**


**greek yogurt not ‘greek style’**

- yoghurt, or the moussee may not set)
- 30g protein powder (I prefer

**to use unflavoured but feel**

- free to use flavoured! (I use Protein Works) pinch of salt
- 1 tbsp sweetener of choice (Optional for added sweetness)
- Notes Another great high volume dessert option,
- and you can try this with
- different flavors of jelly too, works great with lemon lime as well!

## Instructions

1. In a mixing jug, add boiling water and gradually stir in the jelly mix until fully dissolved. In a blender, combine 0% Greek yogurt, the strawberry jelly mixture, protein powder, and a pinch of salt. Blend until completely smooth. Pour the blended mixture into a large bowl, cover with cling film (plastic wrap), and refrigerate for at least 3 hours. Enjoy your high-protein strawberry mousse!

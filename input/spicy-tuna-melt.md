# SPICY TUNA MELT

Servings: 1 | Calories: 593kcal | Protein: 56g | Carbs: 51g | Fat: 18g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients

- 120g drained canned tuna in brine
- 50g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 25g light mayo
- 30g sriracha (or any hot sauce)
- 1 tsp mustard
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 3 tbsp chopped green (spring) onions
- 1/2 jalapeño diced
- 1 tsp chilli flakes
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 90g (2x45) crusty white bread slices
- 50g sliced mozzarella cooking spray

## Instructions

1. Drain the tuna well and add it to a mixing bowl with the Greek yoghurt, light mayo, sriracha, mustard, minced garlic, chopped green onions, diced jalapeño, chilli flakes, and smoked paprika. Mix everything together until fully combined and creamy. Lay one slice of crusty bread on your board and spread over the spicy tuna mixture. Add the sliced mozzarella on top, then place the second slice of bread over it to complete the sandwich. Lightly spray a pan with cooking spray and place the sandwich inside over medium heat. Toast for a few minutes until the bottom is golden and crisp, then flip and repeat on the other side. Once both sides are toasted, cover the pan for a minute to help the mozzarella melt fully. Remove from the pan, slice in half, and enjoy immediately while it’s hot and cheesy. Perfect for a quick, high-protein meal with a spicy kick.

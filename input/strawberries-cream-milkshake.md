# strawberries & Cream milkshake

Servings: 1 | Calories: 319kcal | Protein: 38g | Carbs: 29g | Fat: 7g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 200ml 0% (fat free) milk
- 100g 0% skyr (sub with 0% Greek yoghurt)
- 100g frozen strawberries
- 30 ml single cream
- 25g vanilla protein (I use Protein Works)
- 1 tsp sweetener of choice (I used stevia:

**brand: Pure via)**

- ½ a light rich tea biscuit crushed (optional topping) light squirty cream (optional topping)
- To your blender cup, add fat-free milk, fat-free skyr or
- Greek yogurt, frozen strawberries, single cream, vanilla

**protein powder, and sweetener.**


## Instructions

1. Blend everything until completely smooth. Pour into a glass, optionally garnish with light squirty cream and a crushed rich tea biscuit, and enjoy your refreshing protein packed milkshake!

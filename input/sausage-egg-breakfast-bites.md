# Sausage & egg breakfast bites

Servings: 6 | Calories: 153kcal | Protein: 15g | Carbs: 5g | Fat: 9g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients

- 200g chicken sausages (brand:

**Musclefood)**

- 6 large eggs
- 50ml 2% milk
- 60g grated mozzarella
- ½ red onion (diced) salt and pepper chopped Chives cooking spray
- Notes Perfect on the go for those quick mornings!
- Preheat the oven to 180°C (360°F). Dice the chicken
- sausages and red onion, then sauté them until cooked and
- softened. In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, salt, and
- pepper. Add in some chopped chives for extra flavour.

## Instructions

1. Spray a 6-hole muffin tin with cooking spray. Divide the sausage and onion mix among the muffin holes, then pour the egg mixture on top, filling each about 3/4 full. Sprinkle 10g of grated mozzarella over each one. Bake for 18-20 minutes or until the eggs set. Let them cool a bit before taking them out. These breakfast bites are meal prep-friendly, lasting up to 4 days in the fridge. Reheat and enjoy!

# Crispy Chicken Tenders & Fries

Servings: 4 | Calories: 590kcal | Protein: 57g | Carbs: 62g | Fat: 13g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**crispy chicken tenders**

- 650g chicken breast (flatten
- and slices into tender sized strips)
- 120g plain flour
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp mixed herbs
- 1/2 tsp cayenne
- 2 tsp salt
- 1-2 beaten eggs
- 100g crushed cornflakes cooking spray

**easy sauce**

- 60g low sugar ketchup
- 30g light mayo
- 5g sriracha
- 5g mustard

**water to adjust consistency**


**Other ingredients**

- 700g frozen fries (only a few
- extra calories and way crispier out of the Airfryer)

## Instructions

1. Start by flattening the chicken breasts slightly, then slice them into tender-sized strips. In a bowl, mix together the flour, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, mixed herbs, cayenne, and salt. Set up your coating stations: seasoned flour in one bowl, beaten eggs in another, and crushed cornflakes in a third. Coat each chicken strip first in the flour, then dip into the egg, and finally press into the cornflakes until fully coated. Place the coated tenders on a rack, spray lightly with cooking oil, and cook until golden and crispy. Air fry at 180°C (355°F) for 12–15 minutes, or oven bake at 200°C (400°F) for 18–20 minutes. Flip halfway and sprinkle lightly with salt as soon as they come out. Cook the frozen fries according to the package instructions — air frying works best for maximum crisp with minimal effort. For the easy sauce, mix the ketchup, light mayo, sriracha, and mustard in a bowl, adding a splash of water to loosen it to your preferred consistency. Serve the crispy chicken tenders hot alongside the fries and dipping sauce. These are best enjoyed fresh, but you can store cooled tenders in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, air fry for a couple of minutes until hot and crispy again.

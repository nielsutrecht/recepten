# choc-chip cookie dough bites

Servings: 2 | Calories: ?kcal | Protein: 22g | Carbs: 17g | Fat: 13g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**Dry ingredients**

- 30g oat flour (finely blended oats)
- 7g almond flour (ground almonds)
- 25g brown stevia (brand:

**pure via) pinch of salt**

- 28g melted light butter (brand: lurpak light)
- 45g vanilla protein powder (I used 23g whey and 23g casein - a whey/casein blend gives the best
- texture for this recipe (I use Protein Works)
- 15g milk choc-chips

**wet ingredients**

- 28g melted light butter (brand: lurpak light)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 25g 0 calorie maple syrup (I use Protein Works)

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, mix the oat flour, almond flour, brown stevia, and a pinch of salt until well combined. Add the melted butter, vanilla extract, and maple syrup to the dry ingredients. Stir until a smooth paste forms. Gradually mix in the whey/casein protein powder blend, stirring until fully incorporated and a dough forms. The mixture might feel too dry at first but be patient and keep mixing, If you would like the cookie dough more scoopable you can add a small splash of milk (but this is not ideal for making the bites). Fold in the chocolate chips, then cover and chill the dough in the fridge for 1-2 hours to firm up. Once chilled, roll into bite-sized pieces, shape into protein bars, or use as an addition to a Ninja Creami. Enjoy!

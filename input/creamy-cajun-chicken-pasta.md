# Creamy cajun Chicken pasta

Servings: 4 | Calories: 529kcal | Protein: 58g | Carbs: 63g | Fat: 5g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**cajun chicken**

- 800g chicken breast
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp oregano
- ½-1 tsp cayenne (spice level preference)
- 1 tsp salt & pepper cooking spray (I used avocado)

**Creamy cajun sauce**

- 1 medium white onion (diced)
- 2-3 garlic cloves (diced)
- 1 can (400g) chopped tomatoes
- 275g uncooked penne pasta
- 500ml chicken stock (brand:

**oxo)**

- 150g light cream cheese

**(brand: Philadelphia lightest)**


**Garnishes**

- 2 tbsp parmesan (sprinkle between 4 preps) fresh chopped parsley

## Instructions

1. Dice the chicken breast into cubes, then season with garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, oregano, cayenne, salt, and pepper. Mix until the chicken is fully coated, then add it to a pan with cooking spray and cook on medium-high heat until halfway done. When the chicken is halfway done, add diced onion and garlic, stirring until soft and fragrant. Push everything to the side, then add a can of chopped tomatoes, uncooked pasta, and 500ml of chicken stock. Bring it to a bubble, then cover and simmer for 1012 minutes to cook the pasta. Once the pasta is cooked, add in the cream cheese and stir until creamy. Distribute the pasta into four equal servings in meal prep containers, and garnish with parmesan and chopped parsley. Store refrigerated for up to five days. To reheat, microwave partially covered for 3-4 minutes and stir till creamy. Enjoy!

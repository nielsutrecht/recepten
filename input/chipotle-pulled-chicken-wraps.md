# Chipotle PULLED chicken wraps

Servings: 4 | Calories: 579kcal | Protein: 75g | Carbs: 42g | Fat: 13g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**Pulled chicken**

- 700g (4) chicken breast
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp salt and pepper
- 1 tbsp olive oil

**Chipotle sauce**

- 100g 0% greek yoghurt
- 1 juiced lime
- 1 tbsp chipotle paste (brand:

**Tesco)**

- 20g tomato paste
- 1 tsp chopped coriander salt to taste

**Mexican salad**

- 1½ cups shredded lettuce
- 1 Diced green bell pepper
- 1 Diced red bell pepper
- 1 Diced yellow bell pepper
- ½ cup drained black beans juice of 1 lime salt to taste

**Burrito**

- 4 Aldi protein tortillas (or regular tortillas)
- 200g reduced fat grated cheddar (brand: Aldi 50% less)

## Instructions

1. Season chicken breasts with garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, chilli powder, salt, pepper, and a tablespoon of olive oil, ensuring an even coat. Bake or air fry at 190°C (375°F) for 15-18 minutes. While chicken is cooking, to a blender add Greek yoghurt, lime juice, chipotle paste, tomato pureé, tsp chopped coriander and salt to taste. Blend into a creamy chipotle sauce. Once the chicken cooked shred it immediately with a fork or stand mixer and combine with the sauce until well-coated. Prepare Mexican salad by combining and tossing all the listed ingredients in a separate bowl. Warm tortilla wraps in a pan over medium heat or microwave for 15 seconds. Assemble burritos with creamy chicken, 50g of low-fat cheddar, and a quarter of the salad each. Roll and wrap each burrito in foil. Store refrigerated for up to 5 days. For reheating, remove foil, microwave burrito for 3-4 minutes, and pan-toast for 1-2 minutes for a crispy finish. Enjoy your creamy chipotle pulled chicken burritos!

# TEriyaki bEEF noodles

Servings: 4 | Calories: 547kcal | Protein: 55g | Carbs: 57g | Fat: 11g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**crispy beef**

- 800g lean steak (flank or top sirloin)
- 20ml soy sauce
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp salt
- 20g cornflour
- 5g baking soda (tenderises the beef) cooking spray

**TERIYAKI sauce**

- 60ml soy sauce
- 1 tbsp hoisin sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp honey (or sweetener of choice)
- 1 tbsp garlic paste
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp chilli flakes
- 5g cornflour mixed with a tbsp of water
- Begin by preparing the steak: freeze for 30 minutes for easier slicing,

## Instructions

1. then cut into thin strips against the grain. In a bowl, mix steak strips with soy sauce, black pepper, salt, cornflour, and baking soda until evenly coated. For the Lo Mein sauce, blend soy sauce, hoisin sauce, oyster sauce, honey (or sweetener), garlic paste, sesame oil, chilli flakes, and a cornflour-water mixture in a jug; set aside.

2. other ingredients 600g straight-to-wok noodles(brand: blue dragon - Tesco) 1 medium onion (sliced into 1/4 pieces) 80g thinly sliced carrot 100g sugar snap peas (snow peas) 1 tsp garlic paste 1 tsp ginger paste 2 shiitake mushrooms (thinly sliced) dash of water to help soften veg sesame seeds (optional garnish)

3. In a hot pan with cooking spray, cook the beef strips until they're crispy, then remove and set aside. Sauté onions, carrots, and sugar snap peas in the same pan, adding a dash of water to soften. Stir in ginger and garlic paste, and mushrooms until everything is fragrant and tender. Add the noodles and prepared Lo Mein sauce, stir-frying until well mixed. Return the crispy beef to the pan, combining everything thoroughly. Portion the crispy beef Lo Mein into four meal-prep containers and store in the fridge for up to 5 days. For reheating, microwave with a teaspoon of water, covered, for 3-4 minutes. Enjoy!

# Chilli cheese hot pockets

Servings: 8 | Calories: 324kcal | Protein: 29g | Carbs: 40g | Fat: 6g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients

- 2-ingredient dough
- 400g plain flour
- 410g 0% fat Greek yoghurt

**Chilli beef mix**

- 400g 5% ground beef
- 100ml beef stock
- 30g light cream cheese

**(brand: Philadelphia lightest)**

- 1 packet fajita seasoning (brand Tesco)

**Other ingredients**

- 160g reduced fat grated
- cheese (brand: Aldi 50% less fat) Cooking spray
- Notes These can be adapted to use any filling of your choice!
- To make the super simple dough, add flour and 0% fat Greek yogurt to a mixing
- bowl. Mix with a fork until you form doughy clumps. Then knead it with your
- hands into a big dough ball and divide equally into 8 smaller balls.

## Instructions

1. Heat a pan to high and spray with cooking spray. Add the ground beef, flattening it with a spatula to get a crispy sear before breaking it up and cooking through. Once browned, add fajita seasoning, beef stock, and light cream cheese. Stir on medium-low heat until creamy, then set aside. Lightly flour your work surface and flatten each dough ball into a circle about 4 inches in diameter. On each circle, add 1/8 of the chilli beef mix followed by 20g of reduced fat grated cheddar. Carefully stretch and fold in the sides to form a dumpling shape, pinch to seal with your fingers, then press down gently to flatten into a hot pocket. Add the hot pockets to a pan on low heat with cooking spray and cook for 5-6 minutes on each side, until the dough is fully cooked through and the edges are crispy. Wrap each hot pocket in foil and store in the fridge or freezer. To reheat in the microwave: unwrap from the foil and cook for 3-6 minutes on high, flipping halfway (if frozen, wrap in a damp paper towel). To reheat in the air fryer: 180°C (375°F) for 5–10 minutes. To reheat in the oven: 200°C (400°F) for 5-10 minutes.

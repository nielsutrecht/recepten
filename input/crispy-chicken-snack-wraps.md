# crispy chicken snack wraps

Servings: 3 | Calories: 294kcal | Protein: 36g | Carbs: 18g | Fat: 8g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**Crispy chicken**

- 400g chicken breast (cut into 3x
- 130g pieces - I used two breasts
- and saved the extra for later)
- 125g plain (all purpose) flour
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 egg
- 1 tbsp pickle juice
- 100g bread crumbs (not all is used - 45g for tracking)

**garlic sauce**

- 90g 0% greek yoghurt
- 45g light mayo
- 1 tsp garlic paste Salt & pepper to taste
- 1 tsp honey (optional for added sweetness)

**water to adjust consistency**


**snack wrap assembly**

- 1 mini low carb tortilla (brand:
- mission carb balance tortillas 70 calories each)
- 1/3 garlic sauce shredded lettuce
- 1 crispy chicken tender

## Instructions

1. Slice the chicken breasts into three long strips (about 130g each). In a bowl, mix the flour with the smoked paprika, black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and salt. Coat each chicken strip in the seasoned flour, making sure every part is covered. In a separate bowl, whisk the egg with the pickle juice. Dip each flour-coated chicken strip into the egg mixture, then press it into the breadcrumbs until fully coated. Transfer the coated chicken to a rack, spray lightly with cooking oil, and air fry at 190°C (375°F) for 12–15 minutes, or bake at 200°C (400°F) for 18–20 minutes, flipping halfway, until crispy and golden. While the chicken cooks, make the garlic sauce by mixing the Greek yoghurt, light mayo, garlic paste, honey (optional), and a pinch of salt and pepper. Add a splash of water if you want it thinner. Warm your low-carb tortillas, then spread a generous amount of garlic sauce down the centre of each one. Add shredded lettuce and one crispy chicken tender per wrap. Fold it up tightly like a proper snack wrap and repeat for all three. These are best eaten fresh, but if you want to you can prep the chicken tenders ahead of time - just reheat them in the air fryer to get some of the crunch back. Each wrap hits 36g protein for just 294 calories - absolute banger of a macrofriendly McDonald's dupe.

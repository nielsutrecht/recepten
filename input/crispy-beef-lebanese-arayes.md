# Crispy beef lebanese arayes

Servings: 5 | Calories: 372kcal | Protein: 42g | Carbs: 33g | Fat: 9g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**Arayes meat filling**

- 800g lean ground beef (5% or less)
- 1 medium finely diced onion
- 4 garlic cloves (minced or finely diced)

**fresh chopped coriander/cilantro**

- 2 finely chopped green chillies
- (optional if you like the heat)
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp back pepper
- 1 tsp cayenne
- 2 tsp salt

**other ingredients**

- 5 sliced pittas (63g) - I used
- wholemeal for extra fibre and protein
- cooking spray (I used avocado oil)

## Instructions

1. Start by preparing the meat filling. Finely dice the onion, garlic, green chillies, and fresh coriander, then add everything to a large bowl along with the ground beef and all the spices. Mix well until fully combined and evenly seasoned. If you’re using whole pitta breads, carefully slice them open to create a pocket. For pre-sliced pittas, gently separate them without tearing. Weigh out the total meat filling and divide into 5 equal portions — each pitta should be stuffed with around 1/5th of the mixture, pressing it in to about ½ inch thickness.

2. Garlic white sauce 120g 0% Greek yoghurt 60g light mayo juice of half a lemon 1-2 tsp honey 1-2 tsp garlic powder salt to taste

3. Heat a griddle or flat pan over medium heat and lightly spray with cooking oil. Add the arayes and cook for around 6–8 minutes total, flipping every 30– 60 seconds to crisp up both sides and ensure the meat is fully cooked through. The pittas should be golden and crunchy on the outside. For the garlic white sauce, simply add all ingredients to a blender and blitz until smooth. Adjust seasoning to taste. Serve the arayes hot and crispy with a side of dipping sauce. If meal prepping, allow them to cool completely before wrapping in foil. To reheat, microwave for 2 minutes to take the chill off, then finish in an air fryer or pan to bring back that crispy texture.

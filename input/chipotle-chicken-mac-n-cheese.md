# Chipotle chicken mac n’ cheese

Servings: 4 | Calories: 585kcal | Protein: 60g | Carbs: 61g | Fat: 10g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Chipotle chicken bites**

- 600g chicken breast (diced into 1 inch cubes)
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp cayenne pepper
- 1 cup plain flour
- ½ cup cornstarch
- 100g low calorie chipotle sauce (brand: Frank’s) cooking spray

**Chipotle MAc n Cheese sauce**

- 260ml fat free milk
- 150g light cream cheese (brand:

**Philadelphia lightest)**

- 80ml reserved pasta water
- 30g chipotle paste tbsp grated parmesan Juice of ½ a lemon
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp salt

**other ingredients**

- 260g dry macaroni pasta (boil in salted water)
- 60g low fat grated cheddar
- 60g reduced fat grated mozzarella

**Chopped parsley (garnish)**


## Instructions

1. Start by boiling the macaroni pasta in salted water until just al dente. Reserve 80–100ml of the pasta water before draining the rest. Set the cooked pasta aside. Dice the chicken breast into 1-inch cubes and place into a bowl. Add the egg, garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, cayenne, and salt. Mix until the chicken is well coated. In a separate bowl, combine the plain flour and cornstarch. Coat each piece of chicken in the flour mixture, shaking off any excess. Air-fry the coated chicken at 200ºC/400ºF for 10–12 minutes, or bake at 220ºC/430ºF for 15–20 minutes, flipping halfway and respraying with cooking oil until golden and crispy. Set aside. To make the chipotle cheese sauce, add the fat-free milk, reserved pasta water (80ml), light cream cheese, chipotle paste, grated parmesan, honey, garlic powder, and salt to a blender. Blend until completely smooth. Pour the sauce into a saucepan over medium-low heat and stir for 3–5 minutes until thick and creamy. Add the cooked macaroni, followed by the grated cheddar and mozzarella, and stir until melted and fully coated. Toss the chicken bites in the low-calorie chipotle sauce until well coated and sticky. Divide the mac and cheese evenly into four meal prep containers. Top each one with a portion of chipotle chicken bites. Optionally garnish with chopped parsley or extra cheese. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, microwave covered for 2 minutes, then uncover and microwave for another 1–2 minutes until hot. Stir to bring the sauce back to life and enjoy!

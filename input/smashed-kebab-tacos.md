# SMASHED KEBAB TACOS

Servings: 4 | Calories: 229kcal | Protein: 18g | Carbs: 27g | Fat: 5g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**KEBAB MIX**

- 270g lean ground lamb or beef
- 1/2 diced red onion
- 3 garlic cloves diced
- 3 tbsp fresh chopped coriander
- Chopped green chillies (optional for heat)
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp tumeric
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1/2 tsp salt

**GARLIC SAUCE**

- 120g 0% greek yoghurt
- 2 tbsp light mayo 2 tsp lemon juice
- 1/2 tsp dried parsley
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 20-30ml water (adjust for desired consistency)

**Fresh salad**

- 100g cherry tomatoes diced
- 1/2 red onion diced
- 100g cucumber diced (remove seeds) fresh chopped coriander
- 1/2 tsp sumac (optional) salt to taste

**ingredients per taco**

- 1 mini tortilla (use low carb for less calories)
- 1/4 kebab mix kebab salad garlic sauce

## Instructions

1. Add the lean ground lamb or beef to a mixing bowl along with the diced red onion, garlic, chopped coriander, green chillies if using, paprika, cumin, turmeric, pepper, and salt. Mix everything together well until the colour changes and the spices are fully worked in. For even better flavour, cover and marinate for a couple of hours or overnight, but you can also cook it straight away if you’re short on time. Once ready to cook, lay out four mini tortillas and place ¼ of the kebab mixture in the centre of each one. Press the meat down firmly so it spreads out into a thin patty that sticks to the tortilla. Heat a pan over medium-high, spray with cooking spray, and place each tortilla meat-side down. Cook until the meat develops a golden, crispy sear, then flip and let the tortilla side toast until slightly crisp. For the garlic sauce, whisk together the Greek yoghurt, light mayo, lemon juice, dried parsley, garlic powder, black pepper, salt, and a splash of water to loosen it to your preferred consistency. In a separate bowl, mix together the diced cherry tomatoes, red onion, cucumber, coriander, and sumac with a pinch of salt to make the kebab salad. To assemble, add a spoonful of the salad onto each taco, drizzle generously with the garlic sauce, and fold together while hot. These are best eaten fresh from the pan while the meat is crispy and the tortillas still warm. Enjoy - they hit like a proper kebab but in taco form, and each one packs a good amount of protein.

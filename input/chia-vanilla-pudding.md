# chia vanilla pudding

Servings: 1 | Calories: 384kcal | Protein: 30g | Carbs: 30g | Fat: 16g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**Chia pudding mix**

- 180ml almond milk
- 125g 0% fat Greek yoghurt
- 3 tbsp chia seeds
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 20g vanilla protein powder (I use Protein Works French vanilla)
- Notes This recipe is Meal Prep
- Friendly! Just multiply up ingredient measurements to adjust how many servings you want.

## Instructions

1. In a mixing jug, add almond milk, Greek yogurt, chia seeds, vanilla extract, maple syrup, and vanilla protein powder. Mix until well combined. Transfer the mixture into a mason jar or container, seal it tight, and chill in the fridge for at least 2 hours, or overnight for the best results. Dig in when you're ready!

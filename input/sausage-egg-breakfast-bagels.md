# sausage & egg breakfast Bagels

Servings: 4 | Calories: 670kcal | Protein: 58g | Carbs: 49g | Fat: 28g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients

- 260g (8) lean chicken

**breast sausages (decased)**

- 8 eggs (beaten) (100g) 4 slices mozzarella
- 4 protein bagels - click for recipe
- 120g light cream cheese (brand:
- philadelphia lightest) salt and pepper Cooking spray

## Instructions

1. Prepare your protein bagels first (see recipe link in notes). Beat two eggs at a time and pour into a pan lightly greased with cooking spray over medium heat, tilting the pan to coat the base evenly. Cook for a couple of minutes until the top is partially set, then fold in two opposite sides, followed by the remaining two sides, to form a neat folded egg square. Carefully flip to finish cooking, then set aside. Repeat the process until you have four folded egg squares. Remove the chicken sausages from their casings, wet your hands slightly, and shape the meat into four equal balls. Heat the pan to medium-high, spray with cooking spray, and place one ball in the pan. Lay a small piece of parchment on top and use a spatula or burger press to flatten into a patty. Season the top if desired, then cook for 2–3 minutes per side until golden brown and fully cooked through. Repeat until all four patties are ready. Lightly toast the protein bagel halves. Spread 15g of light cream cheese on each half, seasoning lightly with salt and pepper. On the base half, place a slice of mozzarella, followed by the folded egg, the sausage patty, and the top half of the bagel. Repeat for all four bagels. Wrap each bagel in foil and store in the fridge for up to 4 days. To reheat, remove the foil and microwave for 2–3 minutes until hot all the way through. Enjoy as a quick, high-protein breakfast option.

# chocolate protein popcorn

Servings: 1 | Calories: 357kcal | Protein: 20g | Carbs: 49g | Fat: 7g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**Popcorn**

- 60g unpopped kernels (add
- a little extra to account for some that won’t pop) pinch of salt low cal cooking spray

**Chocolate coating**

- 25g milk chocolate
- 30ml unsweetened almond milk
- 15g Chocolate protein powder (I use Protein Works)

## Notes

Give a microwave safe bowl a couple sprays with cooking

## Instructions

1. spray then add the popcorn kernels (tilt the bowl to get an even layer) and give them another spray. Then sprinkle over a pinch of salt and cover with cling film. Microwave for 5 minutes or till most of the kernels are popped In another bowl add the chocolate chopped up into smaller pieces and almond milk. Microwave for 10 seconds just till the chocolate is melted Then stir to combine. Now add the

2. you can switch it up and use white chocolate and vanilla protein powder!

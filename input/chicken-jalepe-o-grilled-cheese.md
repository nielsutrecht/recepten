# chicken Jalepeño Grilled Cheese

Servings: 1 | Calories: 527kcal | Protein: 57g | Carbs: 50g | Fat: 11g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**Crispy steak bites**

- 2 slices bread
- 90g sliced seasoned precooked chicken breast (I used homemade but store bought works too)
- 50g low-fat cheddar mix (I used 50% less fat)
- 25g sliced Jalepenos
- 60g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 30g light cream cheese (Philadelphia lightest)
- 5g (tsp) roasted garlic paste
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp paprika

**parsley flakes (optional)**


## Instructions

1. Add the cooked chicken, sliced jalapeños, low-fat cheddar, Greek yoghurt, light cream cheese, roasted garlic paste, salt, and paprika to a bowl. Mix until everything is well combined and creamy. Spread the mixture evenly over one slice of bread, then place the second slice on top to form a sandwich. Lightly spray the outside with cooking spray. Air fry at 200°C (400°F) for 8–10 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden, crispy, and the cheese is fully melted. You can sprinkle over parsley flakes halfway through if you like. Serve immediately while hot and gooey.

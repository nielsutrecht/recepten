# bbq Shredded chicken tacos

Servings: 4 | Calories: 511kcal | Protein: 55g | Carbs: 33g | Fat: 18g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**Shredded chicken**

- 600g (3) chicken breast
- 400ml salsa (brand: el taquito from lidl)
- 1 small red onion (diced)
- 2 cloves garlic (minced)
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp salt and pepper cooking spray

**Tacos**

- 8 mini tortillas (brand: aldi)
- 80g light grated cheddar Shredded lettuce Sour cream (optional) Chopped fresh parsley (garnish)
- Notes poaching chicken is a great way to prep cooked chicken without adding extra calories!

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Next, bring a large pot of water to a gentle simmer and add the chicken breasts, poach for 12-15 minutes or until cooked completely through then immediately shred using two forks or a electric whisk and bowl (much quicker). Dice red onion and sauté in a pan with cooking spray over medium-high heat until browned. Add garlic, stirring until fragrant, approximately 30 seconds. Add 400ml of salsa to the pan, season with cumin, paprika, chili powder, salt, and pepper. After stirring for 2-3 minutes, incorporate shredded chicken, ensuring it’s well coated with the sauce. For taco shells, position mini tortillas between the oven’s grill bars to create a taco shape. Toast for 3-4 minutes until crispy, monitoring closely. To assemble, layer each taco shell with lettuce, 1/8 of the chicken mixture, 10g of grated cheese, another portion of chicken, a drizzle of sour cream, and a sprinkle of chopped cilantro. Serve and enjoy. If using for meal prep, store the chicken and other taco filling ingredients separately in the fridge, then reheat the chicken and make the taco shells fresh when it is time to eat.

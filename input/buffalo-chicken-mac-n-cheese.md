# buffalo chicken mac n’ cheese

Servings: 4 | Calories: 593kcal | Protein: 64g | Carbs: 73g | Fat: 5g
Section: dinner

- 1 tsp paprika

**other ingredients**

## Instructions

1. Start by boiling 300g of macaroni pasta in salted water until al dente. Once cooked, drain and set aside. For the high protein cheese sauce, to your blender cup add 0% fat cottage cheese, light cream cheese, low-fat milk, buffalo sauce, low-fat cheddar, nutritional yeast, onion powder, and garlic powder. Blend for at least 2 minutes until completely smooth. Now in a bowl, mix the cooked macaroni with the creamy cheese sauce until the pasta is thoroughly coated. For the buffalo chicken, dice chicken breast into cubes and season with paprika, garlic salt, and onion powder. Cook in a pan until crispy and tender. Turn off the heat, add buffalo sauce and honey, and mix until the chicken is well coated. Divide the mac n' cheese and buffalo chicken into four servings in meal prep boxes. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, microwave each portion covered for 2 minutes, then uncover and microwave for an additional minute. The sauce may appear separated in the fridge but will return to a creamy consistency once heated and mixed. Enjoy!

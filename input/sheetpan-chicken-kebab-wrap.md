Servings: 4 | Calories: 586kcal | Protein: 62g | Carbs: 45g | Fat: 16g
Section: dinner

## Instructions

1. and a tablespoon of olive oil. Mix well until the colour changes and the mixture becomes uniform. Grease a large sheet pan and spread the chicken mixture across it in an even layer roughly ½ and inch in thickness. Use two fingers to press horizontal lines through the surface to form the classic kebab texture, then use a spatula to vertically divide the mixture into eight long kebab strips. Bake at 190°C (375°F) for 20–25 minutes, draining any excess juices at the halfway point, until golden brown and fully cooked. For the garlic sauce, whisk together the Greek yoghurt, light mayo, garlic powder, dried dill, salt, pepper, lemon juice, and a splash of water until smooth and at your preferred consistency. In a separate bowl, mix the diced red onion, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, coriander, and a pinch of salt to make your salad. To serve, warm the Lebanese wraps and a dollop of garlic sauce followed by two kebab strips to each. Then add a handful of salad, and finish with another drizzle of sauce. Fold and enjoy immediately while hot. If meal prepping, store the kebabs, salad, and sauce separately. Wrap the kebabs in foil and refrigerate for up to 5 days. To reheat, pan-fry or air fry until hot, then assemble your wrap fresh.

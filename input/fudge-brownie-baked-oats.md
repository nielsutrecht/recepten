# fudge brownie baked oats

Servings: 6 | Calories: 413kcal | Protein: 25g | Carbs: 51g | Fat: 12g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**Dry ingredients**

- 350g blended oats
- 60g chocolate protein powder (I use Protein Works chocolate silk) ⅓ cup granulated

**sweetener (brand: pure via)**

- 10g baking powder
- 40g cocoa powder tsp salt

**Wet ingredients**

- 480ml almond milk
- 4 large eggs
- 2 medium bananas

**Filling**

- 40g dark chocolate chips

**toppings**

- 15g mini marshmallows (optional - 398 cals per prep without)

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 180°C (360°F) and lightly grease a 12” x 9” (30cm x 23cm) baking dish. firstly blend your oats (this is going to help achieve the fudgy brownie texture). Now in a bowl, mix together the oats, chocolate protein powder, granulated sweetener, baking powder, cocoa powder and salt. In a separate bowl, mash the bananas. Add in eggs and almond milk and whisk until smooth. Gradually mix the wet ingredients with the dry, stirring until combined. Now fold in 30g chocolate chips into the mixture, ensuring they are evenly distributed. Pour the mixture into your prepared baking dish, spreading it out for an even bake. Top with an additional 10g of chocolate chips then bake at 180°C (360°F) for 30 minutes. Keep an eye on this one while it’s in the oven! You want to bake it on shorter end of the suggested time for max brownie fudginess! Allow it to cool slightly before cutting into six portions. Store in refrigerated meal prep containers for up to six days. When you’re ready to eat, add a little splash of milk on top and reheat it in the microwave for 2 minutes, Enjoy!

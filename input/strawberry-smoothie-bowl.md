# Strawberry SMoothie bowl

Servings: 1 | Calories: 271kcal | Protein: 34g | Carbs: 26g | Fat: 4g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 100g ice cubes
- 150g frozen strawberries
- 100g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 75ml 2% milk (use
- almond for even less cals) strawberry protein powder (I use Protein

**Works) - sub with vanilla**

- 1 tsp honey (or your choice of sweetener)
- ½ tsp xantham gum (for
- that extra thick & creamy texture)
- Notes Smoothie bowls are a great high volume
- dessert option, and super customisable too! Feel free to experiment with

**different frozen fruits and toppings!**

- To your blender cup add 100g of ice cubes, 150g of frozen
- strawberries (or your fruits of choice), 100g of 0% Greek
- yogurt, 75ml of milk (or almond milk for fewer calories), a
- scoop of strawberry protein powder, a teaspoon of honey
- (or your choice of sweetener) to taste.

## Instructions

1. Then optionally add 1/2 tsp of xanthan gum for an extra thick and creamy texture. Blend all the ingredients until smooth and creamy and the final texture should be thick enough to eat with a spoon. Scoop it out into a bowl, top with any other fruits or toppings of your choice and enjoy your high-protein strawberry smoothie bowl! The perfect refresher postworkout or as a healthy dessert option.

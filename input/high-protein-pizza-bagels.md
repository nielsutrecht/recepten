# High Protein pizza bagels

Servings: 2 | Calories: 231kcal | Protein: 18g | Carbs: 24g | Fat: 7g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**Pizza bagels**

- 1 plain bagel cut in half (brand: New York) - you can also use the protein bagel recipe!
- 1 packet (45g) Chicken

**fridge raiders - sub with**

- 40g cooked chicken slices
- 2 tsp tomato paste
- 50g light grated cheddar

**Sprinkle of Italian mixed herbs**


**garnish chopped parsley**

- Take your plain bagel and slice it in half and preheat your
- oven to 180°C (360°F) for baking.

## Instructions

1. Spread 1 teaspoon of tomato paste on each bagel half, then evenly distribute the chicken Fridge Raiders or cooked chicken slices over the bagel halves. Sprinkle on 25g of light grated cheddar on each half and some Italian mixed herbs. Place the prepared bagel halves on a baking sheet and bake for about 10-12 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and the bagels are toasty. Once baked optionally garnish with chopped parsley for aesthetics and enjoy!

# Crispy Chicken Parm Sandwich

Servings: 1 | Calories: 611kcal | Protein: 59g | Carbs: 53g | Fat: 16g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients

- For 1 sandwich
- 160g chicken breast (large breast halved) paprika garlic powder salt pepper
- 100g ciabatta roll (you can also remove some of the filling to save on cals)
- 60g reduced fat fresh mozzarella
- 40g panko (10-15g for tracking)
- 80g all purpose flour (5-10g tracking)
- 1 egg
- 15g pesto (included in cals this is optional but highly recommended) tbsp fresh chopped basil leaves
- 2 tsp Parmesan cheese (grated)
- 40g high quality pizza sauce (I used mutti)

## Instructions

1. Slice the chicken breast in half horizontally so you end up with two thin cutlets roughly 150g each. Season one cutlet with paprika, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper. Then set up your coating stations: add the flour to one bowl, beat the egg in a second bowl, and place the panko breadcrumbs in a third. Dust each chicken piece in the flour until fully coated, then dip into the beaten egg, and finally press into the panko to create an even crispy layer. Place the crumbed chicken onto a rack, spray lightly with cooking spray, and air fry at 190°C/375ºF for 12–15 minutes, or oven bake at 200°C/400ºF for 18–20 minutes, until golden and cooked through. Toast your ciabatta roll, then spread a little pizza sauce on the base. Add one piece of crispy chicken, spoon over more pizza sauce, and top with the sliced reduced-fat mozzarella. Air fry or broil for a minute to melt the cheese, then finish with grated parmesan and fresh chopped basil. If you’re including it, add a small layer of pesto — it takes the flavour to the next level and is highly recommended. Close the sandwich with the top half of the roll, slice, and enjoy immediately while hot and crispy.

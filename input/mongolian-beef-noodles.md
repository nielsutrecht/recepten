# Mongolian Beef Noodles

Servings: 4 | Calories: 498kcal | Protein: 50g | Carbs: 59g | Fat: 7g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**crispy beef**

- 700g lean beef steak (Fillet or flank steak)
- 1 tsp baking soda (tenderises the meat)
- 25g cornflour
- 1 tsp salt & pepper
- 1 tbsp sesame oil (substitute with olive oil)

**mongolian sauce**

- 3 tbsp oyster sauce (brand:

**Tesco)**

- 30ml Mirin (substitute with rice wine vinegar)
- 60ml soy sauce
- 2 tbsp brown stevia (brand:

**Truvia)**

- 5g cornflour (mixed with water to avoid clumping)

**other ingredients**

- 600g cooked noodles (brand:
- Amoy, straight to wok medium noodles)
- 10g chopped ginger
- 3 cloves minced garlic
- 1 Medium white onion chopped Green onions
- 120g of shiitake mushrooms Sesame seeds (garnish)
- Begin by slicing the beef into thin strips. In a mixing bowl, combine the
- beef with baking soda, cornflour, salt, pepper, and sesame oil. Mix well with
- your hands until the beef is fully coated. Heat a pan over high heat. Add the
- beef and cook for about 8-10 minutes until crispy. Once done, remove the
- beef from the pan and set it aside.
- To prepare the Mongolian sauce, combine oyster sauce, Mirin (or rice wine
- vinegar), soy sauce, brown stevia, and cornflour (mixed with a little water to
- prevent clumping) in a mixing jug. Stir well until everything is combined.
- In the same pan used for the beef, add ginger, garlic, white onion, and the
- white parts of the green onions. Sauté until they soften. Then add the
- shiitake mushrooms with a splash of water and cook for an additional 2-3
- minutes until the mushrooms are soft.
- Return the beef to the pan along with the Mongolian sauce and cooked
- noodles. Reduce the heat to low and stir everything together until well

# Blueberry & Oat smoothie

Servings: 1 | Calories: 320kcal | Protein: 38g | Carbs: 39g | Fat: 2g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 200ml 0% (fat free) milk
- 100g 0% skyr (sub with 0% Greek yoghurt)
- 15g quick oats
- 100g frozen blueberries
- 25g vanilla protein (I use Protein Works)
- 1 tsp sweetener of choice (I used stevia:

**brand: Pure via)**

- To your blender cup add fat free milk, fat free skyr or
- greek yoghurt, quick oats, frozen blueberries, vanilla

**protein powder and sweetener.**


## Instructions

1. Blend everything until completely smooth. Then pour it into a glass and enjoy your refreshing and energising smoothie!

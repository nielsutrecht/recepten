# apple pie breakfast muffins

Servings: 6 | Calories: 244kcal | Protein: 17g | Carbs: 32g | Fat: 3g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**dry ingredients**

- 190g self raising flour

**(sub with plain flour and**

- 2 tsp of baking powder)
- 75g vanilla protein powder (I Use Protein Works)
- 2 tsp cinnamon
- ¼ cup 0-calorie baking sweetener (brand: pure via)
- 1 tsp baking soda pinch of salt

**Wet ingredients**

- 2 ripe medium bananas
- 225g 0% fat cottage cheese (blended)
- 120ml almond milk
- 10ml vanilla extract

**Mix ins**

- 150g frozen diced apple

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 190°C (375°F). Line a jumbo muffin tin (you can find the silicone tray and cups I use here). In a mixing bowl, whisk together plain flour, vanilla protein powder, cinnamon, 0-calorie baking sweetener, baking powder, baking soda and a pinch of salt. Blend the 0% fat cottage cheese for at least 2-3 minutes till completely smooth and creamy. In a separate bowl, mash the bananas. Mix in the blended cottage cheese, almond milk, vanilla extract. Stir until smooth. Combine the wet and dry ingredients, stirring just enough to combine everything together (don't over mix for moister and fluffier muffins!). Gently fold in frozen diced apple. You can use either home-frozen or store-bought diced apples. Divvy up the batter evenly among the jumbo muffin cups, filling each almost to the top. Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the middle comes out clean. Remove the muffins from the oven and let them cool in the pan for a bit before moving them to a wire rack to cool completely. For storage, use an airtight container. They'll stay good at room temperature for up to 2 days and in the fridge for up to a week.

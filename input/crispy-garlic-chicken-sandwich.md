# Crispy garlic Chicken sandwich

Servings: 2 | Calories: 463kcal | Protein: 47g | Carbs: 43g | Fat: 11g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**CHICKEN marinade**

- 300g (2) boneless, skinless,

**chicken thighs**

- 90g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 1 tbsp garlic paste
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1 tsp black pepper
- ½ lemon juice

**crispy coating**

- Panko bread crumbs (30g for calculating macros)
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp salt & pepper
- 1 tsp oregano

**GARLIC SAUCE**

- 70g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 30g light mayo
- 1 tsp roasted garlic paste lemon juice
- ½ tsp finely chopped dill water for consistency

**OTHER INGREDIENTS**

- 2 brioche buns (brand: Aldi)
- 2 light cheese singles Shredded lettuce light cooking spray

## Instructions

1. Combine chicken with marinade ingredients: Greek yoghurt, garlic paste, onion powder, oregano, black pepper, and lemon juice in a bowl. Ensure the chicken is well-coated and refrigerate for 30 minutes to 2 hours. Prepare the crispy coating by mixing Panko breadcrumbs, paprika, onion powder, oregano, salt, and pepper. Coat the marinated chicken thoroughly with the breadcrumb mixture, pressing down firmly. Spray the coated chicken lightly with cooking spray and bake at 200ºC for 20-25 minutes or air fry at 180°C (360°F) for 1215 minutes. For the garlic sauce, mix Greek yoghurt, garlic paste, lemon juice, salt, finely chopped dilll, water for consistency and stir until smooth. Assemble the sandwich using a toasted brioche bun spread with garlic sauce, followed by chopped lettuce, crispy chicken, a light cheese single, topped with additional garlic sauce. Place the top bun and enjoy your crispy garlic chicken sandwich!

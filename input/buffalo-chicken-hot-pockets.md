# BUFFALO CHICKEN HOT POCKETS

Servings: 8 | Calories: 345kcal | Protein: 31g | Carbs: 40g | Fat: 6g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**BUFFALO CHICKEN SEASoNING**

- 500g chicken breast (diced into small cubes)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp cayenne (optional for added spice)
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper

**hot pocket dough**

- 400g plain flour
- 410g 0% Greek yoghurt optional salt to taste

**other ingredients**

- 130ml hot sauce (I used franks red hot)
- 30g light butter (I used lurpak light)
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 20g brown sugar alternative (sub with brown sugar or regular sweetener)
- 25g honey
- 1 tsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tsp water

## Instructions

1. Start by preparing the dough. Add the plain flour and Greek yoghurt to a bowl and mix with a spoon until doughy clumps form. Then knead with your hands until it comes together into a smooth dough ball. Divide into 8 equal-sized balls and set aside while you make the filling. Dice the chicken breast into small 1cm cubes, then season with garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, cayenne, salt, and black pepper. Spray a pan with cooking oil and cook the chicken over medium-high heat until golden brown and fully cooked through. Set aside. In the same pan, melt the light butter over medium heat. Stir in the hot sauce, apple cider vinegar, brown stevia (or sugar), and honey. Let it simmer gently for 1–2 minutes until combined and slightly thickened. Return the cooked chicken to the pan and stir to coat. Mix 1 tsp of cornstarch with 2 tsp of water to form a slurry, then pour it into the pan and stir until the buffalo sauce thickens and clings to the chicken. Remove from heat and let cool slightly. Flatten each dough ball into a circular shape. Spoon 1/8 of the buffalo chicken mixture into the centre, top with 25g of low-fat cheese, then fold in the sides and pinch the edges to seal. Gently press down to form the hot pocket shape. Spray a pan with cooking oil and cook the hot pockets over medium heat with the lid on for 3–4 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through. Let cool completely before wrapping each one in foil. Store in the freezer for later. To reheat, remove the foil, wrap the pocket in a damp paper towel, and microwave for 4–5 minutes. Finish in the air fryer or a hot pan for 1–2 minutes to bring back the crisp. Serve hot and enjoy!

# honey buffalo chicken tenders

Servings: 11 | Calories: 86kcal | Protein: 10g | Carbs: 9g | Fat: 1g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**chicken tenders**

- 400g chicken tender loins (or chicken breast cut into strips)
- 45ml franks red hot sauce (or hot sauce of choice)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp cayenne
- 1 tbsp pickle juice
- 1 tsp salt and pepper

**crispy coating**

- 90g cornflakes
- 1 tsp salt and pepper
- 1 large egg
- 20ml franks red hot

**Honey Buffalo sauce**

- 120ml franks red hot
- 25g honey
- 15-20ml pickle juice
- 15g light butter

## Instructions

1. Add the chicken tenderloins to a bowl along with 45ml of Frank's Red Hot, garlic powder, paprika, cayenne, salt, pepper, and a splash of pickle juice for flavor. Mix well until fully coated. In a separate bowl, whisk together one egg and 20ml of Frank's Red Hot sauce. Place cornflakes seasoned with salt and pepper on another plate, partially crushed. Dip each tender in the egg mixture, then coat with the cornflakes, pressing down firmly for an even coating. Place them on a baking tray and repeat for all tenders. Lightly spray the tenders with cooking spray and bake or air fry at 200°C (400°F) for 12-15 minutes, flipping halfway through. Meanwhile make the honey buffalo sauce by adding butter, Franks red hot, honey and pickle juice to a pan. Simmer on a medium low heat till it thickens up, Pour the buffalo sauce over the tenders and enjoy! BONUS: You can freeze the tenders to enjoy later! Once cooked allow them to completely cool, then store in a ziplock or vacuum seal bag (remove as much air as possible to prevent freezer burn). When it’s time to enjoy, reheat in the airfryer at 200°C (400°F) for 8-10 minutes till crispy or if using the oven, 200°C (400°F) for 20-25 minutes, flip halfway.

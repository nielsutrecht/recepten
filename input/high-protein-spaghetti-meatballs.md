# High protein spaghetti meatballs

Servings: 4 | Calories: 570kcal | Protein: 52g | Carbs: 68g | Fat: 10g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Juicy Meatballs**

- 650g lean ground beef (5% or less)
- 2 garlic cloves (diced)

**⅓ cup finely chopped basil**

- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp dried sage
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 15g grated Parmesan or Pecorino
- 30g Panko Bread crumbs
- 1 large egg cooking spray

**tomato sauce**

- 4 garlic cloves (diced)
- 1 tsp chilli flakes
- 800g crushed tomatoes
- 5-10g 0 calorie sweetener of choice (I used stevia)
- salt to taste (taste and test)

**Spaghetti**

- 300g spaghetti pasta (uncooked weight)
- reserve 30-50ml pasta water

**Other ingredients**


**fresh chopped basil (optional)**


**grated Parmesan (optional)**


## Instructions

1. Add your lean ground beef to a bowl with diced garlic, finely chopped basil, grated Parmesan or Pecorino, onion powder, salt, dried sage, panko breadcrumbs, and one egg. Mix everything well with your hands to incorporate, then divide the beef into 20 equal-sized meatballs. Set a pan to high heat, add some cooking spray, and cook the meatballs until they're about halfway done and have a crispy sear on all sides. Set the meatballs aside, then in the same pan on medium heat, add diced garlic and chili flakes, stirring until fragrant. Add crushed tomatoes, zerocalorie sweetener, and salt to taste, making sure to taste test while adding the salt. Bring the sauce to a light bubble, then add the meatballs and simmer for 10 minutes on low heat. Boil your pasta in salted water until al dente, then add it to your saucy meatballs along with a splash of pasta water, mixing well until fully coated. Divide the spaghetti and meatballs into four equal servings. Place them in meal-prep containers and refrigerate for up to five days. To reheat, add a teaspoon of water to the pasta, partially cover, and microwave on high for 3-4 minutes. Stir with a fork and enjoy!

# high protein Sausage rolls

Servings: 11 | Calories: 63kcal | Protein: 4g | Carbs: 9g | Fat: 1g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**pastry**

- 100g plain flour
- 120g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 1 egg (for egg wash adds around 5 calories to recipe) pinch of salt

**other ingredients**

- 150g (3) Lean pork sausages (brand:
- Tesco 50% less fat cumberland sausages)

**Notes You can store these**

- refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 5 days, Enjoy throughout the week or in one go!

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F), then start by making the pastry dough. In a bowl add 100g plain flour, 120g 0% Greek yogurt and a pinch of salt, mix with a fork until you have doughly clumps and then knead with your hands to form a dough (avoid handling it with your palms too much or it will become sticky) Next, prepare the sausages. Remove the skins by making a slit along each one and peeling away the casing, then wet your hands and form it out into one long sausage. Roll out the dough on a lightly floured surface into roughly a 4" by 12" rectangle, about 1/4 inch thick. Then place the sausage meat on the lower portion of the pastry and mould it if needed to fit the entire length. gently lift the edge of the pastry which the sausage meat is on and roll it over to form one big sausage roll, seal it with some egg wash Cut the giant sausage roll into mini sausage roll sizes roughly an inch thick, you should get 11-12 rolls. Brush each one with some egg wash for that golden color when baked then place on a lined baking tray and bake for 15-20 minutes, until golden brown and cooked through. Cool before serving and enjoy!

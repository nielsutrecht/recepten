# Cinnamon roll baked oats

Servings: 6 | Calories: 377kcal | Protein: 20g | Carbs: 62g | Fat: 10g
Section: breakfast

- 60g cinnamon cereal protein

- 1/3 cup 0 cal icing sugar

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 180°C (360°F) and lightly grease a 12” x 9” (30cm x 23cm) baking dish. In one bowl, mix together the oats, vanilla protein powder, brown stevia, baking powder, cinnamon, nutmeg and a pinch of salt. In a separate bowl, mash the bananas. Add in eggs, almond milk, honey, melted butter and vanilla extract, whisking until smooth. Gradually mix the wet ingredients with the dry, stirring until combined. Pour the mixture into your prepared baking dish, spreading it out for an even bake. Bake at 180°C (360°F) for 30-35 minutes. Meanwhile make the glaze by mixing the powdered sugar substitute with with water and stirring into a glaze. Let the cooked oatmeal cool for 5-10 minutes before drizzling over the glaze and allowing to set. Cut into six portions and store in refrigerated meal prep containers for up to six days. When you’re ready to eat, enjoy cold or reheat it in the microwave for 2 minutes and enjoy!

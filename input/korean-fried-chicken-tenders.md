# Korean Fried chicken tenders

Servings: 6 | Calories: 192kcal | Protein: 20g | Carbs: 26g | Fat: 1g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**chicken**

- 500g raw chicken breast (cut into 6 strips)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp white pepper
- ½ cayenne
- ½ tsp ginger

**flour station**

- 120g self-raising flour (sub with plain
- flour and - 1.5 tsp baking powder
- 60g potato starch (sub with cornstarch)
- ½ same seasonings as the chicken

**Wet station**

- ½ cup flour mixture
- ½ cup sparkling water
- (Add a spoonful to the flour mixture
- to make craggly bits - this will make crispier tenders)

**Korean glaze**

- 2 tbsp soy sauce (low sodium)
- 60ml water
- 45g low sugar ketchup (brand: Heinz)
- 2 tbsp gochujang chilli paste (use a
- less spicy one for less heat)
- 30g honey
- 1 tbsp brown stevia (brand: pure via)

**Garnishes**


**Finely sliced green onions**


## Instructions

1. Season the chicken strips with garlic powder, onion powder, salt, white pepper, cayenne, and ginger. Next set up a flour station and a wet station. In the flour station, mix self-raising flour (or plain flour + baking powder), potato starch, and half of the seasonings used on the chicken. In the wet station, combine ½ cup of the flour mixture with sparkling water to create a batter. Add a spoonful of batter to the flour mixture and mix with a spoon to create craggly bits for extra crispiness. Dip each chicken strip into the flour first, then into the wet batter, letting excess drip off, then back into the flour, to get the craggly bits. to adhere. Lightly spray with cooking oil and place in the air fryer basket. Air fry for 25 minutes at 180°C / 375°F, flipping halfway and respraying with cooking oil. They should come out golden and crispy. For the Korean Glaze. In a small pan over low heat, combine soy sauce, water, ketchup, gochujang, honey, and brown stevia. Stir and simmer until thickened. Brush crispy chicken tenders in the Korean glaze. Garnish with finely sliced green onions and your ready to enjoy!

# Chilli-Garlic CHicken fried rice

Servings: 4 | Calories: 493kcal | Protein: 50g | Carbs: 53g | Fat: 9g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**crispy chicken**

- 600g chicken breast (cubed)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp black pepper pepper
- 25ml light soy sauce (brand:

**Tesco)**


**CHilli garlic sauce**

- 80g reduced sugar sweet chilli

**sauce (brand: Blue Dragon)**

- 2 cloves garlic (finely chopped) tbsp light soy sauce

## Instructions

1. Start by dicing the chicken into cubes and seasoning with garlic powder, black pepper, and light soy sauce. If possible, allow it to marinate in the fridge for 30 minutes.

2. Egg fried rice

3. Cook the marinated chicken in. a pan over medium-high heat with cooking spray

4. 600g day-old Refrigerated cooked basmati rice (200g uncooked) 3 eggs (beaten) 25g chopped green onions (white parts only) 3 cloves minced garlic 200g frozen mixed veg (carrots, sweetcorn, peas) 25ml light soy sauce tbsp oyster sauce (brand: Tesco) 10ml toasted sesame oil (More flavour than regular) - sub with regular

5. for 8-10 minutes until it's golden and crispy. Then in a small bowl, combine reduced sugar sweet chilli sauce, finely chopped garlic, and light soy sauce. Pour this chilli-garlic sauce over the cooked chicken and mix well to ensure each piece is coated. For the egg fried rice, in the same pan sauté minced garlic and the white parts of green onions in a pan. Then add the frozen mixed vegetables and cook until they soften. Push the vegetables to one side of the pan. In the cleared space, pour in the beaten eggs and scramble over low heat until they're fluffy. Add the day-old refrigerated cooked rice to the pan, breaking it up first. Stir in light soy sauce, oyster sauce, and toasted sesame oil, combining everything well

6. garnish chopped Green onions - (green parts only) sprinkle of seasme seeds

7. for a flavourful egg fried rice. . To serve, place the chilli-garlic chicken over the egg fried rice. Garnish with the green parts of the green onions and a sprinkle of sesame seeds. Divide the mixture into four equal portions for meal prep. Store in the refrigerator for up to 5 days. To reheat, microwave the portion partially covered for 3-4 minutes. Enjoy!

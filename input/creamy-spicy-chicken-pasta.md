# Creamy spicy Chicken pasta

Servings: 4 | Calories: 561kcal | Protein: 56g | Carbs: 64g | Fat: 9g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**seasoned chicken**

- 650g chicken breast (diced into bite size cubes) tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp cayenne
- 1 tsp salt & pepper cooking spray

**Creamy spicy sauce**

- 200ml light evaporated milk (brand: carnation)
- 100g tomato sauce/passata (brand Tesco)
- 120ml chicken stock (low sodium)
- 80g light cream cheese (brand:

**Philadelphia lightest)**

- 80ml pasta water (reserved from cooking pasta)
- 40g Parmesan
- 1 tsp paprika
- salt to taste (taste sauce and adjust)
- 4 garlic cloves
- 1 tsp chilli flakes
- 15g light butter

**Garnishes**

- 300g penne pasta (dry weight) fresh chopped parsley grated parmesan

## Instructions

1. Season the diced chicken with garlic powder, onion powder, cayenne, salt, and pepper. Heat a pan over medium-high heat, spray with cooking oil, and cook the chicken until golden brown and fully cooked. Remove from the pan and set aside. Next cook the pasta in salted water until al dente, reserving 80ml of pasta water before draining. In the same pan used for the chicken, melt the butter over medium heat. Add minced garlic and chili flakes, sautéing until fragrant. Stir in the evaporated milk, tomato sauce, chicken stock, cream cheese, paprika, and parmesan. Simmer until smooth, then add the reserved pasta water. Taste and adjust seasoning if needed. Return the cooked chicken and pasta to the pan, stirring to coat in the sauce. mix until everything is well combined and heated through. Garnish with fresh chopped parsley and extra grated parmesan Divide the creamy pasta into 4 equal meals and store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, microwave partially covered for 3-4 minutes and stir till creamy. Enjoy!

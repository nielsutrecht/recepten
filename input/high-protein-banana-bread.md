# High protein banana bread

Servings: 8 | Calories: 142kcal | Protein: 14g | Carbs: 19g | Fat: 2g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**Dry ingredients**

- 90g self-raising flour
- 90g vanilla protein powder (I use Protein Works)
- 2 tbsp brown stevia
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp cinnamon pinch salt

**wet ingredients**

- 2 large egg
- 260g 0% greek yoghurt
- 70g unsweetened apple sauce (I easily made my own but you can use store bought)
- 2 medium overripe bananas
- 30g blueberries

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F). In a bowl, combine self-raising flour, vanilla whey protein, brown stevia, baking soda, cinnamon, and a pinch of salt. In a separate bowl, mash two overripe bananas. Whisk in eggs, 0% Greek yogurt, and unsweetened apple sauce until smooth. Gradually mix the wet ingredients into the dry ones until combined. Gently fold in 30g of blueberries then transfer the batter to a lined loaf tin. Sprinkle a few additional blueberries on top. Bake for 25-30 minutes. Allow it to cool before slicing. Enjoy your high-protein banana bread!

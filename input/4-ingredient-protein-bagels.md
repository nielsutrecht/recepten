# 4-ingredient protein bagels

Servings: 4 | Calories: 254kcal | Protein: 18g | Carbs: 44g | Fat: 1g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 250g bread flour (has extra protein and helps with the bagel texture)
- 20g baking powder salt to taste
- 450g 0% Greek yoghurt sesame seeds (garnish) egg wash (1 egg + tbsp water)

## Instructions

1. Mix the flour, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl. Add the Greek yoghurt and stir with a spoon until clumps start to form. Bring the clumps together with your hands, kneading gently until you have a smooth dough ball. Avoid over-handling to prevent stickiness — you can add a light dusting of extra flour if needed, but keep it minimal to avoid adding

2. Notes You can use these for whatever you like! I made a recipe for some breakfast bagels which use these!

3. calories. Divide the dough into four equal pieces. On a lightly floured surface, roll each piece into a log roughly 11 inches long. Brush one end with a little egg wash, overlap slightly with the other end, and pinch together to seal, forming a bagel shape. Make sure the join is secure so it holds its shape while baking. Place the bagels on a lined baking tray and brush the tops lightly with egg wash. Sprinkle with sesame seeds — a mix of regular and toasted works well. Bake at 180°C (fan) or 200°C (without fan) for 25–30 minutes, until golden brown and risen. Transfer to a wire rack to cool. Once cooled, slice and store in the fridge, or slice and freeze for convenience. They work well for anything from sandwiches to breakfast prep — see the recipe notes for my high-protein breakfast bagel variation.

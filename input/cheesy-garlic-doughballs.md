# Cheesy Garlic DOughballs

Servings: 8 | Calories: 112kcal | Protein: 8g | Carbs: 11g | Fat: 4g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**dough**

- 120g bread flour (high in protein - sub with AP flour)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 180g 0% Greek yoghurt

**other ingredients**

- 120g mozzarella cheese slices (sliced into small squares) parsley flakes (optional) cooking spray (I used avocado oil) (I didn’t count basic seasonings and cooking spray as main ingredients because you probably already have them)

## Instructions

1. Add the bread flour, garlic powder, salt, and baking powder to a mixing bowl and stir to combine. Add the Greek yoghurt and mix until clumps start to form, then bring the dough together with your hands and knead until smooth. Transfer the dough to a lightly floured surface, roll it into a ball, and divide into 8 equal pieces. Roll each piece into a small ball, then gently flatten it. Place a square of mozzarella in the centre and fold the dough around it, pinching the edges to fully seal in the cheese. Repeat with all 8 pieces. Arrange the dough balls on a lined baking tray, spray lightly with cooking spray, and sprinkle with parsley flakes if using. Bake or air fry at 180°C (355°F) for 12–15 minutes, or until golden, puffed, and lightly crisp on the outside. Serve hot with your favourite dip — they’re soft, cheesy, and around 8g of protein each, perfect as a quick high-protein snack and super filling too!

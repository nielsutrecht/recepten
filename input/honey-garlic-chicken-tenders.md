# honey GARLIC chicken tenders

Servings: 6 | Calories: 182kcal | Protein: 22g | Carbs: 19g | Fat: 3g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**chicken**

- 500g chicken breast (2-3 chicken breasts)

**flour station**

- 100g plain flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp black pepper

**egg station**

- 1 beaten egg
- 1 tbsp pickle juice

**crispy station**

- cornflakes (90g for tracking)

**Honey garlic glaze**

- 15g unsalted butter
- 4 tbsp of soy sauce
- 3 tbsp honey
- 2 tsp garlic paste
- 1 tsp chilli flakes
- To start, mix the plain flour, salt, onion powder, garlic powder, paprika,
- and black pepper on a plate for the flour station.
- Then, slice each chicken breast into three strips and thoroughly coat
- them on all sides with the seasoned flour mixture. For the egg station,
- beat an egg with a splash of pickle juice on a separate plate, and then
- dunk the flour-coated chicken strips into it until fully coated.
- On the final plate, add the cornflakes and lightly crush them. Press
- each egg-coated chicken strip firmly into the cornflakes to achieve an
- even and crispy coating on all sides, then lightly spray the coated
- tenders with cooking oil. Bake them at 190°C (390°F) for 20-25
- minutes or air fry at 180°C (375°F) for 12-15 minutes, making sure to

**flip them halfway through.**


## Instructions

1. Meanwhile, melt some butter in a pan over low heat, and add the soy sauce, honey, garlic paste, and chili flakes. Bring the mixture to a bubble and stir until the sauce thickens. Now, place the cooked crispy tenders into the pan and roll them around in the sauce until they're fully coated, then serve and enjoy!

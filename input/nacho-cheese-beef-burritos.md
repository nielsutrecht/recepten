# nacho cheese beef burritos

Servings: 6 | Calories: 564kcal | Protein: 57g | Carbs: 38g | Fat: 20g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**taco beef**

- 1kg lean ground beef (5% or less)
- 1 medium red onion (diced)
- 1 green bell pepper (diced)
- 1 red bell pepper (diced)
- 1 (17g) Taco seasoning

**packet (brand: Old El Paso)**

- 130g low sugar ketchup
- 150ml water
- Tbsp worcestershire sauce (optional) cooking spray

**nacho cheese**

- 230ml light evaporated milk (brand: carnation)
- 180g Mexican cheese blend

**(brand: Tesco chilli cheese mix)**

- 30ml hot sauce (brand:

**franks red hot)**


**other ingredients**

- 6 protein tortillas (brand:

**mission)**


## Instructions

1. To a pan on high heat with some cooking spray, add your lean ground beef. Let it get a crispy sear on one side for a few minutes, then flip it over and break it up. Now add in the diced onion and bell peppers along with some taco seasoning and stir fry until the beef is cooked through and the vegetables are softened. Add in some low-sugar ketchup, water, and optional Worcestershire sauce. Continue to mix until the beef is fully coated. For the nacho cheese, in a pan on medium heat, add evaporated milk, a Mexican cheese blend, and hot sauce. Continually stir until the cheese is fully melted. To assemble your burritos, first warm up your tortillas in a pan to get them soft and flexible (one burrito at a time). Lay it down and add 1/6 of the nacho cheese sauce (weigh it out, then take 1/6). Spread the nacho cheese on the middle third of the tortilla, then add 1/6 of the taco beef mix on top. Roll it up tightly into a burrito and toast in the pan to seal the burrito. Wrap the burritos in foil and store in the freezer for up to two months. To reheat, remove the foil, wrap in a damp paper towel, and microwave on a plate for 5-6 minutes, flipping over halfway. Unwrap carefully from the paper towel and enjoy! Optionally, toast in the pan after reheating to get it crispy!

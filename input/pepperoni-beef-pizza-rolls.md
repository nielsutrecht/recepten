# pepperoni & beef pizza rolls

Servings: 10 | Calories: 380kcal | Protein: 38g | Carbs: 27g | Fat: 13g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**Pizza roll mix**

- 800g lean ground beef (5% or less)
- 100g low fat pepperoni (I used
- Turkey pepperoni, regular or beef pepperoni also works)
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp Italian herbs
- 1-2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 400g tomato Passata (brand:

**Tesco)**

- 45g tomato paste
- 75g light cream cheese (brand:

**Philadelphia lightest)**

- 10g sugar or sweetener chopped parsley

**other ingredients**

- 125g grated mozzarella
- 125g grated low fat cheddar (brand: Tesco 50% less) chopped parsley
- 10 protein tortillas (brand:

**mission)**

- Preheat the oven to 200ºC or the air fryer to 180ºC.

## Instructions

1. Heat a pan over high heat and spray with cooking oil. Add the ground beef in chunks and sear until about ¾ done. Add the sliced pepperoni and continue cooking until the beef is browned. While browning, season with onion powder, garlic powder, smoked paprika, Italian herbs, salt, and black pepper. Stir in the tomato passata, tomato paste, cream cheese, and sugar or sweetener. Simmer until thickened, then mix in fresh chopped parsley. Remove from heat. To assemble the Rolls: Lay out a warm tortilla and spoon 1/10 of the filling onto the lower third. Sprinkle with grated mozzarella and cheddar, then roll tightly, folding in the sides as you go. Repeat for all 10 tortillas. place the rolls on a baking sheet and lightly spray with cooking oil, then sprinkle with chopped parsley. Bake or air-fry for 8-10 minutes until golden and crispy then allow to cool. Wrap each roll in foil and store in the freezer. To reheat, remove the foil, wrap the roll in a paper towel and run it under water to soak the paper towel. Squeeze off excess water with your hands then place the burrito on a plate and microwave for 4-5 minutes. Unwrap carefully from the paper towel. then air-fry or pan toast on high heat for a couple minutes to get crispy, enjoy!

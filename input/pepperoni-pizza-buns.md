# Pepperoni Pizza BUns

Servings: 4 | Calories: 361kcal | Protein: 31g | Carbs: 37g | Fat: 9g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 180g self-raising flour
- 270g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 180g low fat grated cheese (I used 50% less fat)
- 80g turkey or beef pepperoni pizza sauce (2-3 tsp per bun)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1/2 tsp salt

## Instructions

1. Add the self-raising flour, Greek yoghurt, garlic powder, oregano, and salt to a bowl. Mix until clumps form, then bring everything together with your hands and knead into a smooth dough ball. Transfer the dough to a lightly floured surface, roll it out, and divide into eight equal pieces. Flatten each piece into a small circle and place them onto a lined baking tray. Add 2–3 teaspoons of pizza sauce to the centre of each, then top with low-fat grated cheese and slices of turkey or beef pepperoni. Fold the dough over to close the bun, pressing the edges lightly, then add a little more pizza sauce, cheese, and pepperoni on top so they brown nicely in the oven. Bake at 180°C (355°F) for 15–18 minutes or until the buns are golden, puffed, and the cheese is melted and bubbling. Let them cool slightly before eating. These are high-protein, super satisfying, and perfect for wrapping up to take to work or school. To reheat, remove any foil and microwave or air-fry for a couple of minutes until hot and cheesy.

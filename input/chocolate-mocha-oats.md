# Chocolate mocha oats

Servings: 1 | Calories: 486kcal | Protein: 42g | Carbs: 56g | Fat: 10g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**coffee filling**

- 40g rolled oats
- 160g 0% greek yoghurt
- 60ml almond milk
- 25g chocolate protein powder (I use Protein Works) tbsp chia seeds
- 1 ½ tsp instant coffee
- ½ tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tbsp maple syrup

**topping**

- 5g dark chocolate

## Instructions

1. In a mixing jug, combine almond milk, maple syrup, vanilla extract, and instant coffee. Stir until the coffee is dissolved. Add Greek yogurt, rolled oats, chia seeds, cinnamon, and your chocolate protein powder. Mix until well combined. Transfer the mixture into a mason jar or container. Shave 5g of dark chocolate with a sharp knife or micro grater and sprinkle it over the top. Seal the jar and refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight. Enjoy!

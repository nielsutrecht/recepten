# Steak Egg & Cheese Burrito

Servings: 4 | Calories: 562kcal | Protein: 62g | Carbs: 13g | Fat: 29g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**Taco ground Steak**

- 800g lean ground steak (5% or less)
- 2 tsp paprika
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp cayenne
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp oregano
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp black pepper
- 250ml basic tomato sauce or tomato passata cooking oil spray

**Fluffy scrambled eggs**

- 12 eggs (beaten)
- 15g light butter salt & pepper to taste

**Creamy avocado sauce**

- 1 avocado
- 160g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 60ml fat free or low fat milk
- 1 tsp honey Juice of half a lime salt and pepper to taste

**other ingredients**

- 6 low calorie tortillas (brand:

**mission low carb balance)**

- 180g low fat grated cheese
- 240g (12) low fat cheese slices (brand: Tesco)

## Instructions

1. Heat a pan over high heat and lightly spray with cooking oil. Add the ground steak in small handfuls and cook till it gets a crispy sear, break it apart, then stir in paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, cayenne, cumin, oregano, salt, and black pepper. Pour in the tomato sauce or passata, then simmer and stir on low heat until thickened. Set aside covered. For the fluffy scrambled eggs. Melt butter in a pan over low heat. Add the beaten eggs, seasoning with salt and pepper. Stir continuously, cooking gently until soft and creamy. Remove from heat and cover. Now in a blender add the avocado with greek yogurt, milk, honey, lime juice, salt, pepper and blend till completely smooth. To assemble the burritos, Lay a tortilla flat and place two slices of low-fat cheese in the center. Spoon scrambled eggs on top, followed by the taco steak. Drizzle with avocado sauce, then sprinkle with grated low fat cheese. Fold in the sides, roll tightly, and repeat with the remaining tortillas till you have 6 burritos. Toast in the pan with light butter to seal. Once cooled wrap the burrito in foil and store in the freezer for months. To reheat, remove the foil, wrap in a paper towel and run under water to soak the paper towel. Squeeze off excess water with your hands then place the burrito on a plate and microwave for 6-7 minutes. unwrap carefully from the paper towel. then air-fry or pan toast on high heat for a couple minutes to get crispy, enjoy!

# Jamaican cheesy beef patties

Servings: 7 | Calories: 358kcal | Protein: 25g | Carbs: 24g | Fat: 18g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**FlakEy pastry**

- 240g plain flour
- 100g unsalted butter
- 100ml milk
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp curry powder
- 1 tsp ground turmeric pinch of salt

**Spicy beef filling**

- 500g 5% beef mince
- 1 large white onion (diced)
- 3 cloves (minced)
- 1 tsp ground ginger
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp cayenne
- 1 tsp dried thyme salt and pepper to taste
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 100ml beef stock (brand: oxo 25% less sodium) cooking spray

**other ingredients**

- 140g reduced fat grated
- cheddar -20g per patty (brand:
- Aldi 50% less)
- 1 beaten egg (egg wash)

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, mix flour, baking powder, curry powder, turmeric, and salt. Add 100g of unsalted butter (cut into small cubes) and mix with your hands to achieve a golden crumbly mixture. Incorporate 100ml milk, knead to form a dough, then wrap with cling film, and chill in the fridge. For the filling, season beef with garlic powder, paprika, cayenne, ginger, thyme, salt, and pepper. Sauté onions in a sprayed, high-heat pan until soft, add garlic until fragrant, then add in the beef and brown. Mix in soy sauce and beef stock, simmering on medium-low to reduce. Optionally, blend 1/3 of the beef for texture to achieve the authentic filling texture. Roll out the chilled dough to 0.5cm thickness and cut seven circles 6" inches across (I used a small bowl). Add 1/7 of the filling and 20g light grated cheddar to each. Lightly apply egg wash on the edges with your finger then fold, and crimp edges with a fork. Place on a tray, brush each one in egg wash, and bake at 200°C (400°F) for 20-25 minutes till slightly golden brown. Serve hot or refrigerate for up to 6 days, reheating in the oven or air fryer for 3-4 minutes till hot. BONUS: You can freeze the pre-cooked patties to enjoy later at your convenience! Cook the frozen patties in the oven at 200°C (400°F) for 2530 minutes, Enjoy!

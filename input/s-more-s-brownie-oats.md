# S’more’s brownie oats

Servings: 1 | Calories: 515kcal | Protein: 44g | Carbs: 61g | Fat: 10g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**S’Mores Filling**

- 40g rolled oats
- 150g 0% fat Greek yoghurt
- 90ml almond milk
- 25g chocolate whey protein (I

**use Protein Works chocolate silk)**

- 1 tsp cocoa powder
- 0 cal Sweetener (I used stevia)
- 10g dark chocolate (brand:

**tesco)**

- 15g mini marshmallows pinch of salt

**Toppings**

- 1 rich tea biscuit - sub with
- 10g graham crackers
- 5g mini marshmallows dusting of cocoa powder

## Notes

Bonus: I like to microwave the opened jar for 30 seconds to a minute to melt the marshmallows before enjoying! Tastes unreal... Begin by adding the dark chocolate to a small bowl with a tsp of almond milk and microwave in 10 second intervals, stirring until melted.

## Instructions

1. In a mixing jug, combine the oats, Greek yogurt, milk, cocoa powder, chocolate protein powder. sweetener, melted chocolate, mini marshmallows and a pinch of salt. Mix everything together until well combined. Into a mason jar or container, pour in 1/2 the mixture, followed by 1/2 the rich tea biscuit, crumbled up. Pour in the remaining mixture and top with the rest of the crumbled biscuit, mini marshmallows and a dusting of cocoa powder Seal the jar and refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight. Enjoy!

# Cheesy breakfast rolls

Servings: 10 | Calories: 382kcal | Protein: 41g | Carbs: 9g | Fat: 19g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**Taco ground beef**

- 800g lean beef mince
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp cayenne
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp oregano
- 2 tsp sea salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 200g enchilada sauce

**Fluffy scrambled eggs**

- 10 whole eggs salt to taste

**cooking spray or light butter**


**other ingredients**

- 20 slices light cheese (brand:

**Tesco)**

- 250g low fat grated cheese (I used 50% less fat)

**Chopped parsley (garnish)**

- 10 low calorie tortillas (brand: mission carb balance)

## Instructions

1. Heat a pan over high heat and spray lightly with cooking oil. Add the minced beef in chunks, working slowly so you don’t overcrowd or cool the pan — this helps develop crispier browned edges. Press each chunk flat with your spatula and sear for 2–3 minutes before breaking it up. Cook until fully browned, then stir in the taco seasoning and enchilada sauce. Mix well until everything is coated and saucy. Set aside. In a separate pan, melt some light butter or spray with cooking oil and pour in the beaten eggs. Scramble gently over medium heat, stirring every 10–20 seconds until soft, fluffy and just cooked through. Remove from heat and let cool slightly. To assemble, lay out all 10 tortillas. On each one, place 2 slices of light cheese, followed by 1/10th of the scrambled eggs (weigh and divide evenly), then 1/10th of the taco beef, and finish with 25g of grated low-fat cheese. Fold in the sides, then roll over tightly, tuck and roll until sealed. Repeat for the remaining tortillas. Place all the rolls on a baking sheet and give them a light spray of cooking oil. Sprinkle with fresh or dried parsley, then bake at 200ºC/400ºF for 8–10 minutes or airfry at 180ºC/355ºF for 6–8 minutes until golden and crispy. Allow to cool completely before wrapping each roll in foil. For storage, freeze for up to 2 months. To reheat, unwrap the foil, wrap the frozen roll in a damp paper towel, and gently squeeze out any excess water. Microwave for 4–5 minutes, then unwrap and toast in an air fryer or hot pan for a couple minutes to bring back that crispy finish. Serve warm with your favorite dipping sauce and enjoy!

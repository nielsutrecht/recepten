# Grilled Chicken & Halloumi wrap

Servings: 1 | Calories: 488kcal | Protein: 52g | Carbs: 29g | Fat: 17g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**Main ingredients**

- 125g chicken breast
- 30g sliced Reduced fat halloumi (brand: Tesco)
- 1 low calorie tortilla (brand: Weight Watchers)
- 30g sliced avocado
- 50g Shredded lettuce tsp Paprika
- ½ tsp Cayenne
- 1 tsp Garlic powder
- ½ tsp Onion powder
- ½ tsp Oregano
- ½ tsp Chilli flakes
- 1 tsp Salt & pepper juice of 1 lemon

**Lime sauce**

- 50g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 15g sriracha hot sauce Juice of ½ a lime
- 1 tsp chopped coriander Salt & pepper
- 5 -10ml water

## Instructions

1. Slice the chicken into thin strips and season with paprika, cayenne, garlic powder, onion powder, oregano, salt, pepper, and lemon juice. in a pan on medium-high heat with cooking spray, cook the chicken until golden (8-10 minutes) then set aside. In the same pan, fry sliced halloumi for 1-2 minutes until golden, being cautious not to burn it. Prepare lime sauce in a small bowl by combining Greek yoghurt, sriracha, lime juice, chopped cilantro, salt, pepper and water for consistency. Mix until smooth. Assemble the wrap with shredded lettuce, sliced avocado, cooked chicken, and halloumi. Drizzle with lime sauce, then fold into a wrap, and toast in the pan for 1-2 minutes to crisp. Enjoy!

# Cheesy breakfast quesadilla

Servings: 1 | Calories: 491kcal | Protein: 46g | Carbs: 33g | Fat: 19g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients

- 1 Aldi protein tortilla (or low cal tortilla)
- 2 eggs
- 30g grated mozzarella
- 90g (3) bacon medallions (short-cut bacon) salt and pepper Chopped parsley (garnish) low cal cooking spray
- Notes The bacon can be substituted with beef/turkey bacon or cooked Deli chicken slices! Adjust tracking accordingly.

## Instructions

1. crack the eggs in a bowl, season with salt and pepper and whisk with a fork till beaten heat a pan to high heat, add some cooking spray and cook the bacon medallions till crispy. Then set the bacon aside, turn the heat to low and pour the eggs into the pan spreading them out evenly to cover the bottom of the pan. once the eggs are partially cooked on top, place the tortilla on top and cook for a further 1-2 minutes till the eggs are cooked. Turn the eggy tortilla over and sprinkle grated mozzarella on one half, placing the cooked bacon medallions on top. Fold the uncovered side over on top and allow the cheese to melt for a further 1-2 minutes. Flip and cook on the other side for a further 30 seconds then serve onto a plate Slice into wedges and optionally garnish with fresh chopped parsley. Enjoy!

# steak & Nacho Cheese Potatoes

Servings: 4 | Calories: 543kcal | Protein: 56g | Carbs: 65g | Fat: 7g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Taco Steak bites**

- 900g lean diced steak (I used flank steak)
- 1.4 tsp smoked paprika
- 1.5 tsp garlic powder
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp cayenne
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp oregano Cooking spray

**Crispy potato cubes**

- 1000g peeled diced potatoes
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp pepper
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- any other seasonings you wish Cooking oil spray

**Nacho cheese sauce**

- 135ml 0% fat milk
- 5 light cheese singles (brand Tesco)
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp cayenne

## Instructions

1. Start by dicing the potatoes into roughly 1-inch cubes. Place them in a pot of room temperature water and bring to a boil. Once boiling, set a timer for 5 minutes to parboil. Drain and spread the potatoes out on a tray lined with paper towel to steam-dry for a few minutes. Once dry, spray lightly with cooking oil and season with salt, pepper, smoked paprika, and garlic powder — mix to coat evenly. Bake the seasoned potatoes at 220ºC/430ºF for 25–30 minutes, or air-fry at 200ºC/400ºF for 20–25 minutes until golden and crispy. For the nacho cheese sauce, blend the light cheese singles, milk, garlic powder, paprika, cayenne, and salt until smooth. Transfer the mix to a saucepan over medium heat and stir continuously until thickened and creamy. Set aside. Dice the steak into bite-sized cubes. In a bowl, season with smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, cayenne, cumin, oregano, salt, and black pepper. Heat a pan over high heat with cooking spray and sear the steak for 30 seconds to 1 minute per side until golden and just cooked through. Set aside.

2. Charred veg 1 red bell pepper sliced into petals 1 medium white onion sliced into petals Cooking spray

3. Slice the onion and red bell pepper into petal shapes and sauté in a hot pan until soft and slightly charred. To assemble, divide the crispy potatoes, taco steak, sautéed veg, and any garnishes between four meal prep containers. Store the cheese sauce separately in small tubs. Refrigerate everything for up to 5 days. To reheat, microwave or pan-fry the main portion until hot, then warm the cheese sauce separately and drizzle it over the top. Serve hot and enjoy.

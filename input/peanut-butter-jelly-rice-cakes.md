# peanut butter & jelly Rice cakes

Servings: 1 | Calories: 248kcal | Protein: 12g | Carbs: 41g | Fat: 4g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 3 rice cakes (7g each)
- 3 tbsp PBfit Powder
- 2 tbsp water
- 30g reduced sugar strawberry jam (brand: Tesco)
- Notes I get my PBfit powder from amazon and I can't recommend it enough!

**It’s a lower calorie and**

- higher protein alternative to peanut butter and the taste is genuinely
- amazing... If you can’t get it where you are,

**substitute with regular peanut butter.**

- in a small bowl mix together the PBfit powder and
- water until it reaches a smooth peanut butter consistency.
- spread 1/3 of the peanut butter onto each rice cake,
- followed by 10g of reduced sugar jam. Enjoy straight
- away! I like to make them as a quick pre-workout

**snack / late night dessert.**

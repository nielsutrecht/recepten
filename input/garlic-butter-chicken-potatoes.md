# Garlic butter chicken & potatoes

Servings: 4 | Calories: 511kcal | Protein: 50g | Carbs: 47g | Fat: 11g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Garlic butter chicken**

- 800g diced chicken breast
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 onion powder
- 1 smoked paprika
- 2 tsp salt & pepper
- 20g plain flour
- 30g corn flour
- 60ml water Cooking spray

**Crispy potatoes**

- 900g potatoes (diced into cubes)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 2 tsp salt & pepper
- 1 tsp parsley cooking spray

**garlic butter**

- 50g unsalted butter (melted)
- 4 garlic cloves (finely diced)
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce chopped parsley

**Garlic Aioli - optional side**

- 50g lighter than light mayo (brand:

**hellmans)**

- 15g light cream cheese
- 2 garlic cloves (minced)
- 1-2 tsp lemon juice salt to taste

**splash of water for consistency**


## Instructions

1. Dice the potatoes into cubes and add them to a bowl. Season with paprika, onion powder, garlic powder, parsley, salt, and pepper. Spray lightly with cooking oil and mix well. Air-fry at 180°C (375°F) for 30-35 minutes, shaking halfway through for even crispiness. If using an oven, bake at 200°C (430°F) for 35-40 minutes, shaking halfway. Meanwhile, dice the chicken breast into cubes and season with garlic powder, onion powder, salt, pepper, plain flour, cornflour, and 60ml of water. Mix well to coat, then set the chicken on a rack and air fry at 200ºC for 10-12 minutes or bake in the oven at 220ºC for 15-18 minutes, shaking halfway for both methods. To make the garlic butter, melt unsalted butter in a pan over medium heat and add diced garlic, soy sauce, and chopped parsley. Stir to combine the flavors, then add the cooked chicken to the pan and toss to coat in the garlic butter. For the optional homemade garlic aioli, combine lighter than light mayo, light cream cheese, minced garlic, lemon juice, and salt to taste in a small bowl. Mix and add water to adjust the consistency. Divide the garlic butter chicken and potatoes into four equal servings in meal prep containers, adding a little pot for the homemade aioli if you decide to make it. Store in the fridge for up to five days. When ready to eat, reheat the chicken and potatoes in the air fryer for 3-4 minutes until warm. Pour the aioli over the top and enjoy!

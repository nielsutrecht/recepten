# Cheesy BBQ chicken pasta

Servings: 4 | Calories: 583kcal | Protein: 53g | Carbs: 72g | Fat: 9g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients

- 650g diced chicken breast
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper
- 300g dry pasta (I used Casarecce) Save 1/4 pasta water
- 4 (60g) lean bacon rashers (Turkey, beef or pork)
- 1 medium red onion (diced)
- 3 garlic cloves (diced)
- 400g can chopped tomatoes
- 15g tomato paste
- 80g low sugar bbq sauce
- 50g grated mozzarella (use
- part skim if you can find it) chopped chives

## Instructions

1. Boil the pasta in salted water until al dente, then drain and set aside, saving about a quarter cup of the pasta water for later. While the pasta cooks, add the diced chicken breast to a bowl with the smoked paprika, salt, and pepper, and mix until evenly coated. Heat a pan over high heat and cook the diced lean bacon until golden and crispy. Remove from the pan and set aside. In the same pan, add the seasoned chicken and cook until about halfway done. Add the diced red onion and cook until softened, then stir in the garlic and let it cook until fragrant. Add the chopped tomatoes, tomato paste, and low-sugar BBQ sauce. Stir well and let the sauce simmer for a few minutes. Add the cooked pasta along with a splash of the reserved pasta water and mix until everything is well coated and glossy. Sprinkle over the grated mozzarella and the crispy bacon bits, then cover the pan with a lid for a minute or two to let the cheese melt. Finish with chopped chives for a bit of colour. Divide into four meal prep containers and store in the fridge for up to five days. To reheat, microwave covered with a damp paper towel for 3–4 minutes, stirring halfway, until hot. Enjoy!

# chocolate fudge brownie oats

Servings: 1 | Calories: 492kcal | Protein: 47g | Carbs: 54g | Fat: 10g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**Chocolate Filling**

- 45g rolled oats
- 180g 0% fat Greek yoghurt
- 65ml almond milk
- 25g chocolate whey protein (I use Protein Works chocolate silk)
- 1 tbsp cocoa powder
- 15g dark chocolate chips (brand: tesco)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract Pinch of salt
- 0 cal Sweetener (I used stevia)

**Toppings**

- 5g dark chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. In a mixing jug, combine the oats, Greek yogurt, milk, cocoa powder, chocolate protein powder. a tsp of sweetener, vanilla extract, chocolate chips and a pinch of salt. Mix until well combined. Pour the mixture into a mason jar or container and top with some extra chocolate chips. Seal the jar and refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight. Enjoy!

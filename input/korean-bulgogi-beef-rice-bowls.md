# Korean bulgogi beef rice bowls

Servings: 4 | Calories: 495kcal | Protein: 47g | Carbs: 44g | Fat: 15g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Bulgogi beef marinade**

- 80ml soy sauce
- 45ml rice wine vinegar
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp ginger paste
- 1 tbsp garlic paste
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 30g brown stevia or 0 calorie

**sweetener (brand: purevia)**


**Korean beef**

- 700g 5% ground beef (brand:

**Tesco)**

- 250g uncooked white rice chopped green onions

**sliced red chilli peppers**


**asian cucumber salad**

- 1 whole cucumber (peel into
- strips with a vegetable peeler or mandolin)
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp rice wine vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

**other ingredients**

- 200g white rice (uncooked) Kimchi sesame seeds
- chopped green onions (green parts only)

## Instructions

1. Cook 200g of white rice according to the package instructions and then set it aside. For the bulgogi beef marinade, in a jug, combine soy sauce, rice wine vinegar, sesame oil, ginger paste, garlic paste, black pepper, and either brown stevia or your choice of 0 calorie sweetener. Stir everything to combine and then set aside. In a separate bowl, add the lean ground beef along with roughly half of the marinade and the white parts of the chopped green onions. Mix well to ensure the beef is fully coated, then cover and let it marinate for at least 30 minutes to 2 hours. Meanwhile, make the Asian cucumber salad by peeling a cucumber into thin long strips with either a vegetable peeler or a mandolin. Add these strips to a bowl along with soy sauce, rice vinegar, and sesame seeds. Mix everything to combine. Once the beef has marinated, add it to a pan set over high heat and coated with cooking spray. Flatten the beef out completely with a spatula, allowing it to sear and develop a crust for about 6-8 minutes. Once seared, break it up, add the rest of the marinade and a squirt of sriracha, and mix once more to combine everything. To assemble your bowls, divide the bulgogi beef, rice, and salad into 4 equal servings, optionally adding a side of kimchi. Then, garnish with sesame seeds and the green parts of the green onions. Store the assembled bowls in the fridge for up to 5 days. When you're ready to eat, optionally remove the salad and kimchi (though it's good either way), add the contents of the prep to a pan over medium heat for 3–4 minutes until heated through. Or, reheat it in the microwave for 3–4 minutes, leaving the lid slightly cracked open. Enjoy!

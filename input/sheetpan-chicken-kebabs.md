# SHEETPAN CHICKEN KEBAbs

Servings: 4 | Calories: 329kcal | Protein: 50g | Carbs: ?g | Fat: 11g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**CHICKEN kebab mix**

- 1kg lean ground chicken (5% fat or less)
- 1 medium brown onion
- 1 red bell pepper
- 4 garlic cloves Chopped coriander
- 2 tsp paprika
- 2 tsp cumin
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp pepper
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1/2 tsp tumeric
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Finely dice the onion, bell pepper, and garlic cloves (you can also use a food process
- to speed this up), then squeeze out as much excess moisture as possible using your
- hands or a clean towel/cloth. Add them to a large bowl with the lean ground
- chicken, chopped coriander, paprika, cumin, salt, pepper, chilli powder, turmeric,

## Instructions

1. and a tablespoon of olive oil. Mix well until the colour changes and the mixture becomes uniform. Grease a large sheet pan and spread the chicken mixture across it in an even layer roughly ½ and inch in thickness. Use two fingers to press horizontal lines through the surface to form the classic kebab texture, then use a spatula to vertically divide the mixture into eight long kebab strips. Bake at 190°C (375°F) for 20–25 minutes, draining any excess juices at the halfway point, until golden brown and fully cooked. Serve the kebabs however you like, they go well with rice and veggies or in a kebab wrap (see recipe below). If storing, wrap the kebabs in foil and refrigerate for up to 5 days. To reheat, pan-fry or air fry until hot, Enjoy!

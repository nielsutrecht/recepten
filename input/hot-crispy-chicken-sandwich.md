# HOT CRISPY CHICKEN SANDWICH

Servings: 3 | Calories: 635kcal | Protein: 43g | Carbs: 75g | Fat: 17g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**Dry Ingredients**

- 450g (3x150g) chicken thighs
- 125g all purpose flour (not all is used)
- 60g potato starch (not all is used)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp salt

**wet coating**

- 1/2 cup of dry mix
- 1/2 cup sparkling water

**GARLIC SAUCE**

- 80g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 60g light mayo (brand: Hellman’s lighter than light
- 1/2 lemon juice
- 1 tsp garlic powder salt to taste
- 1 tsp honey tsp garlic powder

**OTHER INGREDIENTS**

- 3 Aldi brioche burger buns
- 3 light cheese singles (brand:

**Tesco) shredded lettuce**


## Instructions

1. Start by preparing your dry coating station. In a large bowl, combine all-purpose flour, potato starch, garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, black pepper, and salt. In a separate bowl, take a ½ cup of the dry mix and combine with ½ cup sparkling water to form a smooth wet batter. To create extra texture, spoon a little of the wet batter into the dry mix and stir – this helps form small clumps that make the coating extra crispy. Now coat each chicken thigh by first dredging it in the seasoned dry mix, then dipping it into the wet batter, and finally returning it to the dry mix. Press to coat well, then shake off any excess flour and set aside on a wire rack. Spray lightly with cooking oil and air-fry at 190ºC/375ºF for 12–15 minutes or bake at 200ºC/400ºF for 20–25 minutes until golden and crispy. While the chicken is cooking, shred some lettuce and mix up the garlic sauce in a bowl until smooth. In another bowl, combine honey and sriracha to create your spicy glaze. Once the chicken is cooked and crispy, brush it with the sriracha-honey mix for a hot and sticky finish. To assemble the sandwich, optionally toast your burger buns, then spread on garlic sauce, add shredded lettuce, layer on your crispy chicken thigh, a slice of light cheese, another drizzle of garlic sauce, and finish with the top bun. Serve hot and enjoy!

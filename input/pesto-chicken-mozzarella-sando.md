# Pesto Chicken & mozzarella sando

Servings: 1 | Calories: 346kcal | Protein: 34g | Carbs: 21g | Fat: 14g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients

- 1 high protein sandwich thin (brand:
- Aldi) - sub with a bagel thins or pita bread
- 50g cooked chicken slices (poached or deli)
- 40g light mozzarella ball (brand: Aldi 50% less fat) tbsp spinach pesto
- ½ cup spinach
- 30g mushrooms (optional) cooking spray
- Notes One of my OG cutting
- recipes! used to make this one almost every day..

## Instructions

1. Start by preparing your chicken. If you're using fresh chicken, poach it in boiling water until fully cooked (10-15 minutes), then slice it. For deli chicken, it's ready as is. If you're including mushrooms, slice them thinly. In a pan over medium heat, add a little cooking spray. Sauté the mushrooms until they're soft and slightly browned, about 3-4 minutes, throw in your spinach halfway to wilt it down. Once done, set them aside. Take your high-protein sandwich thin (or substitute with bagel thin or pita bread) and spread 1/2 a tablespoon of spinach pesto on each side. Layer the cooked chicken slices over the pesto. Next, add the light mozzarella in small slices, then top with the sautéed spinach and mushrooms . Lightly spray the outside with cooking spray and return it to the pan. Toast each side for about 2 minutes, or until the bread is crispy and the mozzarella has started to melt. Serve hot and enjoy!

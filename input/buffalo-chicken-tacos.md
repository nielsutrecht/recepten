# Buffalo chicken tacos

Servings: 8 | Calories: 252kcal | Protein: 22g | Carbs: 13g | Fat: 12g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**Buffalo Chicken**

- 650g boneless skinless chicken thighs (fat trimmed off)
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp salt
- 100ml buffalo sauce

**Green SAUCE**

- 190g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 45g light mayo

**Handful coriander/cilantro**

- 1 tsp honey Juice of half a lemon
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

**Fresh salad**

- 8 mini tortillas (brand: mission)
- 160g low fat cheese (I used 50% less fat) (20g per taco)
- paprika (rub a small amount on each
- side of the tacos with cooking spray) cooking spray salt to taste

**ingredients per taco**

- 1 mini tortilla (use low carb for less calories)
- 1/4 kebab mix kebab salad garlic sauce

## Instructions

1. Slice the chicken thighs into small bite-sized pieces, trimming off any excess fat. Add the onion powder, garlic powder, paprika, black pepper, and salt, then mix well until the chicken is fully coated. Heat a pan over medium-high with a little cooking spray and add the chicken. Let it sear and crisp up, then cook through until golden brown. Pour in the buffalo sauce and stir until everything is coated and glossy. For the green sauce, add the Greek yoghurt, light mayo, coriander, honey, lemon juice, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper to a blender cup. Blend until smooth and creamy, then set aside. Lay out your mini tortillas and add some buffalo chicken to each one, followed by 20g of low-fat cheese. Fold them over gently, spray lightly with cooking spray, and rub a little paprika onto both sides for extra colour and flavour. Toast the tacos in a pan on medium heat for a couple of minutes on each side, flipping often to prevent burning. They should turn golden, crispy, and slightly gooey on the inside from the melted cheese. Repeat until all 8 tacos are done. Serve hot with a drizzle of the green sauce — they’re flavour-packed, high-protein, and unreal for only 252 calories each. If storing, let them cool completely, wrap in foil, and keep in the fridge for up to a week. To reheat, remove the foil and air-fry or pan-toast for a couple of minutes until hot and crispy again.

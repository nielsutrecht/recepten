# cookies n' cream oats

Servings: 1 | Calories: 483kcal | Protein: 44g | Carbs: 53g | Fat: 12g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**cookies & cream filling**

- 40g rolled oats
- 180g 0% fat Greek yoghurt
- 20g light cream cheese (brand:

**philadelphia lightest)**

- 65ml almond milk
- 1 Oreo crushed up
- 25g vanilla protein powder or cookies n’ cream (I use Protein Works cookies & cream) pinch of salt

**topping**

- 1 Oreo crushed up

## Instructions

1. In a mixing jug, combine the oats, Greek yogurt, cream cheese, milk and vanilla protein powder. Stir until all the ingredients are well mixed. Next, crush one Oreo cookie and fold it into the mixture. In a mason jar or container, add the oat mixture, followed by another crushed Oreo on top. Seal the jar and refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight. Enjoy!

# kinder Chocolate Protein shake

Servings: 1 | Calories: 509kcal | Protein: 53g | Carbs: 46g | Fat: 12g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 250ml fat-free milk
- 200g vanilla skyr (brand: Arla) - sub with vanilla greek yoghurt
- 15g (half a scoop) chocolate protein powder (I use Protein Works) Tsp cocoa powder
- 1 kinder chocolate bar (21g)
- Into a blender add 0% milk, vanilla Skyr, 15g (about half a
- scoop) of chocolate protein powder, teaspoon cocoa
- powder and finally one Kinder chocolate bar.

## Instructions

1. Blend everything until smooth. Ensure the Kinder chocolate is well incorporated and the shake has a consistent texture. Then pour into a glass and enjoy your protein-rich Kinder Bueno shake. Perfect for satisfying your chocolate cravings.

# Sesame popcorn chicken

Servings: 4 | Calories: 535kcal | Protein: 52g | Carbs: 68g | Fat: 6g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Popcorn CHICKEN**

- 800g chicken breast
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp white pepper
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 egg white
- 80g cornflour/cornstarch (add a little
- more if needed as not all will be used) cooking spray

**Crispy potatoes**

- 900g raw potatoes
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp cayenne
- 2 tsp salt cooking spray

**sesame glaze**

- 60ml soy sauce
- 150ml chicken stock
- 15ml rice vinegar
- 2 garlic cloves (minced)
- 20ml sesame oil
- 45g honey
- 30g brown sugar alternative
- 1/2 tsp ginger
- 2 tsp cornstarch

**garnishes**

- chopped green onions (green parts) Sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Dice the potatoes into cubes, peeling first if preferred. Lightly spray with cooking spray, then season with 2 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder, 1 tsp black pepper, ½ tsp cayenne, and 2 tsp salt. Toss well to coat. Air fry at 180°C for 20–25 minutes, shaking halfway, or oven bake at 200°C for 25–30 minutes until golden and crispy. Cut the chicken breast into bite-sized cubes and place in a bowl with 2 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder, 1 tsp white pepper, 1 tsp paprika, and 1 egg white. Mix well to coat evenly. Add the chicken to a large container with 80g cornflour/cornstarch, seal with a lid, and shake well to coat — work in batches if needed to avoid clumping. Lightly spray the coated chicken with cooking oil, then air fry at 200°C for 10–12 minutes or bake at 200°C for 18–20 minutes until golden and cooked through. Meanwhile, make the sesame glaze by adding 60ml soy sauce, 150ml chicken stock, 15ml rice vinegar, 2 minced garlic cloves, 20ml sesame oil, 45g honey, 30g brown sugar alternative, ½ tsp ginger, and 2 tsp cornstarch to a jug. Mix until smooth, then pour into a pan over medium heat. Simmer, stirring continuously, until thickened. Add the crispy popcorn chicken to the pan and toss to coat in the glaze. Garnish with sesame seeds. Divide the potatoes and glazed chicken into 4 meal prep containers, topping with chopped green onions for colour. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, add the contents to a pan and warm through, or microwave for 3 minutes until piping hot.

# Korean shrimp fried rice

Servings: 4 | Calories: 582kcal | Protein: 53g | Carbs: 68g | Fat: 10g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Shrimp marinade**

- 800g peeled deveined shrimp
- 1 tsp ginger
- 2 tsp white pepper
- 2 tsp salt
- 20ml oyster sauce
- 30g brown stevia
- 2 tsp sesame oil

**Other Ingredients**

- 3 eggs
- 30g green onion whites
- 20g diced garlic
- 30g gochujang
- 150g frozen mixed veg
- 600g cold cooked white rice
- 30ml soy sauce
- 20g oyster sauce black sesame seeds (garnish) Chopped green onion (garnish)
- Optionally, slice the shrimp into smaller pieces for a more even bite, then

## Instructions

1. marinate with oyster sauce, soy sauce, sesame oil, ginger, white pepper, salt, and brown stevia. Let sit for 15–30 minutes to absorb the flavours. Heat a wok over medium-high heat and spray lightly with cooking oil. Add the marinated shrimp and sauté for 1–2 minutes until just cooked through — be careful not to overcook. Remove and set aside. In the same pan, add a touch more cooking spray and pour in the beaten eggs. Scramble until fluffy and just set, then remove from the pan and set aside. Next, add the white parts of the spring onions, frozen mixed veg, and gochujang paste to the pan. Sauté for a minute until fragrant, then add the cold white rice, followed by the cooked shrimp and scrambled eggs. Pour in the soy sauce and oyster sauce, then mix thoroughly until everything is evenly coated and the rice is hot and fluffy. Garnish with black sesame seeds and chopped green onion tops. Divide into 4 equal portions in meal prep containers. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, remove the lid and place a damp paper towel over the top. Microwave for 3–4 minutes until hot. Serve immediately and enjoy!

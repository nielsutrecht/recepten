# mango & blueberry oats

Servings: 1 | Calories: 473kcal | Protein: 39g | Carbs: 60g | Fat: 6g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**mango filling**

- 40g rolled oats
- 180g 0% fat Greek yoghurt
- 100g fresh mango
- 70ml almond milk
- 25g vanilla protein powder (I use Protein Works)
- 1 tbsp powdered 0 cal sweetener

**Blueberry Jam**

- 25g blueberries
- 15g Honey

**topping**

- 20g fresh mango (diced)

## Instructions

1. To a blender add 80g of fresh mango and almond milk. blend until smooth. In a mixing jug, combine oats, Greek yogurt, sweetener, vanilla extract, vanilla protein powder and the mango puree you just made. Mix until well incorporated. For the quick blueberry jam, heat honey and 25g fresh blueberries in a medium-high heat pan. Stir until it thickens into a jam, then let it cool. In a mason jar or container, layer half of the mango-oat mixture, followed by half of the blueberry jam. Repeat the layers, topping it off with the remaining 20g of diced fresh mango. Seal the container and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, or overnight for best results. Enjoy from the fridge when you’re ready!

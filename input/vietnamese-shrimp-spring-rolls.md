# Vietnamese SHRIMP spring rolls

Servings: 6 | Calories: 81kcal | Protein: 10g | Carbs: 8g | Fat: 1g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**SPring rolls**

- 18 large cooked shrimp (approximately 216g)
- 100g cucumber (cut into matchsticks)
- 100g carrot (cut into matchsticks)

**butter head lettuce leaves**

- 6 large rice papers (22cm)
- 150g konjac noodles

**(optional for added volume)**


**Low cal peanut sauce**

- 30g powdered peanut butter (I used PBfit)
- 10g oyster sauce
- 5ml rice vinegar
- 7g honey
- 35ml water
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder

## Notes

Feel free to swap out the shrimp for chicken or any other protein you wish!

## Instructions

1. Start by prepping the vegetables. Slice the cucumber and carrot into thin matchsticks and set aside. If you're using konjac noodles for added volume, rinse and drain them well. To roll the spring rolls, fill a shallow bowl with water and briefly dip one sheet of rice paper into it for just a couple of seconds – long enough to soften but not so long that it gets too sticky. Lay the rice paper flat on a damp surface or chopping board (this is important to prevent sticking). Start by layering on some lettuce leaves, followed by the cucumber, carrot, and a small handful of konjac noodles. Fold in the sides of the rice paper, then begin to roll it up tightly just until the vegetables are enclosed. Now place the cooked shrimp in a line across the roll, then finish rolling to seal – this way the shrimp show through the translucent paper for a clean look. For the peanut sauce, simply add all ingredients to a bowl and stir until smooth and creamy. Adjust the water or seasoning to your taste. These are best eaten fresh, but if prepping ahead, wrap each roll individually in cling film and store in the fridge. When you're ready to eat, place the rolls under a damp paper towel for 5 minutes to help bring back that soft, fresh texture.

# Crispy potato balls

Servings: 1 | Calories: 375kcal | Protein: 8g | Carbs: 81g | Fat: 2g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 400g raw potato (dice and rinse under water)
- 15g cornstarch / potato starch
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1/2 tsp parsley
- 1/2 tsp salt Cooking spray

## Instructions

1. Dice the potatoes into small cubes and rinse them under cold water to remove excess starch. Transfer them to a microwave-safe bowl, cover tightly with plastic wrap, and pierce a few holes on top with a fork. Microwave on high for 9-12 minutes (depending on your microwave) until the potatoes are fully soft and mashable. Mash the potatoes until smooth, then let them cool completely - this step is important so the mixture firms up and shapes properly. Once cooled, add the cornstarch, garlic powder, smoked paprika, pepper, parsley, and salt. Mix everything together until it forms a dough-like consistency. Now scoop out small portions and roll them into bite-sized potato balls. Place the balls onto an air fryer rack or lined tray, spray lightly with cooking spray, and air fry at 200°C (390°F) for 12–15 minutes, or until golden and crispy on the outside. Enjoy while hot and crunchy!

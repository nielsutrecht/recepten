# Crispy Chicken mac n’ cheese

Servings: 4 | Calories: 572kcal | Protein: 56g | Carbs: 67g | Fat: 8g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Chicken seasoning**

- 600g chicken breast (4x150g breasts)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 2 tsp paprika
- ½ tsp cayenne
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper

**Wet station**

- 2 eggs (beaten)
- 1 tbsp pickle juice (from jar of pickles)

**Crispy coating**

- Crushed cornflakes (50g for tracking add more to the plate, not all will be used)
- same seasonings used on chicken cooking spray

**Mac ‘n’ cheese**

- 550g Macaroni (275g uncooked) - boil
- in salted water till al dente (save 100ml pasta water)
- 280ml 0% fat milk (or low fat)
- 100g light cream cheese (brand:

**Philadelphia lightest)**

- 100ml pasta water (reserved from macaroni)
- 30g low fat cheddar (brand: Tesco 50% less fat)
- 3 light cheese slices (brand: Tesco)
- 70g grated mozzarella

**Hot honey**

- 50g sriracha
- 40g honey

## Instructions

1. add the chicken breast to a bowl and season with garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, cayenne, salt, and black pepper. Mix well till fully coated. Now set up a wet station and a coating station. In the wet station, whisk the eggs with pickle juice. In the coating station, crush the cornflakes and mix with the same seasonings used on the chicken. Dip each piece into the wet station, then coat thoroughly in the seasoned cornflakes, pressing down firmly to get an even coating. Lightly spray with cooking oil then oven bake at 200ºC/400ºF for 20-25 minutes or air fry at 190ºC/375ºF for 15-18 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden brown and fully cooked. Once done, chop up into crispy chunks. Next, Cook the macaroni in salted water until al dente, reserving 100ml of pasta water before draining. Set the pasta aside. Then in a pan on low heat add the milk, cream cheese, cheddar, cheese slices, and mozzarella. Stir until melted and smooth. Add the cooked pasta and reserved pasta water, mixing until well until creamy and cheesy. For the Hot Honey (Optional): In a small pan, gently heat the sriracha and honey until warm and combined. Divide the mac ‘n’ cheese with crispy chicken chunks into 4 equal meal prep containers. Store the hot honey separately if using. To reheat: Add the contents of the prep to a pan over medium heat. Let the mac ‘n’ cheese and crispy chicken heat through for about 3-4 minutes. For extra crispiness, reheat the chicken separately in the air fryer for 2-3 minutes on high. Drizzle with a portion of hot honey and enjoy!

# mac N’ CHeese & porcorn chicken

Servings: 4 | Calories: 576kcal | Protein: 60g | Carbs: 66g | Fat: 8g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Popcorn chicken**

- 700g chicken breast diced into bite-size pieces
- 100g plain flour (use 40g for
- tracking - not all is used)
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1 tsp dried ginger
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard cooking spray

**High protein mac n cheese**

- 600g cooked macaroni (300g uncooked )
- 200ml low fat milk
- 80ml reserved pasta water from macaroni
- 35g light cream cheese (brand:

**Philadelphia lightest)**

- 40g (2 slices) reduced fat burger cheese (brand: Tesco)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 35g grated mozzarella
- Begin by boiling 300g of macaroni until al dente. Remember to reserve about 80ml of the

**pasta water before draining.**


## Instructions

1. Dice 700g of chicken breast into bite-sized pieces. In a bowl, combine 1 beaten egg and some Dijon mustard. Add the chicken pieces to this mixture and marinate briefly. In a separate bowl, mix plain flour with a teaspoon each of paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, oregano, ginger, black pepper, and salt. Coat the marinated chicken pieces in this seasoned flour mixture. Spray the coated chicken pieces with cooking oil. For air frying, cook them at 200°C for 10-12 minutes. If baking, place them in an oven preheated to 190°C and bake for 14-16 minutes, flipping halfway through. While the chicken is cooking, start the mac n' cheese. In a pan on low heat, combine 200ml of low-fat milk, the reserved pasta water, 35g of light cream cheese, and 40g of reduced-fat burger cheese. Stir constantly on medium-low heat until the cheese is fully melted and combined. Add a teaspoon each of garlic powder, onion powder, and Dijon mustard to the pan, along with 35g of grated mozzarella. Stir until everything is well combined. Finally, add the cooked macaroni to the cheese sauce and mix until the pasta is thoroughly coated and cheesy. Divide the mac n' cheese and popcorn chicken into four equal servings in meal prep boxes. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days and when you’re ready to eat, add the contents of the prep to a pan over medium heat for 3–4 minutes until heated through. Or, reheat it in the microwave for 3–4 minutes, leaving the lid slightly cracked open. The popcorn chicken can optionally be reheated for a couple of minutes in the oven or air fryer for added crispiness. Enjoy!

# Sweet and SOur CHicken

Servings: 4 | Calories: 519kcal | Protein: 51g | Carbs: 71g | Fat: 2g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**chicken BItes**

- 800g chicken breast (diced into 1” cubes)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp white pepper
- 1 tsp salt
- 60g corn flour
- 60g plain (all-purpose) flour Cooking spray

**Sweet and sour sauce**

- 1 medium red bell pepper (sliced)
- 1 medium white onion
- 35ml soy sauce
- 75ml rice vinegar
- 75g low sugar ketchup
- 60ml pineapple juice (I used

**juice from canned pineapple)**

- 90g brown stevia (sub with any sugar alternative)
- 1 tsp cornstarch + tbsp water
- 75g diced pineapple handful chopped green onions

**other ingredients**

- 220g uncooked white rice

## Instructions

1. Dice the chicken breast into 1-inch cubes, then add the garlic powder, white pepper, and salt. Mix well until the chicken is evenly seasoned. In a large container, combine the cornflour and plain flour, shake to mix, then add the chicken in batches. Seal the lid and shake until every piece is fully coated. Transfer the coated chicken onto a rack, spray lightly with cooking spray, and air fry at 200°C (400°F) for 12–15 minutes, or oven bake at 200°C (400°F) for 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and crispy. While the chicken cooks, sauté the sliced bell pepper and onion in a pan until soft and slightly caramelised. Remove and set aside. In the same pan, add the soy sauce, rice vinegar, low-sugar ketchup, pineapple juice, and brown stevia. Stir well, then add a slurry made from 1 tsp cornstarch mixed with 1 tbsp water. Keep stirring over medium heat until the sauce thickens and becomes glossy. Add the vegetables back into the sauce, then stir in the crispy chicken and toss everything together until fully coated. Add the diced pineapple for that classic sweet-and-sour flavour and warm it through for a minute. Serve hot with cooked rice (included in calories), top with chopped green onions, and divide the rest into meal prep containers. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, place in a microwave-safe bowl and heat for 3–4 minutes until hot, stirring halfway. You can also reheat in a pan if you prefer. Enjoy!

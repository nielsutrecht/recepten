# banana & cinnamon milkshake

Servings: 1 | Calories: 332kcal | Protein: 38g | Carbs: 42g | Fat: 1g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 200ml 0% (fat free) milk
- 100g 0% skyr (sub with
- 0% Greek yoghurt)
- 100g frozen banana
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- 25g vanilla protein (I use Protein Works)
- 1 tsp sweetener of choice (I used stevia:
- brand: Pure via) light squirty cream (optional topping) chocolate sprinkles (optional topping) I added a drop of natural yellow food
- colouring for the classic yellow banana look (completely optiional)
- To your blender cup, add fat-free milk, fat-free skyr or
- Greek yogurt, frozen banana, ground cinnamon,

**vanilla protein powder, and sweetener.**


## Instructions

1. Blend everything until completely smooth. Pour into a glass, optionally top with light squirty cream and chocolate sprinkles and enjoy your refreshing banana milkshake!

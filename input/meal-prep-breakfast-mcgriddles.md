# Meal prep breakfast mcgriddles

Servings: 6 | Calories: 395kcal | Protein: 38g | Carbs: 34g | Fat: 13g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**Sausage patties**

- 550g lean pork or beef mince (5% or less)
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp sage cooking spray

**pancake buns**

- 225g plain flour
- 300ml low fat milk (I used 1%)
- 1 egg
- 2 tsp baking powder pinch of salt to taste

**egg squares**

- 6 large eggs (beaten)
- optional salt and pepper to taste

**other ingredients**

- 6 light cheese singles (brand:
- Tesco) maple syrup to brush pancakes

## Instructions

1. Start by seasoning your lean ground pork or beef with garlic powder, onion powder, salt, pepper, paprika, sage, and one egg. Mix everything well with your hands and shape the mixture into six equal patties. For the fluffy pancake buns, in a large mixing bowl, add plain flour, baking powder, and a pinch of salt, mixing to combine. Then, add low-fat milk and one egg, mixing again to form your pancake batter. Heat a pan on high and add some cooking spray. Cook the sausage patties for 2-3 minutes on each side until they develop a crispy sear. Set the patties aside. Turn the pan to medium heat and wipe it out. Add some more cooking spray followed by heatproof ring molds. Pour in just enough pancake batter to cover the bottom of each mold. Once you see bubbles on top, remove the molds and flip the pancakes over, cooking for a further 1-2 minutes. Set the pancakes aside. Crack six eggs into a bowl and beat them. Using a ladle or large spoon, gently pour just enough egg into the pan to cover the base, tilting the pan to get even coverage. Let the egg cook and set, then gently fold in the sides to form a square. To assemble your McGriddles, place a low-fat cheese single on a pancake bun, followed by a sausage patty, the folded egg square, and top with another pancake bun. Optionally, brush the buns with maple syrup! Wrap them in foil and store in the fridge for up to five days. To reheat from the fridge, unwrap from the foil and microwave for 2-3 minutes until hot. To reheat from frozen, remove the foil, wrap in a damp paper towel, and microwave for 4-5 minutes, flipping halfway. Enjoy!

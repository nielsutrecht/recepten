# Cheeseburger roll ups

Servings: 9 | Calories: 360kcal | Protein: 38g | Carbs: 31g | Fat: 11g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**Cheeseburger beef**

- 800g lean ground beef
- 1 medium diced onion
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp cayenne (or less if you don’t like spice)
- 2 tsp salt
- 200ml beef stock
- 120g light cream cheese
- 90g low sugar ketchup
- 10g mustard
- 120g diced pickles

## Instructions

1. Add the lean ground beef to a pan sprayed lightly with cooking spray and let it sear undisturbed for a minute to develop flavour. Break it up with a spatula, then add the diced onion, smoked paprika, garlic powder, black pepper, cayenne, and salt. Mix well and cook until the onions soften. Pour in the beef stock, then add the light cream cheese, low-sugar ketchup, mustard, and diced pickles. Stir everything together until the mixture becomes creamy, saucy, and fully combined. Remove from the heat and let it thicken slightly as it cools.

2. Ingredients per roll 1 protein tortilla (brand mission) 1/9 cheeseburger beef mix (weigh total and divide by 9) 20g low fat grated cheese (I used 50% less fat) parsley flakes (garnish) cooking spray

3. Warm your protein tortillas to make them easier to fold. Add one-ninth of the cheeseburger mixture to the centre of each tortilla, sprinkle over 20g of low-fat grated cheese, then fold in the sides and roll tightly into a compact wrap. Place all 9 roll-ups onto a lined baking tray, spray lightly with cooking spray, and sprinkle with parsley flakes. Bake at 180°C (355°F) for 10–12 minutes, or until the outsides are golden and lightly crisp. Allow them to cool before storing. Wrap each one in foil and freeze for long-term storage. To reheat, unwrap the foil, wrap the roll-up in a paper towel, run it briefly under water, squeeze out the excess, and microwave for 5–6 minutes until heated through. Finish by toasting in a pan or air frying for a couple of minutes to get the outside crispy again. A perfect high-protein freezer snack — 38g of protein per roll and insanely satisfying.

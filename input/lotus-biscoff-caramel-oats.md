# lotus Biscoff caramel oats

Servings: 1 | Calories: 476kcal | Protein: 43g | Carbs: 59g | Fat: 12g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**biscoff filling**

- 40g rolled oats
- 160g 0% fat Greek yoghurt
- 65ml unsweetened almond milk
- 15g melted Biscoff spread (I used the crunchy version!)
- 15g brown stevia (brand:
- Pure Via) - optional but recommended!
- 25g caramel protein powder - sub with vanilla ( I use Protein Works Caramel Bandit)

**topping**

- 1 Lotus Biscoff biscuit

## Instructions

1. In a mixing jug, combine oats, Greek yogurt, almond milk, melted Biscoff spread, 15g of brown stevia, and caramel protein powder (or vanilla as a substitute). Stir until everything is well mixed. In a mason jar or container, add half of the oat mixture, followed by a layer of half a crumbled lotus biscuit. Repeat these layers once more. Seal the jar and place it in the fridge for at least 2 hours, or overnight. Enjoy!

# protein packed crispy waffles

Servings: 1 | Calories: 495kcal | Protein: 41g | Carbs: 60g | Fat: 9g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**crispy Waffles**

- 70g self-raising flour or plain + 1/2 tsp baking powder)
- 30g vanilla protein powder (I use Protein Works French vanilla)
- ½ tsp baking soda
- 1 ½ tbsp 0 cal sweetener
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 egg white
- 80ml 2% milk

**Toppings**

- 20g sliced strawberries
- 0 cal maple syrup (brand: Protein Works)

## Instructions

1. Preheat your waffle iron, then to a mixing bowl add the oat flour, vanilla protein powder, sweetener and baking soda. Mix with a whisk till combined. In a separate bowl add the milk, egg white and vanilla extract. Stir to combine everything, then slow add the wet ingredients to the dry ingredients and whisk gently into a batter. Let the batter sit for 5-10 minutes to thicken up. Scoop 1/3 of the batter onto your waffle iron and cook for around 2-3 minutes or until the top is a crispy golden brown. Repeat for the other waffles, then top with sliced strawberries and 0 cal maple syrup. Enjoy! BONUS: you can prep up a batch of these and freeze them to enjoy at your convenience! Simply reheat them in the toaster on the defrost setting until crispy and warmed through.

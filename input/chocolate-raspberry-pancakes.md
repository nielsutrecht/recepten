# CHocolate raspberry Pancakes

Servings: 1 | Calories: 409kcal | Protein: 36g | Carbs: 48g | Fat: 10g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**Pancakes**

- 40g self raising flour
- 1 egg
- 1 tbsp cocoa powder
- 25g chocolate protein

**powder (I use Protein Works)**

- 1 tsp baking powder
- 80ml 2% milk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 40g raspberries (mix ins)

**Toppings**

- 20g raspberries Low cal chocolate sauce (brand: sweet freedom)
- Notes BONUS: prep up a batch of these and freeze them

**to enjoy whenever! Simply**


**reheat them in the toaster**

- on the defrost setting until soft and warmed through.

## Instructions

1. Add to a mixing bowl the oat flour, egg, chocolate whey protein, baking powder, Cocoa powder and milk and mix till smooth, Then add the raspberries and gently fold then into the mix. Let the batter sit for 5-10 minutes to thicken up. Heat a pan on high heat with some low cal cooking spray and once hot turn the heat to medium low and carefully scoop in an ice cream scoop amount of batter into the pan Let the pancakes cook for 1-2 mins or until you see bubbles popping on the top, then flip it over and cook for a further minute (no longer). Repeat till the mixture is finished (keep the pan on medium-low to cook the rest). Top with low cal chocolate syrup, and some frozen raspberries microwaved and mashed up into a jam. Enjoy!

# Chipotle cheesy beef rolls

Servings: 10 | Calories: 365kcal | Protein: 37g | Carbs: 30g | Fat: 11g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**taco beef**

- 800g lean ground beef (5% or less)
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp cayenne
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp oregano
- 2 tsp sea salt
- 1 tsp black pepper cooking spray

**Chipotle sauce**

- 300g 0% fat cottage cheese
- 100g 0% fat Greek yoghurt
- 100g light mayo (brand Heinz lighter than light)
- 20g chipotle paste
- 1/2-1 lime juice
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp garlic powder Salt to taste

**other ingredients**

- 100g grated mozzarella
- 100g reduced fat grated cheddar
- (I used Tesco 50% less fat)

**Chopped cilantro (or parsley)**

- 10 protein tortillas (brand:

**mission)**


## Instructions

1. Start by preparing the chipotle sauce. Add all the sauce ingredients to a blender and blitz until smooth. Set aside in a bowl or bottle. Heat a pan over high heat with cooking spray and add the lean ground beef. Cook until browned with a crispy sear, then stir in the taco seasoning and cook through until fully coated and fragrant. Transfer the taco beef to a large bowl and mix in the blended chipotle sauce, grated cheddar and mozzarella, and freshly chopped coriander (or parsley). Stir until everything is evenly combined. Warm your tortillas slightly to make them easier to roll. Spoon 1/10th of the beef mixture onto each tortilla, fold in the sides, and roll tightly to form your beef rolls. Place all 10 rolls on a lined baking tray. Spray the tops lightly with cooking oil and sprinkle with chopped parsley. Bake at 200ºC/400ºF for 8–10 minutes or airfry at 180ºC/355ºF for 6–8 minutes until golden and crisp. Allow to cool completely before wrapping each roll in foil. Store in the freezer for up to 2 months. To reheat, unwrap the foil, wrap the frozen roll in a damp paper towel, and squeeze out any excess water. Microwave for 4–5 minutes, then toast in an air fryer or hot pan for 1–2 minutes until crispy again. Serve warm with your favourite dipping sauce and enjoy!

# tropical Digestion smoothie

Servings: 1 | Calories: 161kcal | Protein: 3g | Carbs: 35g | Fat: 1g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 300ml water
- 80g frozen mango
- 80g frozen pineapple handful of spinach juice of half a lemon
- ½ tsp chopped ginger
- 3-4 ice cubes
- Notes Packed full of antioxidants and fibre to prevent bloating and keep things moving!
- To your blender cup add water, frozen mango and
- pineapple, a handful of spinach (you can’t taste it but
- it adds a boost of iron and fiber to your smoothie),

## Instructions

1. then squeeze in the juice of half a lemon, add 1/2 a tsp of ginger (aids digestion) and finally toss in 3-4 ice cubes for that refreshing chill. Blend everything until completely smooth. Then pour it into a glass, optionally add a little lemon wedge for aesthetics. Then enjoy your refreshing and rejuvenating digestion smoothie!

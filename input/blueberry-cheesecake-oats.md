# Blueberry cheesecake oats

Servings: 1 | Calories: 455kcal | Protein: 39g | Carbs: 61g | Fat: 8g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**cheesecake filling**

- 120g 0% fat Greek yoghurt
- 45g light cream cheese

**(brand: Philadelphia lightest)**

- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 45ml almond milk
- 20g vanilla protein powder (I use Protein Works)
- 25g frozen blueberries

**cinnamon crumble**

- 40g oats (partially blended)
- 5g maple syrup
- 5g melted light butter
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- Notes All the overnight oats recipes are meal prep friendly! Just multiply
- ingredients to adjust how many servings you want.
- To make the cinnamon crumble, mix partially blended oats,
- a teaspoon of cinnamon, maple syrup, and melted butter in
- a bowl. Stir until it forms a golden crumble.

## Instructions

1. For the cheesecake filling, combine Greek yogurt, cream cheese, maple syrup, almond milk, vanilla protein powder, and frozen blueberries. Stir until well blended. In a mason jar or container, add a layer of half the cinnamon crumble, followed by half of the cheesecake filling. Repeat the layers once more. Seal the container and refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight. Enjoy from the fridge when you're ready!

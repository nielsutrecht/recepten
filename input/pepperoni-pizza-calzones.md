# Pepperoni Pizza Calzones

Servings: 6 | Calories: 365kcal | Protein: 31g | Carbs: 41g | Fat: 8g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients

- 400g plain (all purpose) flour
- 500g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 1 tsp salt
- 150g low fat grated cheese (I used 50% less fat)
- 180g turkey or beef pepperoni
- 120g Pizza sauce parsley cooking spray

## Instructions

1. Add the plain flour, Greek yoghurt, and salt to a bowl and mix until clumps form. Bring the clumps together with your hands and knead briefly until you have a smooth dough ball. Transfer to a lightly floured surface and divide the dough into 6 equal pieces. Gently flatten each piece into a round. Spoon some pizza sauce into the centre, then add low-fat grated cheese and turkey or beef pepperoni. Fold the dough over to enclose the filling and pinch the edges to seal, forming a calzone. Cut a couple of small slits in the top to allow steam to escape. Place the calzones on a lined baking tray, spray lightly with cooking spray, and sprinkle with parsley. Bake at 180°C (355°F) fan / 200°C (400°F) no fan for 15–18 minutes, or air fry at 180°C (355°F) for 10–12 minutes, until golden, puffed, and cooked through. Let them cool slightly before serving. To store, wrap in foil and refrigerate for up to 5 days. To reheat, remove the foil, cover with a damp paper towel, and microwave for a couple of minutes until hot and the cheese has melted. You can finish them in the air fryer for a minute if you want the outside extra crispy.

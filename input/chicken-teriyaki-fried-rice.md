# Chicken teriyaki fried rice

Servings: 4 | Calories: 543kcal | Protein: 56g | Carbs: 65g | Fat: 7g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Crispy Chicken**

- 800g chicken breast
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp cornstarch

**Teriyaki Glaze**

- 60ml reduced salt soy sauce
- 10ml of mirin (or rice wine vinegar)
- 1 tsp ginger paste
- 1 tsp garlic paste
- 3 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds
- 1 tsp cornflour mixed with a 1 tbsp water

**Fluffy Fried Rice**

- 600g day-old cooked white rice
- (cook night before or chill for at

**least an hour before using)**

- 20ml soy sauce

**oyster sauce (or fish sauce)**

- white parts of the spring onions
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (forgot
- to mention in vid - enhances flavour)

**Garnishes**

- green parts of spring onion sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Cook 600g white rice the night before and chill overnight. If short on time, refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before using. Dice the chicken breast into cubes and season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and cornstarch. Ensure it's well-coated. Then prepare the teriyaki glaze by mixing soy sauce, mirin (or rice wine vinegar), ginger paste, garlic paste, honey, sesame seeds, and a cornflour-water slurry. Set aside. In a pan over high heat, cook frozen mixed vegetables until soft. Push the veggies to the side, add 3 beaten eggs to the pan, scramble until fluffy, and then mix with the vegetables. Add the chilled rice, soy sauce, oyster (or fish sauce), and the white parts of spring onions. Break up and mix the rice thoroughly, then drizzle with toasted sesame oil. Divide the fried rice among four meal prep containers. Next cook the seasoned chicken in the same pan until golden and crispy. Set aside. Then pour in the teriyaki sauce, simmering until thickened. Return the chicken to the pan, coating it in the teriyaki sauce. Top the fried rice with equal portions of the teriyaki chicken, garnish with spring onion greens and sesame seeds. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days and when you’re ready to eat, add the contents of the prep to a pan over medium heat for 3–4 minutes until heated through. Or, reheat it in the microwave for 3–4 minutes, leaving the lid slightly cracked open. Enjoy!

# Korean Chicken Bao Buns

Servings: 8 | Calories: 250kcal | Protein: 20g | Carbs: 41g | Fat: 1g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**Crispy chicken**

- 500g chicken breast (diced into 1/2” cubes)
- 1 egg
- 100g cornflakes (60g for
- tracking - not all is used)

**korean glaze Korean Glaze**

- 60ml soy sauce
- 60ml water
- 2 tbsp gochujang
- 2 tbsp ketchup
- 20g sweetener of choice (I used granulated stevia)
- 1 tbsp honey chilli flakes

**bao buns**

- 300g plain flour
- 1 heaped tsp instant yeast
- 1 heaped tsp baking powder
- 165ml fat free milk (at room temp - helps with rise)

## Instructions

1. Dice the chicken breast into small ½-inch cubes and add to a bowl with the egg. Mix until fully coated. In a separate bowl, crush the cornflakes slightly, then add the chicken and toss until evenly coated. Lay the chicken on a rack, spray lightly with cooking spray, and air fry at 200°C (400°F) for 10–12 minutes, or oven bake at 200°C (400°F) for 15–18 minutes, until golden and crispy. While the chicken cooks, make the Korean glaze. Add the soy sauce, water, gochujang, ketchup, sweetener, and honey to a small pan. Stir over low heat until smooth and slightly thickened, then add chilli flakes to taste. Toss the crispy chicken through the glaze until fully coated and glossy. For the bao buns, add the flour, instant yeast, and baking powder to a bowl. Slowly pour in the milk and mix until clumps form, then knead for around 5 minutes until you have a smooth, elastic dough. Divide the dough into 8 equal pieces, roll each into a ball, then flatten into an oval shape. Lightly brush with oil, fold in half, and place onto baking paper. Steam the buns in a bamboo steamer (or covered pan with a steamer insert) for 10 minutes, until puffed and fluffy. To assemble, open each bao bun and fill with sliced cucumber and a generous spoonful of the sticky Korean chicken. Serve immediately while hot and steamy. These are best enjoyed fresh, but you can store cooled buns in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, microwave for a couple of minutes until warm and fluffy again.

# 10 minute Mozzarella bites

Servings: 8 | Calories: 68kcal | Protein: 6g | Carbs: 4g | Fat: 3g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**cheese bites**

- 8 babybel lights (Mozzarella bites)
- 60g cornflakes
- 1 egg
- ½ tsp paprika
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp black pepper

**Other ingredients Hot sauce (dip)**

- Begin by unwrapping your Babybel lights. These snack cheeses are ideal
- for this recipe due to their size and consistency.

## Instructions

1. Add the cornflakes to a blender and blend into crumbs, but not too finely. To cornflake crumbs, add paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, and Italian herbs. Mix these well to create your flavorful coating. Now, in another bowl whisk 1 egg, take each Babybel and dip it first in the egg, ensuring it’s fully coated, and then in the cornflake crumb mixture till completely coated. Once all your cheese bites are coated, you can either place them on a lined baking tray and bake at 180°C (360°F) for 4-5 minutes, or place them on some parchment paper and air fry at 180°C (360°F) for about 34 minutes until crispy and slightly golden. If using an air fryer be sure to check frequently as air fryers can vary. Once cooked, transfer your cheese bites to a plate and enjoy while hot!

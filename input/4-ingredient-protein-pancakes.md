# 4-Ingredient Protein pancakes

Servings: 2 | Calories: 394kcal | Protein: 31g | Carbs: 55g | Fat: 5g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**Pancake mix**

- 125g self-raising flour (sub with plain flour + tsp baking powder)
- 250ml 1% milk
- 1 egg
- 45g vanilla protein powder (I use Protein Works) Pinch of salt

**optional toppings Fresh blueberries**

- 0 cal maple syrup (brand: Protein Works)

## Instructions

1. To a mixing bowl, add self-raising flour, low-fat milk, one egg, vanilla protein powder, and a pinch of salt (to enhance flavors). Whisk just until the flour has combined into a thick batter - Don’t over mix. Set your stove to high heat and coat a pan with lowcalorie cooking spray. Allow the pan to heat up sufficiently, then pour 1/4 cup (60ml) of the batter onto the pan. Immediately reduce the heat to medium-low and cook for 2-3 minutes, or until tiny bubbles appear on the surface. Then, flip the pancake and cook for an additional 2-3 minutes. Repeat with the remaining batter. Serve the pancakes and add your favorite toppings. I used sugar-free maple syrup and fresh blueberries. Enjoy!

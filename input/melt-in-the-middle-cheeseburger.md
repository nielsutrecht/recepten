# Melt-in-the-Middle cheeseBurger

Servings: 1 | Calories: 434kcal | Protein: 42g | Carbs: 29g | Fat: 16g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**melt in the middle patty**

- 150g 5% ground beef Salt & Pepper
- 1 light Babybel cheese (substitute with 20g of low-fat grated cheese)
- 10g light butter

**burger ingredients**

- 1 brioche burger bun (brand: aldi)
- 3 red onion ring slices lettuce sliced pickles

**sliced red onion (ringed)**

- 1 tsp sriracha mayo

## Notes

Never has a melt-in-themiddle burger before? trust me it hits different...

## Instructions

1. Divide 150g of beef mince into two 75g portions, flattening each into 1cm-high patties. Generously season both with salt and pepper. Place a Babybel cheese in the center of one patty and cover with the other, sealing the edges to form a single, cheesefilled patty. In a hot, buttered pan over medium-high heat, sear the patty for 2-3 minutes on each side until crispy. Toast the brioche bun halves in the pan until lightly golden. Assemble the burger with a layer of sriracha mayo on the bun base, followed by lettuce, pickles, the hot cheese-filled patty, red onion rings, and a final dollop of sriracha mayo on the top bun. Serve hot and enjoy your high protein melt in the middle cheeseburger!

# Large Serving chicken Fried Rice

Servings: 1 | Calories: 665kcal | Protein: 67g | Carbs: 49g | Fat: 22g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Chicken marinade**

- 250g chicken boneless skinless thighs (diced) white pepper garlic powder

**tbsp low sodium soy sauce**


**fried rice**

- 120g cold cooked white rice
- 100g frozen mixed veg green onion (white parts only)
- 2 eggs (beaten)
- 1.5 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (optional)

**pinch of MSG (optional for**


**that classic takeout flavour)**


**Garnishes**

- spring onion (green parts only) sesame seeds chilli flakes (optional)

## Instructions

1. Start by slicing the green onions and separating the white and green parts. Dice the chicken thighs into bite-sized cubes, then season with garlic powder, white pepper, and low-sodium soy sauce. Mix well and set aside to marinate while you prep the rest. Heat a wok or large pan over medium-high heat and spray lightly with cooking oil. Add the marinated chicken in a single layer and cook undisturbed for 1–2 minutes to sear, then stir and continue cooking until browned and cooked through. Remove and set aside. Lower the heat to medium, add a bit more cooking spray, and pour in the beaten eggs. Scramble until fluffy and just set, then remove and set aside. Add frozen mixed vegetables and the white parts of the spring onion to the same pan. Sauté for 2–3 minutes until softened, then return the eggs and chicken to the pan along with the cold white rice. Add soy sauce, fish sauce, and optionally a small pinch of MSG for that classic takeout flavour. Stir everything together and fry for a few minutes until the rice is hot, fluffy, and golden brown. For extra depth, finish with a small drizzle of toasted sesame oil. Transfer to a large bowl and garnish with the sliced green parts of the spring onions, sesame seeds, and chilli flakes. Serve hot and enjoy!

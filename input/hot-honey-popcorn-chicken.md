# Hot honey popcorn chicken

Servings: 4 | Calories: 492kcal | Protein: 52g | Carbs: 68g | Fat: 3g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**POPCORN CHICKEN**

- 800g chicken breast
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- ½ tsp cayenne
- 1 tsp salt & pepper
- 1 egg
- 60g cornflour/cornstarch cooking spray

**Crispy potatoes**

- 900g diced potatoes (optionally peeled)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1 tsp paprika
- ½ tsp cayenne
- 1 tsp salt & pepper cooking spray

**hot honey**

- 60g sriracha
- 60g honey

**garnishes finely chopped chives (optional)**


## Instructions

1. Start by peeling (optional) and dicing the potatoes, then season with garlic powder, oregano, paprika, cayenne, salt, pepper, and a bit of cooking spray. Mix well to fully coat, then oven bake at 200ºC (400ºF) for 30-35 minutes, or air fry at 190ºC (375ºF) for 25-30 minutes. For the popcorn chicken, dice the chicken breast into bite-size pieces and add to a bowl with garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, cayenne, salt, black pepper, and an egg. Mix until fully coated, then transfer the chicken to a large container with cornflour and shake well until completely coated. For baking, arrange the chicken on a tray, spray with oil, and bake at 220°C for 15-18 minutes, flipping halfway. For air frying, spray the chicken and cook at 200°C for 10-12 minutes, shaking halfway. Meanwhile, make the hot honey by adding equal parts sriracha and honey to a pan. Bring to a bubble, then reduce the heat and stir until thickened. Toss the cooked popcorn chicken in the hot honey. Divide the chicken and crispy potatoes into four equal portions in meal prep containers, optionally garnishing with chopped chives. Refrigerate for up to five days. To reheat, microwave for 3-4 minutes or heat in a pan. Enjoy!

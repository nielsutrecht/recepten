# Protein iced coffee

Servings: 1 | Calories: 284kcal | Protein: 45g | Carbs: 19g | Fat: 2g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 300ml skimmed milk
- 1 ½ tsp instant coffee
- 20ml boiling water
- 30-45g chocolate whey protein sweetener to taste ice cubes

## Instructions

1. In a small glass add the instant coffee and a small amount of boiling water, stir until fully dissolved. Then In a shaker cup, add a handful of ice cubes, pour in the milk, followed by the dissolved coffee, sweetener (if using), and chocolate whey protein. Shake until smooth and frothy. Pour into a glass and enjoy cold. You can sub in almond milk for fewer calories, or experiment with different protein flavours like vanilla or caramel.

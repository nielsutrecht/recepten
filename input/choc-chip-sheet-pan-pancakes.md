# Choc-Chip Sheet pan Pancakes

Servings: 12 | Calories: 158kcal | Protein: 14g | Carbs: 16g | Fat: 4g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**Wet ingredients**

- 4 eggs
- 720g 0% greek yoghurt
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 60g 0 cal sweetener of choice
- (I use Purevia granulated sugar alternative)

**Dry ingredients**

- 180g plain flour (you can also
- use oat flour for more fibre!)
- 30g vanilla whey protein (I use Protein Works)
- 30g vanilla casein protein (I
- use Protein Works) - sub with whey
- 2 tbsp baking powder pinch of salt (optional)

**TOppings Chocolate chips**


## Notes

you can add any toppings you like! different fruits, or even bacon!

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 180ºC fan-assisted or 200ºC conventional. Line a 30x40cm baking tray with parchment paper and set aside. In one bowl, combine the dry ingredients: plain flour, vanilla whey protein, casein protein (or more whey if substituting), baking powder, and a pinch of salt. In a separate bowl, whisk together the wet ingredients: eggs, 0% Greek yoghurt, vanilla extract, and sweetener. Once mixed, pour the wet ingredients into the dry and gently fold everything together until just combined. Avoid over-mixing to keep the pancakes light and fluffy. Pour the batter into your lined tray and spread it out evenly by gently shaking the tray or smoothing with a spatula. Top with your choice of toppings – I used Choc-chips, but feel free to switch it up with different berries, banana slices or even bacon. Bake for 20–25 minutes, or until golden and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Once baked, lift the pancake slab out using the edges of the baking paper and place on a raised cooling rack to cool slightly. Slice into 12 squares and serve warm, or store in a paper towel-lined meal prep container to keep them fresh. To reheat, microwave for around 2 minutes until soft and bouncy. Enjoy as it or with syrup, yoghurt, or fruit on top.

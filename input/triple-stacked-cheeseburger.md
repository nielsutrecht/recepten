# triple stacked cheeseburger

Servings: 1 | Calories: 540kcal | Protein: 64g | Carbs: 32g | Fat: 17g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**Beef patties**

- 260g 5% ground beef Salt & Pepper Cooking spray

**burger ingredients**

- 1 brioche burger bun (brand: aldi)
- 1.5 light cheesy singles (½ on each patty)
- 10g shredded lettuce sliced pickles

**sliced red onion (ringed)**

- 1 tsp reduced sugar ketchup (brand: heinz)

## Instructions

1. Divide 260g of 5% fat ground beef into three equal balls. Flatten each ball into thin patties using a burger press or your hands. Season both sides with salt and pepper. In a hot pan coated with low-calorie cooking spray over, sear the patties for 2-3 minutes on each side until crispy brown. Place half a cheese single on each patty, allowing it to melt.

2. Notes Why not pair this with some homemade french fries and a diet coke. Who TF needs burger king!

3. To assemble, spread ketchup on a toasted brioche bun, then layer with shredded lettuce, the three cheese-covered patties, sliced pickles, and red onions. Add the top bun and enjoy your low-calorie triple cheeseburger!

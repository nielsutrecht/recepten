# easy chicken egg fried rice

Servings: 4 | Calories: 505kcal | Protein: 50g | Carbs: 56g | Fat: 9g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Crispy chicken**

- 600g (3) chicken breast (diced)
- 20ml light soy sauce
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp black pepper

**Egg fried rice**

- 600g Day-old Refrigerated cooked basmati rice
- 200g frozen mixed veg

**(carrots, sweetcorn, peas)**

- 4 eggs
- 20ml light soy sauce
- 30g oyster sauce (brand tesco)
- 25g chopped green

**onions (white parts only)**

- 10ml sesame oil
- Garnish chopped spring onions (green parts only sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Start by dicing the chicken breast into small cubes. Season them with garlic powder, black pepper, and light soy sauce. Mix well to ensure each piece is evenly coated. Heat a pan over medium-high heat and spray it with cooking spray. Cook the chicken in two batches to ensure it gets crispy and golden. Once cooked, set the chicken aside. In the same pan, over medium heat, add the white parts of the spring onions along with the frozen mixed vegetables. Sauté these until they are soft. Push the vegetables to one side of the pan, then lower the heat. Crack and beat four eggs, pouring them into the pan. Gently scramble the eggs until they are light and fluffy. Next, add the day-old rice into the pan. Pour in the light soy sauce, oyster sauce, and drizzle in the sesame oil. Mix everything thoroughly, ensuring the rice is well coated with the sauces. Add the crispy chicken back into the pan, stirring it through the rice to combine everything evenly. Garnish the dish with chopped green parts of spring onions and a sprinkle of sesame seeds for an added crunch and flavor. To store, divide the chicken egg fried rice into four meal prep containers. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days and when you’re ready to eat, add the contents of the prep to a pan over medium heat for 3–4 minutes until heated through. Or, reheat it in the microwave for 3–4 minutes, leaving the lid slightly cracked open. Enjoy!

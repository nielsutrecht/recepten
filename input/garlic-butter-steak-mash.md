# Garlic butter steak & Mash

Servings: 4 | Calories: 528kcal | Protein: 56g | Carbs: 40g | Fat: 16g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Garlic butter steak bites**

- 900g lean diced steak (I used

**flank steak with fat trimmed)**

- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 2 tsp black pepper
- 2 tsp salt
- 15-20g light butter
- 15g minced garlic fresh chopped parsley cooking spray

**Creamy mash potatoes**

- 900g raw potatoes
- 80g light cream cheese (Philadelphia lightest)
- 120ml 0% fat milk
- 15g light butter
- 30g roasted garlic paste (you
- can make your own or use store bought)
- 1 tsp onion powder
- salt and pepper (taste and adjust)

**Garnishes**


**chopped parsley (garnish)**


**melted butter (optional garnish)**

- Peel the potatoes, then chop into chunks then add to boiling salted water.

## Instructions

1. Boil until fork-tender, then drain and return to the pot. Mash them Up till completely smooth with no lumps. Then add cream cheese, milk, butter, roasted garlic paste, and onion powder. Mix until smooth and creamy, adjusting salt and pepper to taste. Now for the garlic butter steak bites: Dice your lean steak into cubes then season with garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, salt, and black pepper. Heat a pan over high heat and spray with cooking oil. Add the steak in a single layer and sear for 1-2 minutes per side until browned and cooked to just under your desired doneness. Reduce the heat to medium-low then add the butter and minced garlic, stirring to coat the steak in the garlic butter. Cook for another minute until fragrant. Stir in fresh chopped parsley, then remove from heat. lastly, divide the creamy mash with the garlic butter steak bites into 4 equal meal prep containers. Garnish with more chopped parsley and optionally some melted butter. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days and when you’re ready to eat, add the contents of the prep to a pan over medium heat for 3–4 minutes until heated through. Or, reheat it in the microwave for 3–4 minutes, leaving the lid slightly cracked open. Enjoy!

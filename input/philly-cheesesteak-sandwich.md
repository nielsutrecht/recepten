# Philly cheesesteak sandwich

Servings: 1 | Calories: 615kcal | Protein: 52g | Carbs: 45g | Fat: 23g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients

- 150g lean steak sliced against the grain
- 40g sliced brown onion
- 40g sliced mixed peppers
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- 100ml boiling water
- 1/2 cube (5g) beef bouillon
- 36g sliced mozzarella or provolone
- 90g crusty baguette salt and pepper to taste cooking spray

## Instructions

1. place the steak in the freezer for around 30 minutes to firm it up — this makes slicing much easier. Slice the steak thinly against the grain, season lightly with salt and pepper, and mix well. In a jug, add the boiling water, crumble in the beef bouillon, and stir in the Worcestershire sauce until fully dissolved. Set aside. Heat a pan over high heat with some cooking spray and add the steak strips in a single layer. Cook quickly until tender and lightly seared, then remove from the pan and set aside. In the same pan, add the sliced onion and mixed peppers and cook until softened and slightly caramelised. Pour in the prepared bouillon mixture and continue cooking, stirring, until all the liquid has reduced and the pan is nearly dry. Lower the heat, add the steak back into the pan, and mix everything together. Lay the mozzarella or provolone directly over the steak mixture, cover with a lid, and let the cheese melt into everything. Slice open the crusty baguette and lightly toast if desired. Once the cheese has fully melted, load the baguette with the cheesy steak filling, slice, and serve immediately while hot and melty.

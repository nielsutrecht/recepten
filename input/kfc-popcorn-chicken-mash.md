# KFC popcorn chicken & mash

Servings: 4 | Calories: 510kcal | Protein: 52g | Carbs: 56g | Fat: 8g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**chicken seasoning**

- 800g chicken breast
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper

**KFC Breading**

- 100g plain flour
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1/2 tsp ginger powder
- 1 tsp salt & pepper
- 1 tsp baking powder cooking oil spray

**Creamy Mash Potato**

- 900g raw potatoes
- 120ml light crème fraiche
- 60ml fat free milk
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter (optional) chopped chives (garnish)

**low calorie Homemade gravy**

- 2 tbsp cornstarch + tbsp water (slurry)
- 400ml beef stock (1 stock cube + 400ml water)
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce

## Instructions

1. Cut the chicken breast into small, bite-sized pieces and add to a large bowl. Then crack in an egg along with a tsp each of garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, salt, and black pepper. Mix everything up with your hands till the chicken is fully coated. In a separate wide bowl or plate, combine the flour, garlic powder, paprika, oregano, ginger powder, salt, pepper, and baking powder to create the breading mix. Remove the chicken pieces from the egg mixture, letting any excess drip off, then coat them evenly in the breading. Place the breaded chicken on a lined baking sheet and lightly spray with cooking oil, ensuring no dry spots. Preheat the air fryer or oven to 200ºC/400ºF, then cook for 10-12 minutes in the airfryer or 15-20 minutes in the oven, shaking or flipping halfway through until golden and crispy. Peel and chop the potatoes into chunks, then boil in salted water until fork soft. Drain and mash until smooth with no lumps. Next add in crème fraiche, milk, and butter (if using), and stir well until creamy. Adjust seasoning to taste. For the gravy, dissolve the stock cube in hot water to make beef stock. In a pan, bring the stock to a gentle simmer, then stir in Worcestershire sauce. Mix cornstarch with water to form a slurry, then gradually add it to the stock, stirring continuously until the gravy thickens. Adjust with salt and pepper as needed. Now just divide the crispy popcorn chicken, creamy mash and homemade gravy into 4 equal meal prep containers, garnish with chopped chives. Then store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, add the contents of the prep to a pan over medium flame. Let the Mash and popcorn chicken heat through (around 3-4 minutes), you can also add the popcorn chicken to the airfryer instead for 2-3 minutes on high to get them crispier again. Enjoy!

# shredded chicken taquitos

Servings: 12 | Calories: 180kcal | Protein: 21g | Carbs: 6g | Fat: 8g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**Shredded taco chicken**

- 600g chicken breast
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp pepper
- 1 tsp salt Cooking spray
- 100g light cream cheese
- 1 packet Taco seasoning (I use
- store-bought but you can make your own)
- 60-80ml water

**chipotle dip**

- 120g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 60g light mayo
- 1 tsp chipotle paste
- 1 tsp honey
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp salt

**water to adjust consistency**


**other ingredients**

- 240g grated mozzarella (20g per Taquito)
- 12 low carb mini tortillas (brand mission low carb - 28g)

**cooking spray (add a generous**

- amount to the pan for toasting)

## Instructions

1. Spray the chicken breasts with cooking oil and season with smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, pepper, and salt. Heat a pan over medium with some cooking spray and cook the chicken for 10–12 minutes, flipping frequently until golden brown and juicy. Make sure the internal temperature reaches 75°C (165°F). Shred the chicken with two forks, then add it back into the pan with the taco seasoning, light cream cheese, and a splash of water (60–80ml). Stir everything together until the mixture turns creamy and fully coated. Remove from the heat and let it cool slightly so it’s easier to roll. Lay out the mini tortillas and add a spoonful of shredded taco chicken to each one, followed by 20g of mozzarella. Fold the bottom over the filling and roll tightly into a taquito shape. Add a generous amount of cooking spray to a pan and toast the taquitos over medium heat, rolling often, until all sides are golden brown and crispy. Repeat until all 12 are done. For the creamy chipotle dip (optional but unreal), mix the Greek yoghurt, light mayo, chipotle paste, honey, onion powder, garlic powder, salt, and a splash of water until smooth. Once the taquitos cool, wrap them individually in foil and freeze for long-term storage. To reheat, unwrap, microwave for 2 minutes to soften, then air fry or pan-toast for a couple of minutes until crispy again. Each one packs around 20g of protein for just 180 calories - perfect for meal prep or a highprotein snack through the week.

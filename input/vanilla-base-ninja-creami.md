# vanilla base ninja Creami

Servings: 1 | Calories: 159kcal | Protein: 14g | Carbs: 22g | Fat: ?g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 420ml fat free (skimmed) milk
- 45g granulated stevia
- 1g salt
- 5g vanilla extract
- 1g xanthan
- Notes You will need a Ninja Creami for this recipe!

## Instructions

1. Add all the ingredients - skimmed milk, granualted stevia andsalt and vanilla extract to a blender except the xanthan gum, blend for 30-45 seconds then add the xanthan gum and blend for another 10-20 seconds. Pour into a pint, cover, and freeze for 24 hours. Take the frozen pint, run the sides and bottom under hot water for 1 minute, Load it into the Ninja Creami, and hit the ice cream button then scrape down the sides if necessary, add the pint back to the Ninja Creami and run the re-spin setting. The texture should be soft and creamy, grab a spoon and enjoy!

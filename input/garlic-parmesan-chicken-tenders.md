# GARLIC parmesan chicken tenders

Servings: 6 | Calories: 149kcal | Protein: 17g | Carbs: 6g | Fat: 6g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**chicken**

- 400g chicken breast (2 large chicken breast)
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp garlic powder

**flour station**

- 100g plain flour (not tracked
- as amount used is only about
- 10-20g between all tenders)
- To start, slice your chicken breasts into three large strips each, then

## Instructions

1. season with garlic powder, salt, and pepper, mixing well until fully coated.

2. egg station 2 beaten eggs (Roughly 1 egg used for tracking)

3. breading station 100g panko breadcrumbs (40g used for tracking)

4. Garlic butter 30g unsalted butter 4 cloves diced garlic

5. garnishes 30g grated parmesan or pecorino Chopped parsley

6. Set up your coating stations: in the first bowl, add plain flour; in the second bowl, add 2 beaten eggs; and in the final bowl, add panko breadcrumbs. Dunk each strip first in the plain flour to help the egg stick, then into the beaten egg, and lastly into the panko breadcrumbs, pressing down firmly to ensure an even coating. Once the tenders are fully coated, set them on a rack, lightly spray with cooking oil, and bake at 190°C (390°F) for 20-25 minutes, or preferably air fry at 180°C (375°F) for 12-15 minutes, flipping halfway through. When they’re nearly done, melt some unsalted butter in a pan over low heat, add diced garlic and stir until fragrant. Carefully place the cooked crispy tenders in the pan and roll them around in the garlic butter until they're fully coated. Garnish with parmesan and fresh chopped parsley, serve, and enjoy!

# Beef & Broccoli Fried Rice

Servings: 4 | Calories: 658kcal | Protein: 52g | Carbs: 56g | Fat: 23g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**seasoned beef**

- 800g lean steak strips (I used lean flank)
- 30ml (2 tbsp) soy sauce
- 15ml (1 tbsp) Chinese cooking wine
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 15g cornstarch/cornflour
- 1 tsp baking soda/bi carb

**fried rice**

- 2 shallots diced
- 6 garlic cloves diced
- 300g broccoli florets (par-boil for
- 45-60 seconds before using)
- 650g day old cooked jasmine rice
- 30ml soy sauce
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 2 tsp sesame oil
- 15g sweetener
- 15g chilli crisp
- 1 tsp white pepper sesame seeds (garnish)

**crispy onions (optional garnish)**


**chilli flakes (optional garnish)**


## Instructions

1. Start by slicing the steak into thin strips. Add it to a bowl with the soy sauce, Chinese cooking wine, salt, black pepper, cornstarch, and baking soda. Mix well until the beef is evenly coated and let it marinate for around 20 minutes if you have time — this helps keep the beef tender and juicy. Heat a wok or large pan over high heat with some cooking spray. Add the beef in batches and cook quickly until golden brown and slightly crispy. Remove from the pan and set aside. Wipe the pan clean, then add more cooking spray and lower the heat slightly. Add the diced shallots and cook until softened, then add the garlic and stir until fragrant. Add the par-boiled broccoli florets and stir-fry for about 2 minutes. Add the day-old cooked rice, breaking it up as you go. Pour in the soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil, sweetener, chilli crisp, and white pepper. Stir-fry until everything is evenly mixed and heated through. Add the cooked beef back into the pan and toss everything together until well combined. Serve hot, garnished with sesame seeds, crispy onions, and chilli flakes if you like. Divide the rest into four meal prep containers and store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, microwave covered with a damp paper towel for 3–4 minutes, stir well, and enjoy.

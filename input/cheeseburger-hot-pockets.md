# Cheeseburger Hot pockets

Servings: 8 | Calories: 348kcal | Protein: 31g | Carbs: 43g | Fat: 5g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**Cheeseburger beef**

- 400g lean ground beef (5% fat or less)
- 1 medium diced white onion
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp cayenne (optional for added spice)
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper
- 100ml beef stock
- 80g light cream cheese
- 45g reduced sugar ketchup
- 60g diced pickles Cooking spray

**hot pocket dough**

- 400g plain flour
- 410g 0% Greek yoghurt optional salt to taste

**other ingredients**

- 200g low fat cheese (I used
- 50% less fat cheddar)

## Instructions

1. Start by preparing the dough. Add the flour, Greek yoghurt and optional salt to taste to a bowl and mix with a spoon until clumps form. Then knead with your hands until it comes together into a smooth dough ball. Divide the dough into 8 equal-sized pieces and set aside. Heat a pan over high heat and spray with cooking oil. Add the lean ground beef in chunks to help develop a crispy sear. Once browned, add the diced white onion and cook for another 2–3 minutes. Lower the heat to medium and stir in the beef stock, light cream cheese, ketchup, mustard, and diced pickles. Mix well until creamy and everything is combined, then remove from heat. Flatten out each dough ball into a circular shape. Spoon 1/8 of the cheeseburger filling into the centre of each, top with 25g of low-fat cheese, then fold in the sides and pinch the edges to seal. Gently press down to form your hot pocket shape. Spray a pan with cooking oil and cook the hot pockets over medium heat, lid on, for 3–4 minutes per side until golden and cooked through. Let cool completely before wrapping in foil. Store in the freezer for later. To reheat, remove the foil, wrap the pocket in a damp paper towel, and microwave for 4–5 minutes. Then air-fry or add to a hot pan for 1–2 minutes to bring back the crisp. Serve hot and enjoy!

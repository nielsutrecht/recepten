# CHeesy BBQ chicken Fries

Servings: 4 | Calories: 583kcal | Protein: 56g | Carbs: 56g | Fat: 13g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**BBQ Chicken**

- 130g cooked chicken breast
- (shred with two forks or your hands) I poached mine
- 1/4 red onion (diced)
- 15g tomato paste
- 30g bbq sauce (brand: Tesco 16 cals per tbsp)
- 100ml chicken stock
- 15g light cream cheese
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 12/ tsp cayenne
- 1/2 tsp salt Cooking spray

## Instructions

1. Start by slicing your potatoes into thin fries, then place them in a bowl of ice water for 10–15 minutes to draw out excess starch. Drain and pat them completely dry using a clean towel. Lightly spray with cooking oil and spread them out on a lined baking tray. Bake at 200ºC/400ºF for 25–30 minutes, flipping halfway, or air-fry at 180ºC/355ºF for 20–25 minutes until golden and crispy.

2. Homemade fries 250g raw potato cooking spray salt to taste (and any other seasoning you like)

3. Garnish 0g low fat grated cheese (I used 50% less fat + some mozzarella for the cheese pull) drizzle BBQ sauce topping chopped green onions (tops)

4. Meanwhile, prepare the BBQ chicken. Heat a pan over medium-high heat with a little cooking spray. Add the diced red onion and sauté until soft and fragrant. Stir in the tomato paste and BBQ sauce and toast for a minute to deepen the flavour. Then pour in the chicken stock, followed by the light cream cheese and all the seasonings. Stir until smooth and creamy. Add in the shredded chicken (or diced if you prefer), and mix well to coat everything in the sauce. Let it simmer gently for a couple of minutes until hot and creamy. To serve, season your fries with salt and pile them onto a plate. Top with the BBQ chicken mixture, sprinkle over your grated low-fat cheese, and place under the grill (or in the air fryer) for a minute or two until melted and gooey. Finish with a drizzle of BBQ sauce and chopped green onions. Serve hot and enjoy!

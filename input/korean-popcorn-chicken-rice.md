# korean popcorn chicken & rice

Servings: 4 | Calories: 495kcal | Protein: 50g | Carbs: 67g | Fat: 3g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**crispy POPCORN CHICKEN**

- 800g chicken breast (diced)
- 1 tbsp black pepper
- 20ml soy sauce
- 1 egg
- 40g cornflour/cornstarch

**KOREAN glaze**

- 60ml soy sauce
- 80ml water
- 2 tbsp low cal ketchup (brand:
- Heinz 50% less)
- 3 tbsp gochujang red pepper paste
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp honey
- 30g brown stevia (brand: pure via)

**sweet Sticky rice**

- 200g uncooked sticky rice (brand: Tesco)
- 3 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp sweetener salt to taste

**garnishes**


## Instructions

1. chopped green onions (green parts) sesame seeds normal and optionally toasted too for rice

2. Prepare the sticky rice by cooking 200g according to the package instructions. Then let it cool for 10 minutes, and mix in rice vinegar, sweetener, and a pinch of salt. Set aside. For the popcorn chicken, dice the chicken breast into bite size pieces, and add to a bowl with black pepper, soy sauce, and an egg. Mix to fully coat the chicken, then add it to a large container with cornflour and shake well till completely coated. For baking, arrange on a tray, spray with oil, and bake at 220°C for 1518 minutes, flipping halfway. For air frying, spray and cook at 200°C for 10-12 minutes, shaking halfway. Meanwhile make the korean glaze by adding soy sauce, water, low cal ketchup, gochujang, rice vinegar, honey, and brown stevia to pan, bring to a bubble then turn the heat down and stir until thickened. Toss the cooked chicken in the korean glaze. Then divide the popcorn chicken and seasoned sticky rice into four equal portions in meal prep containers, garnishing with green onions and sesame seeds. Refrigerate for up to 5 days. To reheat, microwave for 3-4 minutes or heat up in a pan, Enjoy!

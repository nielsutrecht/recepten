# Fajita chicken & golden rice

Servings: 4 | Calories: 395kcal | Protein: 41g | Carbs: 53g | Fat: 2g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**seasoned chicken**

- 600g chicken breast (I used 2 large breasts sliced in half)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper cooking spray

**Green sauce**

- 200g 0% greek yoghurt
- 60g light mayo (brand: heinz lighter than light)
- 3-4 garlic cloves
- 1 cup coriander/cilantro leaves
- ½ lemon juice
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- Begin by slicing your chicken breasts in half lengthways, then season both sides
- with garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, cumin, salt, and black pepper.

## Instructions

1. Heat a pan over medium-high and spray with cooking oil. Cook the chicken for 4-5 minutes on each side. While the chicken is cooking, prepare the green sauce. In a blender, combine 0% fat Greek yogurt, light mayonnaise, garlic cloves, coriander, salt, pepper, and a squeeze of lemon juice. Blend until smooth and creamy.

2. golden fajita rice 230g washed uncooked basmati rice 1 medium red onion (sliced) 1 red bell pepper (sliced) 1 green bell pepper (sliced) 1 tsp paprika 1 tsp cumin 1 tsp turmeric 1 tsp salt 400ml of water

3. optional garnish chopped coriander/cilantro (garnish)

4. For the golden fajita rice, use the same pan you cooked the chicken in. Add sliced onions and bell peppers, cooking on medium-high heat until soft. Then add your washed, uncooked rice along with cumin, turmeric, paprika, and salt. Toast the rice for 2-3 minutes before adding 400ml of water. Stir, bring to a light simmer, then cover with a lid and simmer on medium-low for another 10 minutes. Once the rice is cooked, remove the lid and fluff it with a spoon. Divide the rice into four equal portions along with the cooked chicken. Drizzle each meal generously with green sauce. Store the meals in prep boxes, refrigerated for up to 5 days. To reheat, microwave each portion partially covered for 3-4 minutes. Enjoy!

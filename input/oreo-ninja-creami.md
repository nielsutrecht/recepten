# Oreo ninja Creami

Servings: 1 | Calories: 384kcal | Protein: 46g | Carbs: 36g | Fat: 7g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 300ml fat free (skimmed) milk
- 90g 0% greek yoghurt
- 5g black cocoa (I got mine of Amazon)
- 15g sweetener of choice (I use pure via sugar alternative)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 30g vanilla protein powder (I use Protein Works) pinch of salt
- 2 oreo cookies (No added sugar ones)
- Notes You will need a Ninja Creami for this recipe!

## Instructions

1. Add all ingredients – skimmed milk, Greek yoghurt, black cocoa powder, sweetener, vanilla extract, vanilla protein powder, and a pinch of salt – directly into a Ninja Creami pint container. Use a milk frother to mix until smooth and fully combined. If you don’t have a frother, you can blend the mixture instead. Seal the pint and freeze for at least 12 hours until solid. Once frozen, run the sides and bottom of the pint under warm water for 20 seconds. Then add to the Ninja Creamy on the “Lite Ice Cream” setting. Check the texture and scrape down the sides if necessary then run it on the re-spin function until smooth and creamy. When ready, use a spoon to make a hole in the centre of the ice cream and drop in 1 crushed Oreo. Run the Mix-In cycle to combine. Top with another crushed Oreo if you’re feeling extra, and enjoy immediately!

2. DRinks.

# Garlic butter steak & potatoes

Servings: 4 | Calories: 522kcal | Protein: 55g | Carbs: 44g | Fat: 14g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Garlic butter steak bites**

- 800g lean diced steak (I used sirloin) salt & pepper
- 15g unsalted butter
- 2 cloves garlic (minced)
- 1 tsp parsley cooking spray

**Crispy potatoes**

- 900g potatoes (cubed)
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp parsley
- 1 tsp salt & pepper cooking spray

**peppercorn sauce**

- 5g unsalted butter
- 1 cup (260ml) beef stock
- 10g partially crushed & whole peppercorns (from pepper mill)
- 2 cloves chopped garlic
- 45g light cream cheese

**(brand: Philadelphia lightest)**

- 5g cornstarch mixed with a tsp of water

## Instructions

1. Dice the potatoes into small cubes and add them to a bowl. Season with paprika, onion powder, garlic powder, parsley, salt, and pepper. Spray them lightly with cooking oil and mix well. Air-fry at 200°C (400°F) for 2025 minutes, shaking halfway through for even crispiness. If using an oven, bake at 220°C (430°F) for 25-30 minutes. Meanwhile, dice your steak into cubes and season with salt and pepper. Cook in a pan over high heat using cooking spray until they're golden and crispy. Lower the heat, add 15g of unsalted butter, minced garlic, and a sprinkle of parsley. Stir for a couple more minutes until the butter has melted and the steak bites are well-coated. Set the steak bites aside. In the same pan, melt 5g of butter, then add beef stock, partially crushed peppercorns, and chopped garlic. Bring to a simmer. Once bubbling, reduce heat and add the light cream cheese, followed by the cornstarch mixed with water to thicken the sauce. Package in meal prep boxes, keeping the sauce separate (I used plastic shot glasses). Store in the fridge for up to 5 days. When you're ready to eat, reheat the potatoes in the air fryer for 2-3 minutes until crispy. Microwave the steak bites for 2-3 minutes until hot. Microwave the sauce for 15-20 seconds, then pour over the steak bites and enjoy!

# original glaze ANabolic donuts

Servings: 6 | Calories: 66kcal | Protein: 4g | Carbs: 8g | Fat: 2g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**Dry ingredients**

- 60g oat flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp baking soda pinch salt

**wet ingredients**

- 1 large egg
- 80g 0% greek yoghurt
- 30ml unsweetened almond milk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tbsp granulated sweetener (brand:

**pure via)**

- original glaze ⅓ cup powdered sweetener (0 cal icing sugar substitute)
- 1 ½ tbsp almond milk
- ¼ tsp vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 165°C (330°F) and lightly grease a donut pan. In a large bowl, combine the oat flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. In another bowl, whisk together the egg, almond milk, Greek yogurt, low-calorie sweetener, and vanilla extract until smooth. Gradually add the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients, stirring until just combined. Then transfer the batter into a large ziplock bag, cut a small hole in one corner, and pipe the batter gently into the donut pan filling each well evenly (about 1/2 to 2/3 full). Bake for 1012 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into a donut comes out clean. While the donuts are baking, make the low calorie glaze by mixing powdered sweetener, almond milk, and vanilla extract in a small bowl until smooth. When the donuts have finished baking cool for a few minutes, dip each one into the glaze, then set on a wire rack to allow the glaze to set. Enjoy your much lower calorie original glaze donuts!

# NO BAKE BROWNIE BITES

Servings: 9 | Calories: 42kcal | Protein: 4g | Carbs: 3g | Fat: 2g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**Ingredeints**

- 15g cocoa powder
- 20g light butter (Lurpak Light)
- 20g granulated stevia (canderel)
- 20g all purpose flour (heat treated - 30 seconds in the microwave to kill bacteria)
- 30ml 0% milk
- 30g chocolate protein powder - whey/casein blend (I use Protein Works code PANACEAPALM for a discount!)

**Coating powdered stevia or sugar**


## Instructions

1. In a mixing bowl, combine the cocoa powder, stevia, all-purpose flour, and a pinch of salt (if using). Stir to mix the dry ingredients thoroughly, then pour in the melted light butter and milk. Mix until mostly combined. Once smooth, add the protein powder — for best results, use a whey–casein blend. This helps create a soft, doughy texture that holds its shape. Stir until a thick, pliable dough forms. If the dough feels too soft, refrigerate it for 30 minutes to help it firm up — this step is optional, but makes shaping easier. Once ready, divide the dough into 8 equal portions and roll each one into a bite-sized ball. For a final touch, roll them in powdered stevia or powdered sugar to coat. These are perfect straight from the fridge as a quick high-protein snack. If you're feeling extra, dice the dough into chunks and mix it into your protein ice cream for a brownie-loaded treat.

# Creamy Garlic steak pasta

Servings: 4 | Calories: 525kcal | Protein: 54g | Carbs: 57g | Fat: 9g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**seasoned steak bites**

- 700g diced lean beef steaks
- (Topside or flank steak are the leanest cuts)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp parsley Cooking oil spray

**Creamy garlic sauce**

- 250ml low fat (1%) milk
- 150g light cream cheese

**(brand: Philadelphia lightest)**

- 2-3 garlic cloves
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp parsley

**other ingredients**

- 520g cooked penne pasta (250g uncooked weight)
- ½ medium white onion (diced)
- 2 garlic cloves (minced)
- 1 tsp paprika chopped chives (optional garnish)
- Begin by cooking 250g (uncooked) of penne pasta, as per the package instructions until al
- dente. Once cooked, drain and set it aside.

## Instructions

1. While the pasta is cooking, dice the lean beef steaks (topside or flank steak) into bite-sized cubes. then add to a bowl and season with garlic powder, onion powder, salt, black pepper, and parsley, ensuring even seasoning. Before cooking the steak bites prepare the creamy garlic sauce. In a blender, combine low-fat (1%) milk, light cream cheese, 2-3 garlic cloves, salt, black pepper, parsley and blend until smooth and creamy. Now heat a pan over medium heat, spray with cooking oil, and add the seasoned steak bites. Cook until the steak is browned and cooked to your preference, then remove from the pan and keep them warm. In the same pan, sauté diced onion and garlic until fragrant, then pour the blended sauce into the pan. Season with a tsp of paprika and stir over medium-low heat until the sauce is heated through and creamy. Lastly, add the cooked pasta to the sauce in the pan. Stir to ensure the pasta is well coated with the creamy garlic sauce. Divide up the creamy pasta and seasoned steak bites into 4 equal servings and store in meal prep boxes, optionally garnish with chopped chives for an extra touch. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days and when you’re ready to eat, add the contents of the prep to a pan over medium heat for 3–4 minutes until heated through. Or, reheat it in the microwave for 3–4 minutes, leaving the lid slightly cracked open. Mix till creamy and enjoy!

# chicken chow mein

Servings: 4 | Calories: 504kcal | Protein: 52g | Carbs: 67g | Fat: 4g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**chow mein chicken**

- 800g boneless skinless chicken

**breast (sliced into thin strips)**

- 20g soy sauce
- 1 tbsp hoisin sauce
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 tsp white pepper
- 1/2 tsp baking soda (this helps
- make the chicken juicy and tender!) Cooking spray

**chow mein sauce**

- 30g dark soy sauce
- 15g light soy sauce
- 15g oyster sauce
- 15g rice vinegar
- 10g sweetener
- 1 tsp cornstarch

## Instructions

1. Slice the chicken breast into thin strips and add them to a bowl with the soy sauce, hoisin sauce, minced garlic, white pepper, and baking soda. Mix well to coat, then let it marinate while you prep the rest - or marinate longer if you want even more flavour. For the chow mein sauce, mix the dark soy sauce, light soy sauce, oyster sauce, rice vinegar, sweetener, and cornstarch until completely smooth. Set aside.

2. other ingredients 600g straight to wok medium noodles 200g sliced white cabbage 150g sliced carrots (julienne style) Green onion white parts Green onion green parts (for garnish) Sesame seeds (garnish) Drizzle hoisin sauce (optional garnish)

3. Heat a pan or wok over high heat with some cooking spray and cook the chicken in batches, keeping it in a single layer so it gets a proper golden-brown sear. Once cooked through and slightly caramelised, set the chicken aside. In the same pan, add the sliced cabbage, julienned carrots, and the white parts of the green onions. Cook for a few minutes until softened. Add the noodles straight in, along with the cooked chicken, then pour over the chow mein sauce. Toss everything together until the noodles are coated and glossy. Finish with the chopped green onion tops and a sprinkle of sesame seeds. Add a drizzle of hoisin on top if you like things sweeter. Divide into four meal prep containers and store in the fridge for up to five days - you can keep the sauce separate if you prefer, but it reheats perfectly mixed too. To reheat, microwave covered with a damp paper towel for 3–4 minutes until hot, mixing halfway. Easy, high-protein, and tastes like proper Chinese takeaway but way leaner.

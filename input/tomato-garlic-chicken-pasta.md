# tomato & Garlic Chicken pasta

Servings: 4 | Calories: 529kcal | Protein: 58g | Carbs: 63g | Fat: 5g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Seasoned chicken**

- 600g raw chicken breast (diced)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper
- cooking oil spray (I use avocado)

**Creamy tomato garlic sauce**

- 20ml olive oil
- 8 garlic cloves diced
- 300g cherry tomatoes sliced generous pinch of salt
- 250ml chicken stock (brand: oxo)
- 1 tsp chilli flakes
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp black pepper add salt to taste
- 100g light cream cheese (brand:

**Philadelphia lightest)**

- 200ml fat free milk
- 30g tomato paste
- 1 tsp sugar
- 60ml pasta water (reserved from cooking pasta) fresh chopped parsley

**other ingredients**

- 280g penne pasta (uncooked

**weight) boiled in salted water**


**grated Parmesan (optional)**


## Instructions

1. Cook the penne in salted water until al dente, reserving 60ml of pasta water before draining. Set aside. Next, season the diced chicken with garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper. Heat a pan over medium-high heat, spray with cooking oil, and cook the chicken until golden brown and fully cooked. Remove from the pan and set aside. In the same pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the diced garlic and stir until fragrant. Add the sliced cherry tomatoes with a generous pinch of salt and cook until soft and jammy. Pour in the chicken stock, then stir in chili flakes, paprika, onion powder, black pepper, and salt to taste. Mix in the tomato paste, sugar/sweetener, cream cheese, and milk, stirring until smooth. Once the sauce is creamy, Return the cooked chicken and pasta, with the reserved pasta water. Mix well till the pasta and chicken is fully coated in the sauce. Finish with fresh chopped parsley, then divide the pasta evenly into four meal prep containers. Garnish with grated parmesan if using. To Reheat: Microwave partially covered for 3-4 minutes. Stir until creamy and enjoy!

# honey BBQ chicken & potatoes

Servings: 4 | Calories: 520kcal | Protein: 57g | Carbs: 48g | Fat: 10g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**BBQ CHICKEN**

- 1000g boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 2 tsp garlic powder tsp onion powder
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp black pepper
- 2 tsp salt
- 100g low sugar bbq sauce (I used Tesco brand) cooking oil spray

**Crispy potatoes**

- 1000g diced potatoes (optionally peeled)
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp pepper any other seasonings you wish cooking oil spray
- Notes this goes great with steamed corn on the cob pan seared tenderstem broccoli!
- Preheat the oven to 200ºC/400ºF.

## Instructions

1. Season the chicken breasts with salt, pepper, smoked paprika, garlic powder, and onion powder. Coat with 50g of BBQ sauce, then place on a lined baking tray. Bake for 20 minutes, flipping halfway. Remove from the oven, brush with another 50g of BBQ sauce, then broil on high for 5 minutes until slightly charred. Let rest for a few minutes. For the Crispy Potatoes: Preheat the air fryer to 180°C or the oven to 200°C. Toss the diced potatoes with salt, pepper, smoked paprika, and garlic powder. Lightly spray with cooking oil. Then air fry for 25-30 minutes or bake for 25-30 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and crispy. Finally, just divide the BBQ chicken and the crispy potatoes into 4 equal meal prep containers and store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat add the contents to a pan and heat through on medium heat for 3-4 minutes. Optionally, you can re-airfry the potatoes to get them extra crispy. Enjoy!

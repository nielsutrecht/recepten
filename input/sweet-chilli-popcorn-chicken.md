# sweet chilli popcorn chicken

Servings: 4 | Calories: 492kcal | Protein: 50g | Carbs: 64g | Fat: 4g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**crispy POPCORN CHICKEN**

- 800g chicken breast
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp salt & pepper
- 1 egg
- 40g cornflour/cornstarch

**sweet chili glaze**

- 90ml reduced sugar sweet chilli

**sauce (brand: blue dragon)**

- 60ml light soy sauce
- 80ml water
- 1 tsp finely chopped/minced garlic
- 1 tsp finely chopped/minced ginger
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar juice of 1/2 a lime

**sweet Sticky rice**

- 200g uncooked sticky rice (brand: Tesco)
- 3 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp sweetener salt to taste
- garnishes Sesame seeds chopped green onions

## Instructions

1. Prepare the sticky rice by cooking 200g according to the package instructions. Let it cool for 10 minutes, then mix in rice vinegar, sweetener, and a pinch of salt. Set aside. For the popcorn chicken, dice the chicken breast into bite-size pieces and add to a bowl with soy sauce, paprika, garlic powder, salt, black pepper, and an egg. Mix to fully coat the chicken, then transfer it to a large container with cornflour, shaking well until completely coated. For baking, arrange the chicken on a tray, spray with oil, and bake at 220°C for 15-18 minutes, flipping halfway. For air frying, spray the chicken and cook at 200°C for 10-12 minutes, shaking halfway. Meanwhile, make the sweet chili glaze by adding reduced sugar sweet chili sauce, soy sauce, water, finely chopped garlic and ginger, rice vinegar, and lime juice to a pan. Bring to a bubble, then reduce the heat and stir until thickened. Toss the cooked chicken in the sweet chili glaze. Divide the popcorn chicken and seasoned sticky rice into four equal portions in meal prep containers, optionally garnishing with green onions and sesame seeds. Refrigerate for up to five days. To reheat, microwave for 3-4 minutes or heat up in a pan, Enjoy!

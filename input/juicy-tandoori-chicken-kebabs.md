# Juicy Tandoori chicken kebabs

Servings: 4 | Calories: 274kcal | Protein: 35g | Carbs: 11g | Fat: 10g
Section: dinner

## Instructions

1. mixing bowl. Add the Greek yogurt, minced garlic, and all the spices: paprika, cumin, garam masala, turmeric, cayenne, and ground ginger. Season with salt and pepper. Squeeze fresh lemon juice over the mixture. Stir everything well to ensure the chicken pieces are thoroughly coated in the marinade. Cover the bowl and refrigerate. Let the chicken marinate for at least 30 minutes, but for the best flavor, aim for 2-3 hours. Once marinated, thread the chicken pieces onto wooden skewers, distributing them evenly to form four kebabs. For even cooking, elevate the skewers on the sides of an oven dish. Bake them in a preheated oven at 200°C (400°F) for 20-25 minutes. After baking, for added flavor and texture, sear the kebabs in a pan over high heat for 1-2 minutes on each side, until they develop a nice golden char. Serve the tandoori chicken kebabs on a bed of pilau rice, accompanied by a fresh side salad and some mint yoghurt over the kebabs for an extra burst of flavor. Enjoy!

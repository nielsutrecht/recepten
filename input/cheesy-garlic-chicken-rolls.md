# Cheesy garlic chicken rolls

Servings: 8 | Calories: 335kcal | Protein: 37g | Carbs: 28g | Fat: 7g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**chicken**

- 650g raw chicken breast
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp cayenne
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper

**Garlic sauce**

- 200g 0% fat cottage cheese
- 60g 0% fat Greek yoghurt
- 50g light mayo (brand Heinz lighter than light)
- 20g garlic paste
- ½ lemon juice
- 1 tsp honey
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp onion powder

**other ingredients**

- 80g grated mozzarella
- 80g grated cheddar chopped parsley
- 8 protein tortillas (brand:

**mission)**

- Preheat the oven to 200ºC or the air fryer to 180ºC.

## Instructions

1. Dice the chicken breast into small pieces. Season with garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, cayenne, salt, and pepper. Heat a pan over medium-high heat, spray with cooking oil, and cook the chicken until golden and fully cooked. Set aside. For the Garlic Sauce: In a blender, combine cottage cheese, Greek yogurt, light mayo, garlic paste, lemon juice, honey, salt, black pepper, and onion powder. Blend until smooth. In a large bowl, mix the cooked chicken, chopped parsley, grated mozzarella, grated cheddar, and the blended garlic sauce until well combined. Then to assemble the rolls: Lay out a warm tortilla and spoon 1/8 of the mixture onto the lower third. fold in the sides and roll tightly. Repeat for all tortillas. Place the rolls on a baking sheet and lightly spray with cooking oil. Sprinkle with chopped parsley then bake or air-fry for 8-10 minutes until golden and slightly crispy. Allow to cool then wrap each roll in foil and store in the freezer. To reheat, remove the foil, wrap the roll in a damp paper towel, and squeeze out excess water. Microwave for 4-5 minutes, then unwrap and air-fry or pan-toast on high heat for a couple of minutes until crispy. Enjoy warm with your favorite dipping sauce!

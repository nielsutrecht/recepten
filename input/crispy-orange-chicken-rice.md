# Crispy orange chicken & Rice

Servings: 4 | Calories: 547kcal | Protein: 53g | Carbs: 74g | Fat: 4g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Crispy chicken**

- 800g chicken breast (diced)
- 90g cornstarch
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp white pepper
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 egg cooking spray

**orange sauce**

- 200ml fresh orange juice (or from a carton)
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp water
- 4 cloves garlic (minced)
- 1 tsp ginger (minced)
- 5g diced red chilli or 1 tsp chilli flakes
- 1 tsp orange zest
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 2 tsp honey
- 30g brown stevia
- 20g cornstarch (mixed with

**water to prevent clumping)**


**other ingredients**

- 200g uncooked white rice
- sliced green onions (garnish) sesame seeds (garnish)

## Instructions

1. Rinse the white rice until the water runs clear, then cook according to packet instructions. Set aside once done. Add the diced chicken to a bowl, season with salt and white pepper, and baking powder (to tenderise), then crack in the egg and mix well to coat. In a shallow bowl, spread out the cornstarch. Add a few pieces of chicken at a time, rolling them to fully coat in the cornstarch. Repeat until all the chicken is coated. Preheat the air fryer to 200ºC/400ºF. Spray the basket with cooking spray and arrange the coated chicken in a single layer. Air fry for 10–12 minutes until golden and crispy, working in batches if needed. In a jug, whisk together the soy sauce, orange juice, rice vinegar, orange zest, brown stevia, and cornstarch slurry. Set aside. Spray a large pan or wok with cooking spray and place over medium-high heat. Add the garlic, ginger, fresh chilli or chilli flakes. Stir for a minute until fragrant, then pour in the sauce mixture. Bring to a bubble and simmer until thickened. Remove the pan from the heat, add the crispy chicken, and toss to coat in the sauce. Serve hot with a quarter of the cooked white rice, garnished with chopped green onion and sesame seeds. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days. When you’re ready to eat, microwave for 3–4 minutes till warmed through. Enjoy!

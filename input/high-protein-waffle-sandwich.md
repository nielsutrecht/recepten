# high protein waffle sandwich

Servings: 1 | Calories: 490kcal | Protein: 42g | Carbs: 40g | Fat: 17g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**Waffle mix**

- 50g oat flour (50g blended rolled oats)
- 30ml 2% milk
- 1 egg
- 0.5 tsp of baking powder Pinch of salt

**for the sandwich**

- 2 lean chicken sausages
- 30g (1) bacon medallion (short-cut bacon)
- 1 beaten egg
- 1 light cheese single (brand: Aldi)

**Optional Topping**

- 0 cal maple syrup (brand: Protein Works)

## Instructions

1. To a mixing bowl add oat flour, low fat milk, 1 egg, baking powder and a pinch of salt. Stir to into a batter then set it aside for 5-10 minutes to thicken up while we make the other sandwich components. Remove the chicken sausage meat from the casing, then wet your hands slightly and form a patty. Cook the patty in a pan with low cal spray on high heat for 2-3 minutes on each side till it gets a golden sear. Set the patty aside and in the same pan cook the bacon medallions till crispy. Next, turn the heat to low, and pour in the beaten egg. Tilt the pan to spread the egg out evenly in the bottom of the pan, then once it’s cooked through, carefully fold it over into a square. Pour 1/2 the waffle mixture into the centre of your preheated waffle iron and cook for 2-3 minutes or till it gets a crispy, golden exterior, repeat for the other waffle. To assemble the sandwich place a slice of light cheese on the bottom waffle followed by the sausage patty, folded egg, bacon and the top waffle. Optionally drizzle in maple syrup and Enjoy!

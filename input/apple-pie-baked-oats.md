# APPLE PIE baked oats

Servings: 6 | Calories: 371kcal | Protein: 21g | Carbs: 52g | Fat: 9g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**Dry ingredients**

- 300g rolled oats
- 60g vanilla protein powder (I use Protein Works)
- 50g brown stevia (brand:

**pure via)**

- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp baking powder pinch of salt

**Wet ingredients**

- 480ml skimmed milk
- 3 large eggs

**Filling**

- 4 medium apples (diced) save some slices for toppings!
- 50g brown stevia (brand:
- pure via) - sub with regular sweetener
- 1 tbsp cinnamon splash of water (about 2 tbsp)

**Frosting**

- 50g low cal icing sugar
- substitute (brand: pure via) sub with regular icing sugar
- 10ml water

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 190°C (375°F) and lightly grease a 12” x 9” (30cm x 23cm) baking dish. Peel and dice the apples, saving a couple slices for toppings. Then to a pan on medium heat, melt some butter and add the diced apples along with some brown stevia, cinnamon and around 2 tbsp of water to help soften them. Stir and cook the apples through just until soft. Now in a bowl, mix together the oats, vanilla protein powder, baking powder and a pinch of salt. In a separate bowl, add the eggs and milk, whisking until smooth. Gradually mix the wet ingredients with the dry ingredients, stirring until well combined then fold in the softened cinnamon apples. Pour the oatmeal mixture into the prepared baking dish (optionally grease before-hand) spreading it out for an even bake. Top with the rest of the sliced apples then bake at 190°C (375°F) for 30-35 minutes. While it’s cooking, prepare the low calorie frosting by mixing the 0 cal icing sugar with water until you form a thick frosting. Allow the baked oatmeal to cool slightly, before cutting into six portions. Store refrigerated in meal prep containers for up to six days. When you’re ready to eat, reheat it in a heatproof dish and microwave for 2 minutes. Enjoy your apple pie baked oatmeal!

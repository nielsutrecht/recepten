# CHEESY BUTTER CHICKEN BURRITOS

Servings: 6 | Calories: 585kcal | Protein: 57g | Carbs: 55g | Fat: 13g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**Butter chicken marinade**

- 800g diced chicken breast
- 120g 0% fat Greek yoghurt
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1.5 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1.5 tsp salt
- 2 tsp garlic paste
- 2 tsp ginger paste Juice of one lemon

**Butter chicken sauce**

- 1 medium onion (sliced)
- 20g light butter (brand: lurpak light)

**same seasoning added to chicken**

- 30g tomato paste
- 2 tsp garlic paste
- 2 tsp ginger paste
- 400g chopped tomatoes
- 60g light cream cheese
- 1/3 cup (80ml) water - use the

**tomato can for extra flavour!**


**other ingredients**

- 6 protein tortillas (brand: mission)

**sub with low carb tortillas**

- 450g cooked white rice (75g per burrito)
- 240g low fat cheese (I used 50% less fat cheddar)

## Instructions

1. Start by cooking 450g of white rice (225g dry) and set aside to cool — you’ll need 75g per burrito when assembling. Dice the chicken breast into small bite-sized cubes and place in a bowl with the marinade ingredients: Greek yoghurt, paprika, cumin, garam masala, turmeric, salt, garlic paste, ginger paste, and lemon juice. Mix well to coat and let marinate for at least 30 minutes. Heat a pan over medium-high heat with cooking spray and cook the marinated chicken until halfway done. Remove and set aside. In the same pan, melt the light butter and add the sliced onions, along with the same seasonings used for the chicken. Add the tomato paste, garlic paste, and ginger paste. Toast the mixture for 1–2 minutes until fragrant. Then add the chopped tomatoes, light cream cheese, and ⅓ cup (80ml) of water — using the tomato can for extra flavour if desired. Simmer on medium heat for 5–10 minutes. Transfer the sauce to a blender and blend until smooth, or use an immersion blender directly in the pan. Return the blended sauce to the pan, add back the chicken, and simmer for another 5–8 minutes until the chicken is fully cooked and tender. To assemble, warm each tortilla and layer with 75g of cooked white rice, followed by 1/6th of the butter chicken mixture (weigh and divide), and 40g of low-fat cheese. Roll up tightly into a burrito, folding in the sides. Toast seam-side down in a hot pan with a little butter to seal. Once cooled, wrap each burrito in foil and store in the freezer. To reheat, remove the foil, wrap the burrito in a damp paper towel and microwave for 6–7 minutes. Then finish in the air fryer or a hot pan to crisp up the outside. Enjoy!

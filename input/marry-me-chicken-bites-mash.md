# Marry me chicken bites & MAsh

Servings: 4 | Calories: 501kcal | Protein: 52g | Carbs: 54g | Fat: 10g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**CHicken Bites**

- 750g chicken breast (diced into 1” cubes)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp italian herbs
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper
- 1 tbsp olive oil

**Creamy mash potatoes**

- 1000g mash potato Pinch of salt
- 20g light butter
- 60ml low fat milk
- 1 tsp minced garlic salt to taste

**MARRY ME SAUCE**

- 1/2 red onion
- 3 garlic cloves
- 20g tomato paste
- 40g sun dried tomatoes
- 350ml chicken stock
- 90g light cream cheese
- 15g grated Parmesan handful of chopped basil
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 10g light butter

## Instructions

1. Boil your potatoes in salted water for around 15 minutes, or until they’re completely soft when poked with a fork. Drain well, then mash until smooth. Add the light butter, low-fat milk, minced garlic, and a pinch of salt, then mix until the mash is creamy and fluffy. Set aside while you prep the chicken. Dice the chicken breast into 1-inch cubes and add them to a bowl with the smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, Italian herbs, salt, pepper, and a tablespoon of olive oil. Mix well until every piece is coated. Heat a pan over medium heat, add a little cooking spray, and cook the chicken bites until they’re golden on the outside and juicy and cooked through on the inside. Remove them from the pan and set aside. In the same pan, melt the light butter and add the diced red onion. Cook until soft, then add the garlic and let it become fragrant. Stir in the tomato paste and toast it for a minute before adding the sun-dried tomatoes, chicken stock, and light cream cheese. Let everything melt together, then add the parmesan, chopped basil, and a tiny pinch of sugar to balance the sauce. Stir on low heat until it thickens and turns creamy. Serve the chicken bites over a scoop of creamy mash and spoon the Marry Me sauce all over the top. It’s rich, comforting, and still super macro-friendly. Divide the rest into four meal prep containers and store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, microwave in a bowl covered with a damp paper towel for 3–4 minutes, stirring halfway, or warm gently in a pan.

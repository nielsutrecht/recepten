# Blueberry Pancake Muffins

Servings: 6 | Calories: 179kcal | Protein: 16g | Carbs: 21g | Fat: 5g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 170g fat free cottage cheese
- 4 eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 20g granulated sweetener
- 120g self raising flour
- 45g vanilla protein powder (use a whey casein blend for the best results) (I use Protein Works)
- 1/2 cup fresh blueberries cooking spray

## Instructions

1. Add the cottage cheese, eggs, vanilla extract, and sweetener to a blender and blend until completely smooth. In a separate bowl, mix together the self-raising flour and vanilla protein powder. Slowly pour the blended wet mixture into the dry ingredients and stir until fully combined with no dry flour remaining. Gently fold in the fresh blueberries. Lightly grease a muffin tin with cooking spray and divide the batter evenly between 6 muffin cups. Sprinkle a few extra blueberries on top if you like. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 15–18 minutes, until risen, lightly golden, and set in the centre. Serve warm while fluffy and soft, or allow to cool completely before storing. Keep in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, microwave for about 1 minute until steamy.

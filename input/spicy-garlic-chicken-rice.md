# Spicy garlic chicken & rice

Servings: 4 | Calories: 465kcal | Protein: 53g | Carbs: 57g | Fat: 3g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Spicy Chicken**

- 800g raw chicken breast
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp olive oil

**Fluffy golden rice**

- 250g washed uncooked white rice
- 15g light butter
- 2 tsp paprika tsp cumin
- ½-1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 350ml chicken stock
- ½ lemon juice

**pomegranate seeds (optional)**


**Garlic sauce**

- 120g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 80g light mayo (Heinz: lighter than light)
- ½ lemon juice
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp black pepper salt to taste

## Instructions

1. Dice the chicken breast into cubes then add it to a bowl with garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, chili powder, cumin, salt, black pepper and olive oil. Mix well with your hands till the chicken is fully coated. Now heat a pan over medium-high heat and add the chicken. Cook, stirring occasionally, until golden brown in colour and fully cooked. set aside in a bowl covered. For the Fluffy Golden Rice. in a saucepan over medium heat, melt the butter. Then add the washed rice and stir in the paprika, cumin, salt, and turmeric. Toast for a 12 minutes, coating it in the spices. Next Pour in the chicken stock, bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low. Cover with a lid and cook until the liquid is fully absorbed and the rice is tender (about 12-15 minutes). Add a squeeze of lemon juice then fluff the rice with a fork and garnish with pomegranate seeds and coriander (if using). For the garlic sauce, in a bowl, mix Greek yogurt, light mayo, lemon juice, honey, garlic powder, black pepper, and salt. Stir until smooth and well combined. Store bottled in the fridge for up to 5 days. Now divide the spicy chicken and fluffy golden rice between 4 meal prep containers. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days and when you’re ready to eat, add the contents of the prep to a pan over medium heat for 3–4 minutes until heated through. Or, reheat it in the microwave for 3–4 minutes, leaving the lid slightly cracked open. Drizzle with the garlic sauce and enjoy!

# raspberry breakfast muffins

Servings: 6 | Calories: 268kcal | Protein: 18g | Carbs: 49g | Fat: 3g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**dry ingredients**

- 190g self raising flour (sub
- with plain flour and 2 tsp of baking powder)
- 50g vanilla protein powder (I use Protein Works)
- 25g raspberry protein

**powder - sub with vanilla**

- ¼ cup 0-calorie baking

**sweetener (brand: pure via)**

- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp baking soda pinch of salt

**Wet ingredients**

- 2 ripe medium bananas
- 225g 0% fat cottage cheese (blended)
- 120ml almond milk
- 10ml vanilla extract

**Mix ins**

- 150g frozen raspberries
- 30g white chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 190°C (375°F). Line a jumbo muffin tin (you can find the silicone tray and cups I use here). In a mixing bowl, whisk together the plain flour, raspberry protein powder, vanilla protein powder, 0-cal baking sweetener, baking powder, baking soda, and a pinch of salt. Blend the 0% fat cottage cheese for at least 2-3 minutes till completely smooth and creamy. In a separate bowl, mash the bananas. Mix in the blended cottage cheese, almond milk, and vanilla extract. Mix until smooth. Combine the wet and dry ingredients, stirring just until mixed (don’t over mix - for moister and fluffier muffins!). Gently fold in frozen raspberries and white chocolate chips then distribute the batter evenly among the jumbo muffin cups, filling each almost to the top. Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted comes out clean. Remove the muffins from the oven and cool in the tray for a few minutes before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely. Store them in an airtight container to keep them moist and fresh. You can keep them at room temperature for up to 2 days, or in the fridge for up to a week.

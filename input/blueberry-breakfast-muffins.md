# Blueberry breakfast muffins

Servings: 6 | Calories: 242kcal | Protein: 17g | Carbs: 40g | Fat: 2g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**dry ingredients**

- 190g self raising flour (sub
- with plain flour and 2 tsp of baking powder)
- 75g vanilla protein powder (I use Protein Works)
- ¼ cup 0-calorie baking

**sweetener (brand: pure via)**

- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp baking soda pinch of salt

**Wet ingredients**

- 2 ripe medium bananas
- 225g 0% fat cottage cheese
- 120ml almond milk
- 10ml vanilla extract

**other ingredients**

- 150g frozen blueberries sprinkle icing sugar

## Notes

Coffee and a high protein muffin. One of the BEST breakfast combos!

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 190°C (375°F). Line a jumbo muffin tin (you can find the silicone tray and cups I use here). In one bowl, whisk together plain flour, vanilla protein powder, 0-cal baking sweetener, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Blend the 0% fat cottage cheese for at least 2-3 minutes till completely smooth and creamy. In a separate bowl, mash the bananas. Mix in the blended cottage cheese, almond milk, and vanilla extract until smooth. Combine the wet and dry ingredients, stirring just until mixed (don’t over mix - for moister and fluffier muffins!) Gently fold in the blueberries and distribute the batter into the jumbo muffin cups, filling almost to the top. Bake for 20-25 minutes or until a toothpick inserted comes out clean. Cool in the tray for a few minutes, then move to a wire rack to cool completely. Optionally give them a light dusting of icing sugar and Enjoy! Store them in an airtight container to keep them moist and fresh. You can keep them at room temperature for up to 2 days, or in the fridge for up to a week.

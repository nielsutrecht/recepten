# EASY SINGLE SERVE SHAKSHUKA

Servings: 1 | Calories: 585kcal | Protein: 33g | Carbs: 53g | Fat: 25g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients

- 200g chopped tomatoes
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 red bell pepper
- 1/2 medium onion
- tsp minced garlic
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp chilli powder
- 1/2 tsp sugar (or sweetener - optional)
- Salt & pepper (to taste)
- 2 tsp olive oil
- 40g low fat feta (crumbled on top)
- chopped parsley (garnish)
- 85g slice of sourdough bread
- Notes Feel free to add more eggs!

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a pan over medium heat, then add the diced onion and bell pepper with a pinch of salt and pepper. Cook for a few minutes until they soften and start to caramelise. Add the minced garlic and stir until fragrant, then mix in the cumin, smoked paprika, chilli powder, and a small dash of sugar if you’re using it. Toast the spices for a minute so the flavour deepens. Pour in the chopped tomatoes along with a splash of water, then stir everything together and let it simmer for a few minutes until the sauce thickens. Use a spoon to make two small wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each one. Reduce the heat slightly, cover with a lid, and cook until the eggs are set to your liking. Crumble the low-fat feta over the top and finish with some chopped parsley. Serve straight from the pan with a slice of toasted sourdough on the side - rich, comforting, and perfect for a quick and nutritious high-protein meal.

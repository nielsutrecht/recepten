# Creamy Cajun Meatball pasta

Servings: 4 | Calories: 509kcal | Protein: 50g | Carbs: 57g | Fat: 9g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Juicy Meatballs**

- 600g 10% ground beef
- 1 egg
- ½ red onion (diced)
- 2 cloves garlic (minced)
- 1/4 cup chopped parsley
- 1 tbsp cajun seasoning
- 1 tsp salt & pepper

**Creamy cajun sauce**

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- ½ red onion (diced)
- 2 garlic cloves (minced)
- 300ml smooth tomato sauce (brand: tesco)
- 50ml low fat milk ( I used
- 2%)
- 70g reduced fat cream

**cheese (brand: Philadelphia lightest)**

- 1 tbsp cajun seasoning
- 1 tsp salt & pepper

**other ingredients**

- 300g dry penne pasta (brand: Tesco)

**Chopped parsley (garnish)**


## Instructions

1. Cook pasta in boiling water seasoned with a tsp of salt until al dente. Save 1/2 cup of pasta water for later. In a mixing bowl, combine ground beef, egg, diced onion, minced garlic, chopped parsley, cajun seasoning, salt, and pepper. Mix well and form into 25-30 meatballs. Cook these in a high-heat pan with cooking spray until golden, about 8-10 minutes, then set aside. In the same pan, sauté diced onion and minced garlic until fragrant. Add tomato sauce, low-fat milk, and light cream cheese, stirring on low heat until the cheese melts. Season with cajun spice, salt, pepper, and the reserved pasta water. Stir until creamy. Mix in the cooked pasta and meatballs, making sure everything is well coated. Divide into four meal-prep containers and refrigerate for up to 5 days. To reheat, add a tsp of water and microwave partially covered for 3-4 minutes. Enjoy!

# Creamy garlic steak burritos

Servings: 6 | Calories: 582kcal | Protein: 52g | Carbs: 35g | Fat: 24g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**Steak strips**

- 900g lean sliced steak (I used flank steak
- slice against the grain for a tender bite)
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp black pepper
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp salt cooking spray

**Lemon & Herb rice**

- 200g washed white rice
- 20g light butter (brand lurpak light)
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 salt
- 400ml chicken stock Fresh chopped parsley
- 1/2 lemon juice

**Creamy garlic sauce**

- 200g 0% fat cottage cheese
- 60g 0% fat Greek yoghurt
- 50g light mayo (brand Heinz lighter than light)
- 20g garlic paste
- 1/2 lemon juice tsp honey
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp onion powder

**other ingredients**

- 80g grated mozzarella
- 80g grated cheddar (I used 50% reduced fat) Chopped parsley
- 6 protein tortillas (brand: mission)

## Instructions

1. Start by preparing the lemon herb rice. Melt the light butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the washed white rice along with paprika, garlic powder, and salt. Stir and toast the rice for 1–2 minutes until fragrant. Pour in the chicken stock, bring to a gentle bubble, then reduce the heat to low and cover. Simmer for 15–20 minutes until the liquid is fully absorbed. Turn off the heat and let it sit covered for 10 minutes, then stir in the lemon juice and chopped parsley. To make the creamy garlic sauce, add the cottage cheese, Greek yoghurt, light mayo, garlic paste, lemon juice, honey, salt, black pepper, and onion powder to a blender. Blend until smooth and creamy. Set aside. Slice the lean steak into strips and season with smoked paprika, garlic powder, black pepper, and salt. Heat a pan over high heat with cooking spray and sear the steak strips in batches for 1–2 minutes per side until golden brown and caramelised. Avoid overcrowding the pan to get a proper sear. Transfer the cooked steak to a large bowl and add the grated cheddar, mozzarella, chopped parsley, and all of the garlic sauce. Mix well until everything is creamy and evenly coated. To assemble, warm the tortillas and spoon 75g of the lemon herb rice into the centre. Top with 1/6th of the creamy garlic steak mixture (weigh and divide evenly), then fold in the sides and roll tightly into a burrito. Toast the burritos seam-side down in a hot pan with a small amount of light butter until golden and sealed. Once cool, wrap each burrito in foil and store in the freezer. To reheat, remove the foil and wrap the burrito in a damp paper towel. Microwave for 4–5 minutes from chilled or 6–7 minutes from frozen, then finish in the air fryer or hot pan for 1–2 minutes until crisped up. Serve hot and enjoy!

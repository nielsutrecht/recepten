# Chocolate french toast Sticks

Servings: 1 | Calories: 557kcal | Protein: 47g | Carbs: 59g | Fat: 14g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**French Toast sticks**

- 4 slices medium wholemeal bread
- 2 large eggs
- 30ml almond milk
- 15g chocolate protein
- powder (I use Protein Works chocolate silk)
- 1 tbsp 0-cal sweetener (I used stevia)
- 1 tsp cinnamon low cal cooking spray

**NO-Tella Filling**

- ½ banana
- 15g chocolate protein powder
- 1 tbsp almond milk
- 1 tbsp cocoa powder

**toppings**

- 0 cal choc fudge syrup (brand: Protein Works) sliced strawberries (optional)

## Instructions

1. To a wide dish or bowl add the eggs, milk, chocolate protein powder, 0-cal sweetener and cinnamon. Whisk well until smooth. To a separate bowl mash 1/2 a banana then add 15g chocolate protein powder, almond milk and cocoa powder. Mix together until a smooth. This is your delicious No-tella filling! Spread no-tella onto two of the slices of bread and place the other two on top to form two sandwiches. Cut off the crusts (optional) then slice each sandwich into thirds to form six french toast sticks. Add them to a pan with low cal cooking spray and toast on all sides till golden brown. Serve with your choice of toppings, I used sliced strawberries and sugar free chocolate syrup. Enjoy!

# Chocolate Spread Rice cakes

Servings: 1 | Calories: 199kcal | Protein: 14g | Carbs: 30g | Fat: 2g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 3 rice cakes (7g each)
- 15g chocolate protein powder (I use Protein Works)
- 25ml 2% milk
- 1 tsp cocoa powder
- 1 tsp maple syrup pinch of salt
- in a small bowl mix together the chocolate protein
- powder, cocoa powder, milk maple syrup and a pinch
- of salt until it reaches a thick and smooth consistency
- spread 1/3 of the chocolate mixture onto each rice
- cake, and Enjoy - I like to put these in the microwave
- for 10-15 seconds, to give them it that warm melted chocolate feel!

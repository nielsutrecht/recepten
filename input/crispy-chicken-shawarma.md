# crispy Chicken Shawarma

Servings: 4 | Calories: 565kcal | Protein: 53g | Carbs: 36g | Fat: 22g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**shawarma chicken**

- 900g boneless skinless chicken thighs (fat trimmed off)
- 80g 0% Greek yoghurt juice of half a lemon
- 2 tsp minced garlic

**shawarma seasoning (listed below)**

- Shawarma seasoning (homemade) tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp all spice
- 1 tsp cayenne
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- 2 tsp salt

**rispy Shawarma coating**

- 3 tbsp avocado oil
- 2 tbsp tomato paste salt to taste dried chilli flakes

**Garlic Sauce**

- 120g 0% greek yoghurt
- 30g light mayo
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp garlic powder or minced garlic
- 1/2 tsp salt tsp honey (optional)

## Instructions

1. Slice the chicken thighs into strips and add them to a bowl with the Greek yoghurt, lemon juice, minced garlic, and all of the shawarma seasoning. Mix well until fully coated and let it marinate for at least 30 minutes, or longer if you have time — the flavour only gets better. While the chicken marinates, make the garlic sauce by mixing the Greek yoghurt, light mayo, lemon juice, garlic, salt, and honey if using. Stir until smooth and set aside. Line a baking tray and spray lightly with cooking spray. Spread the marinated chicken out in an even layer and bake at 200°C (400°F) for 20–25 minutes, tossing halfway. Once cooked, switch the oven to grill/broil on high and cook for another 3–5 minutes until you get a golden char on the chicken. For the crispy shawarma coating, mix together the avocado oil, tomato paste, salt, and dried chilli flakes until combined. Carefully separate each Lebanese wrap into two thinner layers.

2. Other ingredients 4 Lebanese wraps (60g each) carefully seperate into two layers sliced pickes

3. To assemble, spread a generous layer of garlic sauce onto a wrap, add the shawarma chicken, and top with sliced pickles. Roll tightly into a wrap. Heat a pan over medium heat, brush or spoon the shawarma coating onto the outside of the wrap, and toast, rolling frequently, until golden and crispy on all sides. Serve hot while crispy and loaded with flavour. These are best enjoyed fresh, but you can store cooled wraps wrapped in foil in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, microwave for a couple of minutes to remove the chill, then air fry or pan toast to bring the crisp back.

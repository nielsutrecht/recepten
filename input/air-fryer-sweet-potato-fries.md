# air fryer Sweet potato fries

Servings: 1 | Calories: 270kcal | Protein: 5g | Carbs: 44g | Fat: 8g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**Ingredients**

- 250g sweet potato (raw & peeled)
- ½ tsp paprika
- 1-½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ¼-½ tsp chili powder cooking oil spray
- Notes Serve these up with your favourite low cal dip. I
- like to make a garlic dip with a greek yoghurt base!
- Peel and cut the sweet potato into evenly sized fries. Add to a
- bowl and toss with paprika, salt, garlic powder, onion powder,
- black pepper, and chili powder until well coated.

## Instructions

1. Spray the fries lightly with cooking oil and toss again to distribute. Preheat the air fryer to 190ºC/375ºF. Arrange the fries in a single layer in the basket, working in batches if needed. Air fry for 15 minutes, shaking the basket halfway through, until crispy and golden on the edges. Serve hot, with your favorite dip and enjoy!

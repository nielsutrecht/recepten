# Mexican cheesy beef burritos

Servings: 6 | Calories: 583kcal | Protein: 51g | Carbs: 57g | Fat: 15g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**taco beef**

- 800g lean ground beef (5% or less)
- 1 medium red onion (diced)
- 1 red bell pepper (diced)
- 2 tsp paprika
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp cayenne
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp oregano
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 200g fajita sauce (brand: old el paso) cooking spray

**Mexican red rice**

- 15g light butter
- 200g washed rice
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp cayenne
- 1 tsp salt
- 35g tomato paste
- 400ml chicken stock (brand: oxo)

**other ingredients**

- 6 protein tortillas (brand: mission)
- sub with low carb tortillas
- 180g low fat cheese

## Instructions

1. Spray a large pan with cooking spray and place over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef and cook until halfway browned, breaking it up as it cooks. Stir in the paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, cayenne, cumin, oregano, salt, and black pepper. Add the diced red onion and red bell pepper, and sauté until softened. Pour in the fajita sauce and stir to coat evenly. Simmer for a few more minutes, then remove from heat. In a separate saucepan, melt the light butter over medium heat. Add the washed rice, garlic powder, onion powder, cayenne, salt, and tomato paste. Toast the rice for a couple of minutes until fragrant. Pour in the chicken stock, bring to a bubble, then reduce the heat to low. Cover and simmer for 15–20 minutes, or until the liquid is absorbed and the rice is cooked through. To assemble, warm the tortillas slightly to make them pliable. Spoon about onesixth of the rice (roughly 80g) into the center of a tortilla, followed by one-sixth of the taco beef (roughly 150g), and top with 30g of cheese. Fold in the sides, then roll up tightly into a burrito. Repeat with the remaining ingredients to make six burritos. Toast each burrito in a hot pan to seal, optionally with a little butter for added crisp. Allow to cool then wrap tightly in foil and store in the freezer for months. To reheat, remove the foil and wrap the frozen burrito in a damp paper towel. Microwave on a plate for 6–7 minutes, then unwrap and toast in an air fryer or pan on high heat for a couple of minutes until crispy. Enjoy!

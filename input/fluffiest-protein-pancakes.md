# FLuffiest protein Pancakes

Servings: 1 | Calories: 685kcal | Protein: 65g | Carbs: 70g | Fat: 15g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**Wet ingredients**

- 3 eggs
- 120g fat free cottage cheese dash of vanilla extract (optional)

**Dry ingredients**

- 80g plain flour (you can also
- use oat flour for more fibre!)
- 30g vanilla whey protein (I use Protein Works)
- 15g 0 calorie sweetener of choice
- 1 tsp baking powder pinch of salt (optional)

**TOppings**


## Instructions

1. In a blender, combine the eggs, cottage cheese, and vanilla extract. Blend until completely smooth. In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, whey protein, sweetener, baking powder, and salt. Gradually pour in the blended wet ingredients, stirring gently until just combined. Avoid over-mixing to keep the pancakes light and fluffy.

2. maple syrup (optional)

3. Notes These work great for meal prep, when you need a quick snack. Just make a big batch and freeze!

4. Preheat a lightly buttered pan over low heat. Add about 1/5 of the batter (around ¼ cup) to the pan. Cover with a lid and cook for about 2 minutes, or until bubbles appear on the surface. Flip, replace the lid, and cook for another 1-2 minutes until golden brown. Repeat with the remaining batter. Serve warm with maple syrup or toppings of choice. For meal prep, allow the pancakes to cool fully, then put them in a vacuum seal bag (or ziploc) and place in the freezer. To reheat microwave for 1-2 minutes till warm and bouncy to touch.

# freezable chicken & cheese tacos

Servings: per | Calories: 203kcal | Protein: 23g | Carbs: 14g | Fat: 6g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**Shredded taco chicken**

- 800g chicken breast
- taco seasoning (brand: old El Paso)
- 45g of Tomato paste/puree
- 30g diced jalapeños

**(optional), feel free to use**

- diced bell peppers as well for extra veg!
- 130-150ml of water (keeps chicken juicy) cooking spray

**cheese mix**

- 120g reduced fat grated cheddar (brand: Tesco)
- 120g grated mozzarella

**(you can used reduced fat**

- if you have it for less cals)

**other ingredients**

- 12 (28g) mini corn tortillas (brand Old El Paso)
- To start, bring a large pot of water to a gentle simmer and add the chicken
- breasts. Poach for 12-15 minutes, then immediately shred using two forks
- or an electric whisk and bowl (much quicker).
- Now, to a hot pan with cooking spray, add the chicken with some taco

## Instructions

1. seasoning, tomato paste, water, and some diced jalapeños. Mix everything together until the chicken is well coated. For the cheese mix, in a bowl, combine reduced-fat grated cheddar and grated mozzarella and mix to combine. Then, assemble each taco on a mini corn tortilla by adding 70g of the chicken mix and 20g of the cheese mix. Fold over into a taco and repeat for 12 tacos. Toast the tacos in a pan with cooking spray for 1-2 minutes on each side until crispy and the cheese is melted, then allow them to cool on a rack. For storage, you have a few options: 1) Flash freeze for 1 hour (spread out on a baking tray), then bag up in one and return to the freezer. This will stop them from sticking together. 2) Wrap in foil and store immediately in the freezer. 3) Vacuum seal as shown in the video and store in the freezer. The vacuum bags are from Zwilling. To reheat, remove the foil if applicable, then place the taco in a hot pan with no oil. Heat for 2-3 minutes on each side until the sides are crispy and the cheese is melted. Enjoy!

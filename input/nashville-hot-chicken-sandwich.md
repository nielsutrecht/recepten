# NASHVILLE HOT Chicken sandwich

Servings: 2 | Calories: 563kcal | Protein: 51g | Carbs: 69g | Fat: 8g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**Crispy chicken**

- 300g chicken breast
- 2 lidl brioche buns (150 cal)
- 100g all purpose flour (not all is used)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 egg
- 30ml pickle juice
- 100g cornflakes (not all are used,
- weigh the left over amount and subtract) cooking spray

**NASHVILLE glaze**

- 80ml buffalo sauce
- 15g light butter
- 10g brown stevia
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder

**high protein ranch**

- 150g cottage
- 35g light mayo
- 30ml pickle juice
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp onion powder
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp dill
- 1 tsp honey

**OTHER INGREDIENTS**

- 2 Aldi brioche buns (159 cals each!)

## Instructions

1. Flatten the chicken breast slightly, then slice it into two even fillets. Set up your coating stations: crush the cornflakes gently in one bowl, whisk the egg with the pickle juice in a second bowl, and add the flour to a third bowl along with the smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, oregano, and salt. Mix the flour well so the seasoning is evenly distributed. Coat each chicken fillet in the seasoned flour, making sure every part is covered, then dip it into the egg mixture, and finally press it into the crushed cornflakes. Press down firmly so the coating sticks and creates a crunchy exterior. Place the coated chicken on a rack, spray lightly with cooking spray, and air fry at 190°C (375°F) for 12–15 minutes, or oven bake at 200°C (400°F) for 18–20 minutes, flipping halfway until crispy and golden. While the chicken cooks, melt the light butter in a small bowl (10-15 seconds in the microwave), then whisk in the buffalo sauce, brown stevia, smoked paprika, and garlic powder to make your Nashville glaze. Keep it warm until the chicken is ready then glaze with a brush. For the quick coleslaw, add the cottage cheese, light mayo, pickle juice, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, dill, and honey to a blender cup. Blend until smooth, then transfer to a bowl with shredded cabbage and mix well to coat. Lightly toast the brioche buns, then assemble: layer slices of pickles on the base bun, add your hot and glazed Nashville chicken, pile on the creamy slaw, and finish with the top bun. Serve while hot. This is spicy, crispy, creamy, and absolutely unreal for just 560 calories and 51g of protein.

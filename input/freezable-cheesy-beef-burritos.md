# Freezable CHeesy beef Burritos

Servings: 6 | Calories: 433kcal | Protein: 45g | Carbs: 35g | Fat: 13g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**CHeesy beef**

- 700g 10% ground beef
- 1 white onion (diced)
- 2 cloves garlic (minced)
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp salt & pepper
- 1 packet taco seasoning (brand: Old El Paso)
- 150g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 60g light cream cheese (brand: Philadelphia lightest)
- 60ml water
- 80g low fat grated cheddar (brand: Aldi:
- 50% less fat) low cal cooking spray

**Other ingredients**

- 6 protein tortillas (brand:

**Aldi protein tortillas)**


## Instructions

1. In a high-heat pan with low-calorie cooking spray, add the lean ground beef and cook until browned (about 8-10 minutes). Add diced onions and garlic, stirring until softened. Season with paprika, onion powder, salt, pepper, and taco seasoning, making sure the beef is well coated. Mix in 150g of 0% Greek yogurt, 60g light cream cheese, and 60ml water. Stir on low heat until creamy. Add low-fat cheddar and stir to melt. Place 1/6 of the cheesy beef mix on the lower third of a protein tortilla. Fold into a burrito and repeat for the remaining five. Wrap the burritos in foil and store in the freezer for up to two months. To reheat, remove the foil, wrap in a damp paper towel and microwave on a plate for 5-6 minutes, flipping over at halfway, unwrap carefully from the paper towel and enjoy! Optionally toast in the pan after reheating to get it crispy!

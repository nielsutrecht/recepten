# salt ‘n’ pepper chicken & chips

Servings: 4 | Calories: 478kcal | Protein: 54g | Carbs: 52g | Fat: 6g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**salt ‘n’ pepper chicken**

- 800g chicken breast
- 20ml soy sauce
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 20g corn flour
- 5g baking soda (tenderises the chicken) cooking spray

**Crispy seasoned chips**

- 900g potatoes (sliced into chip/fry shapes)
- 2 salt & pepper
- 1 ½ garlic powder
- pinch of MSG for that classic

**takeout flavour (optional) cooking spray**


**other ingredients**

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 garlic cloves (diced)
- 2 medium bell peppers (sliced into 1” pieces)
- 1 medium white onion (halve and slice into 1/4’s)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- sliced green onions (green parts only)
- Optionally peel and slice 900g of potatoes into chip shapes.

## Instructions

1. Season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder, then spray with cooking oil. Bake at 200°C (400ºF) for 25-30 minutes or air fry at 200°C for 18-20 minutes, shaking halfway through for even cooking. Cube 800g of chicken breast and place in a bowl. Season with garlic powder, onion powder, salt, black pepper, cornflour, and baking soda for tenderness. Ensure the chicken is well-coated. In a pan with cooking spray, cook the chicken over medium-high heat until golden and crispy. Remove and set aside. In the same pan, heat a tablespoon of olive oil and sauté sliced onions and bell peppers until soft. Add diced garlic and cook for another 2 minutes until fragrant. Introduce the crispy chips back into the pan, seasoning as before, a pinch of MSG if using and stir to combine everything well. Portion out the salt and pepper chicken and chips into four mealprep containers. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days. For reheating, microwave for 3 minutes until warm. For extra crispiness, air fry the chips separately for 2-3 minutes. Enjoy!

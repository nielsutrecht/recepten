# 3 ingredient Blueberry muffins

Servings: 12 | Calories: 139kcal | Protein: 11g | Carbs: 20g | Fat: 2g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**Dry ingredients**

- 270g sugar-free vanilla cake mix (Brand: Betty Crocker but any other brand works)
- 140g vanilla whey/casein blend
- Preheat the oven to 180ºC/350ºF and line a 12-hole

**muffin tray with paper cases.**


**wet ingredients**

- 375ml zero calorie lemonade or sparkling water

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, mix together the vanilla cake mix and protein powder until well combined. Pour in the lemonade or sparkling water and stir gently until just combined with no clumps—don’t overmix. Evenly divide the batter between the 12 muffin cases. Bake for 15-17 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Let cool completely before removing from the tray. Store in an airtight container and enjoy!

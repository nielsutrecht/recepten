# Korean Fried CHicken Burger

Servings: 2 | Calories: 486kcal | Protein: 42g | Carbs: 65g | Fat: 6g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**Crispy chicken**

- 300g (2) chicken breast
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp salt & pepper
- 100g cornflakes (25g per burger for tracking)
- 1 egg (for egg wash)

**Korean glaze**

- 2 tbsp soy sauce

**Reduced sugar ketchup (brand:**


**heinz)**

- 1 tsp Gochujang paste (brand:

**tesco)**

- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 25 ml water

**Korean slaw**

- 130g Green cabbage (shredded)
- 30g carrot (shredded)
- 20g green onions (sliced)
- 50g lighter than light mayo (brand: lidl)
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar

**Other ingredients**

- 2 brioche burger buns (brand:

**aldi)**


## Instructions

1. Slice chicken breast into halves and season with paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Whisk an egg in a bowl. In another bowl, lightly crush approximately 100g of cornflakes. Dip chicken in egg, then press into cornflakes until evenly coated. Bake or air fry at 200°C (400°F) for 15-20 minutes until golden and crispy. Prepare the Korean slaw by combining shredded green cabbage, shredded carrot, chopped green onion tops, light mayo, and apple cider vinegar in a bowl. For the glaze, on low heat, mix soy sauce, ketchup, Gochujang paste, honey, apple cider vinegar, and water in a pan. Stir until it thickens into a glaze. Toss the crispy chicken in the glaze. Assemble the burger with lightly toasted brioche buns, Korean slaw, your crispy glazed chicken, and top bun. Enjoy!

# LOADED BREAKFAST BURRITOS

Servings: 4 | Calories: 567kcal | Protein: 48g | Carbs: 50g | Fat: 18g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**Crispy Potatoes**

- 720g diced and optional peeled potatoes
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper
- 2 tsp olive oil (or cooking spray)

**Bacon mix**

- 400g diced shortcut bacon (I used
- lean pork bacon you can also use turkey or beef bacon)
- 1 diced bell pepper (I used half green, half red
- 1 medium diced brown onion cooking spray

**fluffy scrambled eggs**

- 8 beaten eggs
- 10g light butter

**Creamy sauce**

- 200g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 60g light mayo
- 30ml sriracha
- 20-30ml water (adjust to desired consistency)

**Optional tsp honey for sweetness**


**other ingredients**

- 180g low fat grated cheddar (I used
- 50% reduced fat)
- 6 protein tortillas (brand: mission)
- Peel and dice the potatoes, then toss them with smoked paprika, salt, pepper, and a

## Instructions

1. drizzle of olive oil (or cooking spray). Air fry or bake until golden and crispy - around 20– 25 minutes at 200°C (400°F), shaking halfway. Heat a pan with a little cooking spray and add the diced shortcut bacon. Cook until it gets a crispy sear, then remove and set aside. In the same pan, add the diced onion and bell pepper. Cook until soft and fragrant, then add the bacon back in and mix everything together. Scramble the eggs in a pan with the light butter over medium heat, stirring gently until soft and fluffy. For the creamy sauce, mix the Greek yoghurt, light mayo, sriracha, and a splash of water until smooth. Add honey if you want a hint of sweetness. Warm your tortillas, then layer on a spoonful of the sauce followed by crispy potatoes, scrambled eggs, the bacon-pepper mix, and a sprinkle of low-fat cheddar. Fold in the sides and roll tightly into a burrito. Repeat for all six. Toast each burrito in a pan - folded side down - with a tiny bit of butter or spray to seal and crisp the outside. Let them cool before wrapping in foil. Store in the fridge for up to a week or freeze for a couple of months. To reheat, remove the foil, wrap the burrito in a damp paper towel, and microwave for 4–5 minutes from the fridge or 8–10 minutes from frozen, flipping halfway. Then toast or air fry for a couple of minutes to bring back the crisp. Super filling, high protein, and perfect for grab-and-go breakfasts through the week.

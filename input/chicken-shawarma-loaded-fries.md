# CHicken shawarma loaded fries

Servings: 4 | Calories: 562kcal | Protein: 46g | Carbs: 52g | Fat: 19g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**shawarma chicken**

- 1000g boneless skinless chicken thighs (trim excess fat)
- 100g 0% Greek yoghurt Juice of 1 lemon
- 2 tsp honey
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp allspice
- 1 tsp cayenne
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- 1.5 salt

**homemade fries**

- 1000g raw potato

**Cooking spray or olive oil**

- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1.5 tsp salt

**garlic sauce**

- 125g 0% greek yoghurt
- 60g light mayo
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp honey
- 1/2 lemon juice Salt and pepper to taste
- 60-80ml water to preferred consistency

**pickled red onions**

- 1 large red onion
- 100ml distilled vinegar
- 100ml water
- 1/4 cup sugar
- other ingredients sliced gherkins fresh parsley to garnish

## Instructions

1. Slice the potatoes into fry shapes, then toss them with smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, salt, and a good spray of cooking oil (or a drizzle of olive oil). Air fry at 180°C for 25–30 minutes or oven bake at 200°C for 25–30 minutes, shaking halfway, until the fries are golden and crispy. While they cook, trim the chicken thighs and add them to a bowl with the Greek yoghurt, lemon juice, honey, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, allspice, cayenne, oregano, cumin, and salt. Mix well to coat, then marinate for at least 30 minutes (overnight is even better). Air fry at 180°C for 15–20 minutes or oven bake at 200°C for 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway. Once cooked, slice into strips. For the garlic white sauce, mix together the Greek yoghurt, light mayo, garlic powder, honey, lemon juice, salt, pepper, and enough water to reach your preferred consistency. To make quick pickled red onions, thinly slice the onion and add it to a heat-safe jar. Bring the vinegar, water, and sugar to a boil, then pour it over the onions. Seal the jar and let it pickle for 30–60 minutes. Now assemble your loaded fries: start with a base of crispy fries, add the sliced shawarma chicken, then top with pickled onions, sliced gherkins, plenty of garlic white sauce, and a sprinkle of chopped parsley. For meal prep, store the fries, chicken, sauce, and toppings in separate containers for up to 5 days. Reheat the fries in the air fryer or oven to get them crispy again, warm the chicken in the microwave for 2–3 minutes, then build your loaded fries fresh.

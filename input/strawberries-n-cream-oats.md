# strawberries n' cream oats

Servings: 1 | Calories: 468kcal | Protein: 47g | Carbs: 56g | Fat: 6g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**strawberry filling**

- 40g rolled oats
- 160g 0% fat Greek yoghurt
- 20g light cream cheese (brand: Philadelphia lighter)
- 60ml unsweetened almond milk
- 3 medium strawberries

**finely diced up or mashed**

- 30g strawberry protein
- powder - sub with vanilla (I use Protein Works strawberries ‘n’ cream)

**topping**

- 15g light Squirty cream (brand: anchor light)
- 1 diced strawberry

## Instructions

1. In a mixing jug, combine oats, Greek yogurt, cream cheese, almond milk, strawberry protein powder (or vanilla) and three mashed strawberries. Mix until everything is well combined. Transfer the mixture into a mason jar or container. Seal the container and place it in the fridge for at least 2 hours or overnight. When it’s time to enjoy, top with whipped cream, diced strawberry and enjoy!

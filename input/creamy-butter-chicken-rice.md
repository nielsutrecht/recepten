# Creamy butter chicken & rice

Servings: 4 | Calories: 527kcal | Protein: 57g | Carbs: 57g | Fat: 8g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**butter Chicken marinade Chicken marinade**

- 800g chicken breast
- 180g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 1 tsp garlic paste
- 1 tsp ginger paste
- 1 tsp paprika
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp coriander
- 1 tbsp garam masala
- 1 tsp turmeric
- butter chicken sauce Butter chicken sauce
- 20g unsalted butter
- 1 medium white onion (sliced julienne)
- 1 tsp garlic paste
- 1 tsp ginger paste
- 1 tbsp Tomato paste
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp coriander
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp chilli powder (optionally sub

**with paprika for less spice)**

- 200ml chicken stock (brand: oxo)
- 70g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 400g chopped canned tomatoes

**other ingredients**

- 200g basmati rice (uncooked weight)
- 50ml light single cream coriander leaves

## Instructions

1. Cook the rice according to the package and set it aside. For the marinade, combine diced chicken breast, Greek yogurt, garlic and ginger paste, paprika, cumin, salt, dried coriander, garam masala, and turmeric in a bowl. Mix well and let it sit for 30 minutes to 2 hours. In a hot pan coated with cooking spray, cook the chicken until it’s just about done, then take it out and keep it aside. In the same pan, melt a bit of butter and cook the onions until they're soft. Add in garlic and ginger paste, tomato paste, and all the spices. Cook everything for a couple of minutes, then add chicken stock and chopped tomatoes. Mix in the Greek yogurt, then take the mixture off the heat and blend until it’s smooth. Put the chicken back in the pan, pour the smooth sauce over it, and let it simmer for about 8-10 minutes until the sauce has thickened. Drizzle with light cream before serving. To assemble, spoon the butter chicken over the cooked rice into meal prep containers. They’re good in the fridge for up to 5 days. When you’re ready to eat, microwave with a cover for 3-4 minutes, stirring halfway. Enjoy!

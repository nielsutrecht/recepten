# creamy cajun chicken & rice

Servings: 4 | Calories: 547kcal | Protein: 55g | Carbs: 55g | Fat: 9g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**cajun seasoning (homemade)**

- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp ground black pepper
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper (adjust for heat preference)
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 2 tsp salt

**Cajun chicken**

- 800g boneless skinless chicken breast (flattened)
- 20g Cajun seasoning mix Cooking spray

**cajun fried rice**

- 700g cooked cold white rice (roughly 230g dry, cook

**beforehand and refrigerate)**

- 20g light butter (brand: lurpak)
- 1/2 red onion (diced)
- 2 cloves garlic (diced)
- 2 tsp Cajun seasoning mix

## Instructions

1. Start by making the Cajun fried rice. Melt the light butter in a pan over medium heat, then add the diced onion and garlic. Cook until fragrant and slightly softened, then sprinkle in the Cajun seasoning and toast it for a minute to bring out the flavour. Add the cooked cold white rice and toss well until every grain is coated in the buttery Cajun mix. Set aside while you cook the chicken. Flatten the chicken breasts so they cook evenly, then spray lightly with cooking oil and coat both sides with the Cajun seasoning mix. Cook over medium-high heat for 3–4 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through. Once done, set them aside and keep warm.

2. Creamy cajun sauce 200ml low fat or fat free milk 150g light cream cheese 20g Parmesan 1 tsp cajun seasoning (Rest of Cajun seasoning) 10g light butter

3. In the same pan, melt the light butter for the sauce. Add the remaining Cajun seasoning, then pour in the low-fat milk and add the light cream cheese. Stir gently until everything melts together into a smooth sauce. Add the grated parmesan and keep stirring on low heat until it thickens and turns creamy. Serve the chicken over a bed of Cajun fried rice and spoon the creamy Cajun sauce on top. It’s rich, spicy, and incredibly satisfying for only 547 calories and 55g protein per serving. Store for up to 5 days in the fridge — you can keep the sauce separate if you prefer. To reheat, microwave in a bowl covered with a damp paper towel for 3–4 minutes, mixing halfway, or warm gently in a pan.

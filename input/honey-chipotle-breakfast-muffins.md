# Honey CHipotle breakfast muffins

Servings: 6 | Calories: 381kcal | Protein: 40g | Carbs: 29g | Fat: 12g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**Chipotle beef patties**

- 720g lean ground beef
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp parsley
- 20g chipotle paste (brand:

**Waitrose)**

- 1 tbsp honey cooking spray

**Egg slices**

- 6 large eggs (beaten) salt to taste

**chopped chives (optional)**


**sriracha mayo**

- 45g lighter than light mayo
- 15g sriracha (you can also use store bought, but it will have slightly more calories)

**other ingredients**

- 6 English muffins
- 6 light cheese singles (brand: Tesco)
- Into a greased shallow baking dish crack the eggs, whisk to combine,
- then season with salt & chives.

## Instructions

1. Bake the eggs at 180°C (375°F) for 12-15 minutes, keep checking on them so they don’t burn! Once cooked slice them into 6 even squares and set aside. Now season your lean ground beef with garlic powder, onion powder, salt, parsely, chipotle paste and honey. Mix well till the beef changes colour. Then roughly divide the meat into 6 equal portions and shape them out into patties. Cook these with some cooking spray for 2 mins on each side on a medium-high heat till golden brown. To assemble, lightly toast the sliced muffin, spread 15g of sriracha mayo, then place on one slice of light cheese, followed by the beef patty, the egg slice and the top bun optionally sauced with more sriracha mayo. Repeat for the other 5, then wrap in foil and refrigerate for up to 6 days or freeze to enjoy at your convenience. To reheat from the fridge, unwrap from the foil and microwave for 2-3 minutes till hot. To reheat from frozen, remove the foil, wrap in a damp paper towel and microwave for 3-4 minutes, flipping over at halfway, enjoy!

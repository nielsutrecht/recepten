# Buffalo ranch Chicken sandwich

Servings: 4 | Calories: 419kcal | Protein: 41g | Carbs: 50g | Fat: 5g
Section: lunch

- 550g (2 large) Chicken breast

- Tbsp buffalo sauce (brand: Franks red hot)
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper

**buffalo glaze**

- 180g buffalo sauce (brand: Franks red hot)
- 35g honey

**high protein ranch**

- 180g 0% fat cottage cheese
- 30-40ml pickle juice (from pickle jar)
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp pepper
- ½ tsp cayenne

## Instructions

1. add cornflakes; crush them slightly and season with salt, pepper, and paprika. Dunk each chicken steak first in the egg white mixture, then in the cornflakes. Press down firmly to ensure the cornflakes adhere well. Repeat that for the remaining chicken breasts then bake at 190°C (375°F) for 15-20 minutes, or air fry at 180°C (360°F) for 15-20 minutes. While the chicken is cooking, prepare the sauce. In a pan over low heat, stir together buffalo sauce and honey. Then for the ranch, blend 0% fat cottage cheese, pickle juice, Worcestershire sauce, onion powder, garlic powder, pepper, and cayenne in a blender for at least 2 minutes, until completely smooth. Once the chicken is cooked, coat it in the honey buffalo glaze and assemble the sandwiches with the crispy buffalo chicken, sliced pickles, a tablespoon of high-protein ranch per sandwich, and the top bun. Enjoy!

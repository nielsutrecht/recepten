# Giant pepperoni pizza slice

Servings: 1 | Calories: 575kcal | Protein: 50g | Carbs: 57g | Fat: 16g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**Pizza crust**

- 80g 0% Greek yoghurt (brand: Fage)
- 70g plain flour salt to taste

**TOppings**

- 20g (1) reduced fat string cheese
- 30g pizza sauce
- 75g low fat grated cheese (50% less fat)
- 15g Turkey pepperoni (you can also use beef or regular pepperoni)
- Preheat the oven to 190ºC/375ºF.

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, mix Greek yogurt, flour, and salt until a dough forms. Lightly flour a surface and roll the dough into a large triangular pizza slice shape. Transfer to a lined baking tray. Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border. Tear or slice the string cheese and distribute it across the base. Sprinkle with grated cheese, then top with turkey pepperoni. Bake for 12-15 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted and bubbly. Let it cool slightly before serving. Enjoy.

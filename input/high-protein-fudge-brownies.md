# High Protein Fudge brownies

Servings: 9 | Calories: 173kcal | Protein: 12g | Carbs: 10g | Fat: 10g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**Dry ingredients**

- 70g self-raising flour (sub
- with plain flour + tsp of baking powder)
- 300g of granulated 0 cal sweetener - caster sugar

**alternative (brand: Pure via**

- Tesco)
- 50g cocoa powder
- 60g chocolate protein powder (I use Protein Works chocolate silk) Pinch of salt

**wet ingredients**

- 4 large egg
- 170g 0% greek yoghurt
- 60ml melted coconut oil

**(sub with neutral flavoured oil)**


**other ingredients**

- 20g dark chocolate chips (mix-ins)
- 5g chocolate chips (toppings)

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 175°C (350°F) and prepare a square baking dish by greasing it or lining it with parchment paper. In one bowl, combine self-raising flour, granulated 0 cal sweetener, cocoa powder, chocolate protein powder, and a pinch of salt. Mix these dry ingredients thoroughly to ensure they are well incorporated. In a separate bowl, whisk together the four large eggs, 0% Greek yoghurt, and melted coconut oil. Once the wet ingredients are well combined, gradually fold them into the dry mixture until a uniform batter forms. Stir in 20g of dark chocolate chips into the batter for added texture. Transfer the batter to the prepared baking dish, smoothing it into an even layer. Sprinkle an additional 5g of chocolate chips on top. Bake for 20-25 minutes (keep on the shorter end for max fudginess!). Allow them to cool before slicing, then enjoy the BEST fudge protein brownies!

# One Pot Chicken & Rice prep

Servings: 8 | Calories: 575kcal | Protein: 56g | Carbs: 40g | Fat: 20g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Chicken seasoning**

- 1500g (12) chicken drumsticks all purpose seasoning cayenne pepper salt to taste
- any other seasoning you like cooking oil spray (helps seasoning stick)

**Buttery Beef broth**

- 125g light butter (brand:

**lurpak)**

- 45g tomato paste
- 3 cups water (luke warm)
- 2 beef stock cubes

**Fluffy Fried Rice**

- 300g dry white rice (50g per prep)

**optional veg tenderstem broccoli**

- Notes You can also make this recipe in one big baking dish!

## Instructions

1. Start by seasoning the chicken drumsticks generously with all-purpose seasoning, cayenne pepper, salt, and any additional spices you like. Set aside to marinate while you prepare the broth. In a saucepan over medium heat, melt the light butter and stir in the tomato paste. Let it toast for a minute to bring out the flavour, then add the water and crumble in the beef stock cubes. Stir continuously until fully dissolved and combined into a rich broth. Keep the broth warm on low heat. Next, add 50g of dry white rice and 2 drumsticks to each of 8 glass, ovensafe meal prep containers. You can also cook this in one large baking dish, but note that the cooking time will be slightly longer. Weigh out the total broth and divide it evenly across the 8 preps — roughly 135–140ml per portion. Pour the broth directly over the rice and chicken in each container, place all the preps onto a large oven tray, and bake at 200ºC/400ºF for 50–60 minutes, or 1 hour 10–20 minutes if using a single large dish. Once done, carefully remove from the oven and let cool to room temperature. Store sealed in the fridge for up to 7 days. To reheat, simply microwave each portion until piping hot, stirring the rice and rotating halfway for even heat.

Servings: 1 | Calories: 471kcal | Protein: 53g | Carbs: 33g | Fat: 14g
Section: lunch

- 90g cooked chicken (I used store bought but you can use your own)
- 35g low fat cheese
- 80g blended 0% cottage cheese (you don’t have to blend it but I like to for a creamier texture)
- 1 tbsp light mayo

1. filling into the centre. Fold in the sides, tuck the bottom up, and roll into a tight parcel to keep everything sealed. Toast the parcel in a pan over medium heat with a little cooking spray until the outside is golden, crispy, and the cheese inside has fully melted. Alternatively, air fry at 180°C (375°F) until crispy and heated through. Serve hot and enjoy. They’re quick, high-protein, and super customisable, so feel free to swap in any other fillings you like to switch things up.

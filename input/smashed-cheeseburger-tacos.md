# SMASHED CHeeseburger tacos

Servings: 4 | Calories: 265kcal | Protein: 26g | Carbs: 18g | Fat: 8g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients

- Ingredients for 1 taco
- 1 mini tortilla (use low carb for less calories)
- 90g lean ground beef (95/5 or less) salt black pepper smoked paprika
- 1 slice light burger cheese shredded lettuce finely diced red/brown onions finely diced pickles low cal Burger sauce (recipe below)

**burger SAUCE**

- 80g low sugar ketchup
- 60g lighter than light mayonnaise
- 8g American mustard
- 20-30ml water (adjust for desired consistency)
- Lay out four mini tortillas and place a 90g ball of lean ground beef
- onto each one. Press each ball down firmly so the beef spreads into
- a thin, even layer across the tortilla. Season generously with salt,

**black pepper, and smoked paprika.**


## Instructions

1. Spray a pan with a little cooking spray and cook the tacos meatside down over medium-high heat until the beef forms a golden, crispy sear. Flip them over to let the tortilla side toast, then add a slice of light burger cheese to each and let it melt as the bottom crisps up. Make the burger sauce by mixing the low-sugar ketchup, lighterthan-light mayonnaise, American mustard, and a splash of water until you reach your desired consistency. To assemble, top each taco with shredded lettuce, finely diced onions, diced pickles, and a generous drizzle of burger sauce. Fold them up while hot and enjoy immediately. They’re crispy, highprotein, and way better than anything you’d get from a takeaway.

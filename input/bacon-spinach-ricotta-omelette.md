# Bacon Spinach Ricotta omelette

Servings: 1 | Calories: 329kcal | Protein: 35g | Carbs: 4g | Fat: 19g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients

- 3 large eggs
- 60g (2) bacon medallions (short-cut bacon)
- 40g Ricotta cheese
- (brand: Tesco) - sub with mascarpone or cottage cheese!
- 1 cup washed baby spinach salt and pepper chopped Chives (garnish) low cal cooking spray
- Notes Omelettes are super customisable! Feel free to sub in a different protein source such as chicken slices or a different choice of veg!

## Instructions

1. crack the eggs in a bowl, season with salt and pepper and whisk with a fork till beaten meanwhile dice the bacon into 1 cm cubes. Heat a pan up to a high heat, spray with low cal cooking spray and once hot add in the diced bacon and cook till crispy. Once the bacon is done, turn the heat down and add in the spinach and allow to wilt. Next spread the bacon and spinach out evenly in the pan before pouring in the beaten eggs. Tilt the pan to allow the eggs cover the bottom of the pan and allow to cook till partially set on the top. When the omelette is partially set on top add the low fat ricotta to one half and carefully fold the side without ricotta over on top. Allow the cheese to melt for 1 minute then turn the omelette over to cook for another 30 seconds on the other side. Serve hot straight away and optionally garnish with more fresh chives

# sausage & egg breakfast muffins

Servings: 6 | Calories: 375kcal | Protein: 31g | Carbs: 35g | Fat: 11g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients

- 400g (12) lean chicken breast sausages
- 6 English muffins
- 5 eggs
- 100g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 6 light laughing cow cheeses
- 6 slices light cheese single (brand: Aldi)
- 30g reduced sugar ketchup (brand: Heinz) salt and pepper Mixed herb seasoning Light cooking spray
- Into a greased shallow baking dish crack the eggs and add the Greek
- yoghurt, mix well till fully combined, then season with salt & pepper and some mixed herbs.

## Instructions

1. Bake the eggs at 200°C (400°F) for 5-8 minutes, keep checking on them so they don’t burn! Once cooked slice them into 6 even squares and set aside. Take the chicken sausages out the casing then mash them up in a bowl, generously wet your hands and shape out 6 even patties. Fry these off with some cooking spray for 2-3 mins on each side on a medium-high heat. To assemble, lightly toast the sliced muffin, spread 1 light laughing cow cheese on the base, then place the egg, the sausage patty, 1 light cheese single, and your top bun with a layer of ketchup, repeat for the other 5, Wrap in foil and refrigerate for up to 6 days or freeze to enjoy at your convenience. To reheat from the fridge, unwrap from the foil, wrap in a damp paper towel and microwave for 2-3 minutes till warmed through, flipping over at halfway. To reheat from frozen, remove the foil, wrap in a damp paper towel and microwave for 3-4 minutes, flipping over at halfway, enjoy!

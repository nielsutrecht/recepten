# Cheesy Chicken Taco Rice

Servings: 4 | Calories: 533kcal | Protein: 55g | Carbs: 54g | Fat: 9g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients

- 650g diced chicken breast
- 1 red bell pepper (diced)
- 1 medium onion (diced)
- 4 garlic cloves (diced finely
- 30g light butter (brand:

**lurpak light)**

- 1 packet taco seasoning (brand: old El Paso) or
- 25g homemade
- 220g salsa (brand: old El Paso)
- 200g washed white rice
- 400ml chicken bone broth (brand: freja)
- 120g low fat cheese (50% less fat cheddar) diced Tomato (garnish) chopped green onions (garnish)

**chopped parsley (garnish)**


## Instructions

1. Heat a pan over medium heat and melt the light butter. Add the diced onion and red bell pepper and cook until softened. Stir in the garlic and cook until fragrant, then add the diced chicken breast and cook until about halfway done. Sprinkle over the taco seasoning and mix well to coat the chicken evenly. Add the salsa, washed white rice, and chicken bone broth. Stir everything together, bring it to a gentle bubble, then cover with a lid and reduce the heat to low. Let it simmer for around 20 minutes, or until the rice is cooked and most of the liquid has been absorbed. Once cooked, sprinkle over the low-fat cheese and let it melt into the rice. Finish with diced tomato, chopped green onions, and parsley for freshness. Divide into four equal meal-prep containers and store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, microwave covered with a damp paper towel for 3–4 minutes, stir well, and enjoy.

# cinnamon sugar anabolic donuts

Servings: 6 | Calories: 66kcal | Protein: 4g | Carbs: 8g | Fat: 2g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**Dry ingredients**

- 60g oat flour
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- ½ tsp nutmeg tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp baking soda pinch salt

**wet ingredients**

- 1 large egg
- 80g 0% greek yoghurt
- 30ml unsweetened almond milk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tbsp granulated 0 cal sweetener (brand: pure via)

**Cinnamon sugar**

- 2 tbsp granulated 0 cal sweetener
- 1 tsp cinnamon

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 165°C (330°F) and lightly grease a donut pan. In a large bowl, combine the oat flour, cinnamon, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. In another bowl, whisk together the egg, almond milk, Greek yogurt, low-calorie sweetener, and vanilla extract until smooth. Gradually add the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients, stirring until just combined. Then transfer the batter into a large ziplock bag, cut a small hole in one corner, and pipe the batter gently into the donut pan filling each well evenly (about 1/2 to 2/3 full). Bake for 10-12 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into a donut comes out clean. While the donuts are baking, make the cinnamon sugar coating by mixing granulated sweetener, cinnamon in a small bowl. When the donuts have finished baking cool them just till you’re able to handle but still warm to the touch, this helps the coating adhere better. Dip each one into the cinnamon sugar coating, and enjoy your lower calorie cinnamon sugar donuts!

# Crispy bang bang chicken

Servings: 4 | Calories: 491kcal | Protein: 47g | Carbs: 69g | Fat: 3g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**chicken marinade**

- 600g chicken breast (diced)
- 250g fat free quark (brand:
- Tesco) - Sub with 0% Greek yoghurt
- 1 ½ tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp chilli powder lime juice
- Crispy coating panko bread crumbs salt and pepper to taste

**Bang bang sauce**

- 115g lighter than light mayo (brand: Hellmann’s)
- 60g reduced sugar sweet chilli

**sauce (brand: Blue Dragon)**

- 15g sriracha hot sauce

**other ingredients**

- 600g cooked basmati rice (200g uncooked weight)
- chopped spring onions -green parts only (garnish)
- chopped red chilli (garnish) lime wedges (garnish)

## Instructions

1. Dice the chicken into bite-sized pieces and add to a mixing bowl with the quark (or Greek yoghurt), garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, chilli powder, salt, pepper, and lime juice. Mix well to coat evenly and marinate for at least 30 minutes, or ideally 2 hours for best flavour and texture. In a separate bowl, mix the panko breadcrumbs with a little salt and pepper. Coat each piece of marinated chicken in the breadcrumbs, pressing down firmly to ensure full coverage. Lightly spray with low-calorie cooking spray, then bake or air-fry at 200°C (400°F) for 12–15 minutes, flipping halfway through for even crisping. While the chicken cooks, stir together the light mayo, reduced-sugar sweet chilli sauce, and sriracha until fully combined to make the bang bang sauce. Once the chicken is golden and crispy, serve it over a bed of cooked basmati rice (around 125g per portion). Drizzle over a quarter of the bang bang sauce and finish with chopped green onions, red chilli, and lime wedges if desired. If prepping ahead, store the chicken and rice together in meal prep containers, keeping the sauce in a separate container to avoid sogginess. Refrigerate for up to 5 days. When ready to eat, microwave the chicken and rice (without sauce) for 2–3 minutes, then add the sauce and heat for another 1–2 minutes until hot.

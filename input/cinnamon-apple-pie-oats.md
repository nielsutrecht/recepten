# cinnamon apple pie oats

Servings: 1 | Calories: 474kcal | Protein: 46g | Carbs: 56g | Fat: 5g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**apple pie filling**

- 45g rolled oats
- 180g 0% fat Greek yoghurt
- 65ml almond milk
- 25g vanilla protein powder (I use Protein Works French vanilla)
- 1(80g) regular gala apple
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp Vanilla extract
- 0 cal sweetener (I used stevia)

## Instructions

1. In a mixing jug, combine oats, Greek yogurt, almond milk, vanilla extract, cinnamon, maple syrup, and vanilla protein powder. Stir until everything's well mixed. Peel and dice an apple, adding 2/3 of it to the mixture, saving the rest for later. Pour the oat mixture into a mason jar or container, and top with the remaining apple cubes and a sprinkle of cinnamon and a tsp 0 cal sweetener. Seal the jar and store it in the fridge for at least 2 hours, or overnight. Enjoy!

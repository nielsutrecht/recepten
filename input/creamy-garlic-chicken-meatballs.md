# Creamy Garlic chicken Meatballs

Servings: 4 | Calories: 585kcal | Protein: 52g | Carbs: 68g | Fat: 12g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Chicken Meatballs**

- 700g lean ground chicken
- 1 egg
- 1/2 diced red onion
- 40g panko breadcrumbs
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp italian herbs
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp salt cooking spray

**Creamy Garlic Pasta**

- 300g dry linguine or spaghetti
- pasta (boil in salted water till al dente)
- 1/2 diced red onion
- 4 cloves garlic diced
- 250ml chicken bone broth
- 100g light cream cheese
- 10g grated Parmesan
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp oregano

## Instructions

1. Boil the pasta in salted water until al dente, then drain and set aside. In a large bowl, add the lean ground chicken, egg, diced red onion, panko breadcrumbs, smoked paprika, black pepper, Italian herbs, garlic powder, and salt. Mix well with your hands until everything is evenly combined, then shape the mixture into roughly 20 meatballs (about 5 per serving). Heat a pan over medium heat and spray lightly with cooking spray. Add the meatballs and cook, turning often, until golden brown on all sides and fully cooked through. Set them aside on a plate. In the same pan, add the remaining diced red onion and cook until softened. Add the garlic and let it become fragrant, then pour in the chicken bone broth. Add the light cream cheese and stir gently until melted and smooth. Mix in the grated parmesan, paprika, black pepper, and oregano, and let the sauce simmer for a few minutes until slightly thickened. Add the cooked pasta to the sauce and toss until fully coated and creamy. Return the meatballs to the pan or serve them over the pasta as you portion it out. Divide everything into 4 meal prep containers and store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, place into a microwave-safe bowl, cover with a damp paper towel, and microwave for 3–4 minutes until hot, stirring halfway. You can also reheat it in a pan if you prefer.

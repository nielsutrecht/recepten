# Cheeseburger quesadillas

Servings: 1 | Calories: 407kcal | Protein: 43g | Carbs: 16g | Fat: 19g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 100g 5% ground beef
- 1 low cal tortilla (Brand: Carbzone 112 cal tortillas)
- 40g reduced fat grated cheddar (brand: Aldi
- 50% less fat)
- 20g sliced pickles salt and pepper
- 20ml burger sauce (brand tesco) low cal cooking spray
- Notes These are a super quick and easy and high
- protein snack. Perfect if you are short on time or feeling lazy!
- To a pan on medium-high heat add some cooking spray and

## Instructions

1. allow it to heat up, then go in with your lean ground beef, press the beef flat with your spatula and let it sear for a 2-3 minutes before breaking it up, this will give the beef a delicious golden crisp! Once seared break it up and season it with salt and pepper (use any other seasonings you like too). Cook it off for around 56 minutes till brown. Set the cooked mince aside and place the tortilla in the pan to heat up for 30 seconds. Once the tortilla is warm, place the mince on one half, followed by an even topping of grated cheddar and sliced pickles. Drizzle over some burger sauce, then fold it in half and cook on both sides for 1-2 mins to melt the cheese and get it crispy. Once cooked slice it into four segments using a pizza cutter and enjoy!

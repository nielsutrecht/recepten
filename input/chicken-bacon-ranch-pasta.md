# Chicken bacon ranch pasta

Servings: 4 | Calories: 523kcal | Protein: 61g | Carbs: 50g | Fat: 7g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**Seasoned chicken**

- 500g chicken breast
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper cooking oil spray

**Ranch sauce**

- 250g 0% fat cottage cheese
- 60g lighter than light mayo
- 60ml chicken stock (brand: oxo)
- 40g low fat grated cheddar
- (brand Tesco: 50% less fat)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp dried dill
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp pepper Juice of half a lemon
- 2 tsp honey

## Instructions

1. Boil the pasta in salted water according to packet instructions. Drain and set aside. Dice the chicken breast and season with garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, salt, and pepper. Spray a pan with cooking oil and place over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and cook until golden and cooked through. Set aside to cool slightly. In the same pan, add the diced turkey bacon and cook until browned and slightly crispy. Remove from the heat.

2. other ingredients 180g Turkey bacon rashers (diced) you can also you regular centre cut or beef bacon 250g cooked fusilli pasta (dry weight - boil in salted water) chopped chives

3. Add the cottage cheese, mayo, chicken stock, low fat cheddar, garlic powder, dill, salt, pepper, lemon juice, and honey to a blender. Blend until completely smooth. In a large mixing bowl, combine the cooked pasta, chicken, bacon and chives. Pour over the ranch sauce and toss until everything is evenly coated. Divide into 4 equal portions and store in the fridge for up to 5 days. When it’s time to eat, add a tablespoon of milk and stir to loosen the sauce. Enjoy cold, or heat in the microwave for 3–4 minutes covered until warmed through.

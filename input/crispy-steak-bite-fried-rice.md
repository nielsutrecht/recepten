# CRISPy steak Bite fried rice

Servings: 4 | Calories: 588kcal | Protein: 54g | Carbs: 57g | Fat: 16g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Crispy steak bites**

- 800g lean steak (I used lean flank steak)
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp white pepper (sub with black pepper)
- 1 tsp baking powder (tenderises steak)
- 40g cornstarch cooking oil spray

**fluffy fried rice**

- 600g day old cooked white rice
- (refrigerate for at least 30

**minutes before if not possible)**

- 200g frozen mixed veg
- 30g green onions (white parts
- 3 eggs
- 45ml soy sauce
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil cooking spray

**garnishes**

- chopped green onions (green parts only)

**teriyaki sauce (optional)**

- Pat your steaks dry with a paper towel then dice into cubes and add to a
- bowl with salt, garlic powder, white pepper, baking powder and cornflour.
- Toss the steak well till each cube has an even coating.

## Instructions

1. Then heat a pan over high heat and spray with cooking oil. Add the steak in a single layer, cooking in batches if needed. Sear until golden brown and crispy on all sides, then remove from heat and set aside on a rack. Now in the same pan over medium-high heat add another spray of cooking oil. Add the white parts of the green onions and frozen mixed veg, stir-frying until softened. Push to one side, then crack in the eggs and scramble until just set. Add the chilled rice, breaking up any clumps, and stir-fry until heated through. Pour in the soy sauce, fish sauce, rice vinegar, and sesame oil, tossing everything together until well coated. Divide the fried rice and crispy steak bites into 4 equal meal prep containers and garnish with chopped green onions. and store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, add the contents of the prep to a pan over medium heat for 3-4 minutes until heated through. Drizzle with teriyaki sauce if using, and enjoy!

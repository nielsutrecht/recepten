# Crispy chicken tender wrap

Servings: 1 | Calories: 521kcal | Protein: 59g | Carbs: 42g | Fat: 10g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**chicken tenders**

- 1 (175g) chicken breast corn flakes (25g for tracking)
- 30ml your favourite hot sauce (I used sriracha)
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp paprika

**other ingredients**

- 1 low calorie tortilla (Brand: Carbzone) lighter than light mayo
- 30g reduced fat grated cheddar (brand: Aldi
- 50% less fat)
- 15g reduced sugar

**sweet chilli sauce (brand:**

- blue dragon) shredded lettuce tomato slices cucumber slices

## Instructions

1. Start by slicing the chicken breast into three tender-sized strips. In a bowl, combine your favorite hot sauce with garlic powder, onion powder, and paprika. Toss the chicken strips in this mixture, ensuring they're well coated. Next, add some cornflakes to a ziplock bag with salt and pepper, then crush and mix together. Pour the crushed cornflake mixture onto a plate and roll the chicken strips in it, ensuring each strip is evenly coated. Arrange the coated chicken tenders on a baking tray. Bake them at 200°C (or 400°F) for about 15-20 minutes. Alternatively, air-fry them at the same temperature for 12-15 minutes, flipping halfway for even crispiness. While the chicken is cooking, prepare your wrap and other fillings. Lay out a low-calorie tortilla and spread a thin layer of lighter-than-light mayo over it. Once the chicken tenders are done, let them cool slightly before assembling the wraps. Place the crispy chicken tenders on the tortilla, then top with grated cheddar, a drizzle of reduced sugar sweet chili sauce, and your desired amount of shredded lettuce, tomato, and cucumber slices. Roll it up into a wrap and enjoy your protein-packed crispy chicken tender wrap.

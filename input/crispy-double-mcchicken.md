# Crispy double mcchicken

Servings: 3 | Calories: 463kcal | Protein: 47g | Carbs: 43g | Fat: 11g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**seasoned chicken patties**

- 600g lean ground chicken (I
- made mine with chicken breast in a food processor)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp sage powder
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp 0 cal sweetener of choice

**mcchicken sauce**

- 70g light mayo (brand: Heinz lighter than light)
- 1 tsp American mustard
- 1 tsp garlic powder pinch of salt
- 1 tbsp water (adjust to consistency)
- coating stations (1, 2, and 3)
- 100g plain flour (not all will be used)
- 2-3 eggs beaten
- 150g cornflakes (not all will be used)

**other ingredients**

- 3 brioche burger buns (brand:

**aldi) shredded lettuce**


## Instructions

1. Start by seasoning your lean ground chicken with garlic powder, onion powder, sage powder, black pepper, salt, and your sweetener of choice (I used stevia). Mix well with your hands until the seasonings are well incorporated. Now, wet your hands and shape out 6 equal patties, about a cm wider in width than your burger buns (roughly 100g each). Place the patties on a lined baking sheet that will fit in your freezer, and freeze for 20-30 minutes to firm up. Meanwhile, make the McChicken sauce in a small bowl by combining light mayo, American mustard, garlic powder, a pinch of salt, and some water to adjust the consistency. Next, assemble your coating stations. On the first plate, add plain flour. On the second plate, crack 2-3 eggs and whisk to beat. On the third plate, add crushed cornflakes (you can blend them for 5-10 seconds or crush in a zip-seal bag). Now, take the patties out of the freezer and dip them first in the flour, then into the egg, and finally in the crushed cornflakes, pressing down gently to ensure an even coating. Place the patties on a rack, give them a spray with cooking oil, and air fry at 200°C for 12-15 minutes or, if using the oven, 200°C for 18-20 minutes, flipping the patties halfway. Finally, to assemble your McChicken, spread a dollop of the McChicken sauce on a toasted brioche burger bun, then add two crispy chicken patties followed by some shredded lettuce and the sauced-up top bun. Repeat for the other two McChickens and enjoy!

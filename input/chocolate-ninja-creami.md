# Chocolate ninja creami

Servings: 1 | Calories: 318kcal | Protein: 47g | Carbs: 25g | Fat: 4g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 300ml fat free (skimmed) milk
- 90g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 10g cocoa powder
- 20g sweetener of choice (I use Purevia sugar alternative)
- 35g chocolate protein powder (I use Protein Works) pinch of salt
- Notes You will need a Ninja Creami for this recipe!

## Instructions

1. Add all ingredients — skimmed milk, Greek yoghurt, cocoa powder, sweetener, chocolate protein powder, and a pinch of salt — directly into a Ninja Creami pint container. Use a milk frother to mix until smooth and fully dissolved. Alternatively, you can blend if preferred. Seal the pint and freeze for at least 12 hours until fully solid. Once frozen, run the sides and bottom of the pint under warm water for 20 seconds. Then add to the Ninja Creami on the “Lite Ice Cream” setting. Check the texture and scrape down the sides if necessary then run it on the respin function until smooth and creamy. Scoop and enjoy straight away, or top with extras like sugar-free chocolate chips or crushed protein bar chunks for extra crunch.

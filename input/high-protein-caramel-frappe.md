# High Protein Caramel Frappe

Servings: 1 | Calories: 280kcal | Protein: 32g | Carbs: 19g | Fat: 9g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**frappe**

- 320ml unsweetened almond milk
- ½ cup ice cubes
- 2 tsp instant coffee
- ½ tsp vanilla extract
- 20g chocolate protein powder (I use Protein Works)
- 20g caramel protein powder
- 1 tsp cinnamon

**toppings**

- 15g light squirty cream
- 15g caramel sauce (brand: carnation)

## Instructions

1. Add all the frappe ingredients to a blender: Almond milk, ice cubes, instant coffee, vanilla extract, chocolate protein powder, caramel protein powder, and cinnamon. Blend till smooth. Drizzle some caramel sauce around the edges of a pint glass then Pour in the blended frappe and top with whipped cream and little more caramel sauce on top, Enjoy!

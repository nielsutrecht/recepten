# High protein banana muffins

Servings: 6 | Calories: 233kcal | Protein: 10g | Carbs: 27g | Fat: 10g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**Dry ingredients**

- 200g self-raising flour (sub
- with 200g plain flour + 1 ½ tsp baking powder)
- 60g chocolate protein powder (I use Protein Works) ⅓ cup brown stevia
- 5g baking soda pinch salt

**wet ingredients**

- 2 over-ripe medium bananas
- 2 large egg
- 30ml unsweetened almond milk
- 65 ml vegetable oil

## Instructions

1. Notes These are super moist and fluffy muffins, one of my favourite high protein baking recipes!

2. Preheat your oven to 175°C (350°F). In a mixing bowl, add self-raising flour, chocolate protein powder, brown stevia, baking soda, and a pinch of salt. Whisk everything until combined. In a separate bowl, mash 2 overripe bananas. Add eggs, almond milk, and vegetable oil, then whisk until fully combined. Gradually mix the wet ingredients into the dry, stirring until combined. Transfer the batter to a lined muffin tray—this recipe makes 6 large muffins. Bake at 175°C (350°F) for 25-30 minutes. Let them cool on a rack for 5-10 minutes and enjoy!

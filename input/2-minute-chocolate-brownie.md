# 2 MINute Chocolate brownie

Servings: 1 | Calories: 297kcal | Protein: 26g | Carbs: 32g | Fat: 7g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 30g Oat flour (blended oats) sub with AP flour
- 5g cocoa powder
- 20g chocolate protein powder (I use Protein Works)
- 15g zero cal sweetener of choice pinch of salt
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 15g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 100ml skimmed (fat free) milk
- 7g chocolate chips (or sugar free ones if you want even less cals) cooking spray (to grease ramekin) protein ice cream (optional topping)

## Instructions

1. Add the oat flour, cocoa powder, chocolate protein powder, sweetener, salt, and baking powder to a bowl and mix well to combine. Add the Greek yoghurt and skimmed milk, then stir again until you have a smooth brownie batter. Lightly grease a microwave-safe ramekin with cooking spray and pour in the batter. Sprinkle the chocolate chips over the top. Microwave for 90–120 seconds, checking every 10–20 seconds to make sure it doesn’t overflow. The brownie should be just set on top but still soft and fudgy in the middle. Serve straight away while warm, or top with a scoop of protein ice cream if you want to take it to the next level.

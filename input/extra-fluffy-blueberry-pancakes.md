## Instructions

1. add the oat flour, egg, vanilla protein, baking powder, milk and vanilla extract to a mixing bowl. Mix just until a thick batter forms - don’t overmix, Then add in the blueberries and fold in carefully, trying not to crush the blueberries while doing this. Let the batter sit for 5-10 minutes to thicken up. Heat a pan on high heat with some low cal cooking spray and once hot turn the heat to medium low and carefully scoop in an ice cream scoop amount of batter into the pan Let the pancakes cook for 1-2 mins or until you see bubbles popping on the top, then flip it over and cook for a further minute (no longer). Repeat till the mixture is finished (keep the pan on medium-low to cook the rest). Serve up with a drizzle of sugar free maple syrup or your choice of toppings, Enjoy!

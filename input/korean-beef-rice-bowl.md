# Korean beef rice bowl

Servings: 4 | Calories: 483kcal | Protein: 43g | Carbs: 51g | Fat: 11g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Korean beef marinade**

- 30g oyster sauce
- 15g hoisin sauce
- 15ml rice wine vinegar
- 35ml dark soy sauce
- 2 tbsp brown stevia
- 1 tsp sesame oil

**(substitute with vegetable oil)**

- ½ tsp black pepper

**other ingredients**

- 700g 5% ground beef (brand: Tesco)
- 250g uncooked white rice (750g cooked weight) chopped green onions

**sliced red chilli peppers**

- Garnishes sesame seeds green onion tops
- Begin by cooking the rice to package instructions, then prepare the
- Korean beef marinade. In a jug or bowl, combine oyster sauce, hoisin
- sauce, rice wine vinegar, dark soy sauce, brown stevia, sesame oil,
- and black pepper. Mix these ingredients well and set aside.

## Instructions

1. Heat a pan over medium-high heat and coat it with cooking spray. Once hot, add the ground beef. Cook it until it's thoroughly browned. Once browned, reduce the heat to low, and add the prepared Korean beef marinade to the pan. Stir well to ensure the beef is evenly coated and simmer for 6-8 minutes, till the sauce thickens. Add sliced red chili and chopped green onion tops to the pan. Stir and cook for another 1-2 minutes, allowing them to soften slightly. Garnish with sesame seeds, then divide the korean beef & cooked rice equally into four meal prep containers. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days and when you’re ready to eat, add the contents of the prep to a pan over medium heat for 3–4 minutes until heated through. Or, reheat it in the microwave for 3–4 minutes, leaving the lid slightly cracked open. Enjoy!

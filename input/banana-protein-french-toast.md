# banana protein french toast

Servings: 1 | Calories: 486kcal | Protein: 48g | Carbs: 63g | Fat: 8g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**French Toast**

- 3 slices medium wholemeal bread
- 120ml or 3 large egg whites
- 50ml 2% milk
- 25g vanilla protein powder (I use Protein Works French vanilla) tsp cinnamon
- ½ medium banana low cal cooking spray

**Toppings**

- ½ banana
- 0 cal maple syrup (brand:

**Protein Works)**


## Instructions

1. In a blender cup add milk, egg whites, protein powder, half a banana, cinnamon and blend till smooth and creamy. Pour the mixture into a shallow dish and soak each slice of bread for around 1-2 minutes till the mixture is fully absorbed. Heat a pan over medium heat and lightly coat with cooking spray. Gently place the soaked slices into the pan and cook for 2-3 on both sides till golden brown, Top with the other 1/2 of the banana sliced up and some low calorie maple syrup.

# Loaded Cheeseburger bowls

Servings: 4 | Calories: 531kcal | Protein: 56g | Carbs: 43g | Fat: 15g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**cheeseburger beef**

- 800g lean ground beef (I used 5% fat)
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- ½ tsp cayenne
- 2 tsp pepper
- 2 tsp salt
- cooking oil spray (I use avocado)
- splash of water (30-60ml)

**crispy potatoes**

- 900g diced potatoes (optionally peeled)
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp pepper
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp parsley flakes cooking oil spray

**crunchy salad**

- ½ head of iceberg lettuce (thinly chopped)
- ½ red onion (diced)
- 80g sliced pickles
- 150g sliced cherry tomatoes salt to taste

**burger sauce**

- 80g low sugar ketchup (brand Heinz)
- 60g lighter than light mayonnaise (brand: hellmans)
- 8g American mustard
- 20-30ml water (adjust for desired consistency)

**other ingredients**

- 120g low fat cheddar cheese (brand:
- Tesco 50% less)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the air fryer to 180ºC/355ºF or the oven to 190ºC/375ºF. Toss the diced potatoes with salt, smoked paprika, pepper, garlic powder, and parsley flakes. Lightly spray with cooking oil. Air fry for 25-30 minutes or bake for 30-35 minutes, shaking or stirring halfway, until golden and crispy. Now heat a pan over medium-high heat and spray with cooking oil. Add the ground beef, let it get a crispy sear and break it apart as it cooks. Season with onion powder, garlic powder, paprika, cayenne, salt, and pepper. Add a splash of water and continue cooking until browned and fully cooked. For the salad, In a bowl, mix shredded iceberg lettuce, diced red onion, sliced pickles, and cherry tomatoes. Season with salt to taste. Lastly to make the sauce, mix ketchup, mayonnaise, mustard, and water until smooth. Adjust the consistency as needed. Assemble the bowls with the crispy potatoes, cheeseburger beef, and shredded cheddar. For eating straight away, Drizzle with burger sauce and serve with the crunchy salad on the side. For storage store the salad and burger sauce separately. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat add the contents to a pan and heat through on medium heat for 3-4 minutes. Drizzle with burger sauce and add the salad on the side. Enjoy!

# pepper steak rice bowls

Servings: 4 | Calories: 483kcal | Protein: 53g | Carbs: 52g | Fat: 7g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Crispy beef strips**

- 800g lean steak (sliced into thin strips)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp salt & pepper
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- cooking spray (I used avocado oil spray)

**pepper steak glaze**

- 60ml light soy sauce (preferably low sodium)
- 80ml water
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 5g garlic paste
- 5g ginger paste
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 30g brown stevia (brand: pure via) sub with any low-zero cal sugar alternatives
- 1 tbsp cornflour mixed with a tbsp of water
- Begin by cooking the rice according to the package instructions and set it
- aside. For easier slicing, briefly freeze the steak while the rice cooks.
- Thinly slice the steak against the grain into strips. Combine the strips in a
- bowl with soy sauce, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, black pepper, and
- baking soda to flavor and tenderize the beef. Ensure the beef is well-coated,
- then leave it to marinate briefly.

## Instructions

1. Prepare the pepper steak glaze by mixing soy sauce, water, rice vinegar, garlic paste, ginger paste, black pepper, brown stevia, and a slurry of cornflour with water in a separate bowl.

2. other ingredients 1 medium white onion (sliced) 1 red bell pepper (sliced) 1 green bell pepper (sliced) tbsp water (to help soften while stir frying) cooked steak strips pepper steak glaze 200g dry white rice (600g cooked weight) - divide between 4 preps

3. Heat a pan over high heat and coat with cooking spray. Add the beef strips, frying them until golden and tender. Remove from the pan and set aside. In the same pan, add sliced onions and bell peppers with a splash of water, cooking until soft. Add the steak strips back into the pan along with the pepper steak glaze, stirring continually until the sauce thickens and coats the steak strips. Divide the pepper steak and cooked rice into four meal-prep containers. These can be stored in the refrigerator for up to five days. When you're ready to eat, add a teaspoon of water over the top and microwave covered for 3-4 minutes. Enjoy!

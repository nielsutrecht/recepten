# creamy Cajun steak Alfredo

Servings: 4 | Calories: 612kcal | Protein: 57g | Carbs: 60g | Fat: 16g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**cAjun steak bites**

- 800g lean steak (I used flank steak)
- ½ cajun seasoning

**Cajun seasoning**

- 3 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp ground black pepper
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp cayenne

**creamy alfredo sauce**

- 250ml low fat milk (I used fat free)
- 80g light cream cheese (brand:

**Philadelphia lightest)**

- ½ Cajun seasoning
- 15 light butter
- 600g cooked & drained
- fettuccine pasta (300g uncooked)
- 30ml of pasta water

**other ingredients**

- 300g penne pasta (dry weight)

**fresh chopped chives (garnish)**


**grated parmesan (garnish)**

- To make the homemade cajun seasoning, in a small bowl, mix smoked paprika,
- garlic powder, black pepper, salt, onion powder, oregano, and cayenne.

## Instructions

1. Then dice the lean steak into 1" cubes and season with half of the Cajun seasoning. Heat a pan over high heat and spray with cooking oil. Add the steak bites in a single layer (cook in batches) and sear for 1-2 minutes per side until crispy and browned and cooked to your preferred doneness. Remove from the pan and set aside. Next cook the pasta in salted water until al dente, reserving some pasta water before draining. In the same pan used for the steak, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the milk, cream cheese, and the remaining Cajun seasoning, stirring until smooth. Add a splash of reserved pasta water and stir to incorporate and let the sauce thicken slightly. Add the drained pasta to the sauce, tossing until fully coated and creamy. Now divide the pasta and steak bites into 4 equal meal prep containers, and store in the fridge for up to 5 days. When you’re ready to eat, add the contents of the prep to a pan over medium heat for 3–4 minutes until heated through. Or, reheat it in the microwave for 3–4 minutes, leaving the lid slightly cracked open. Mix till creamy and enjoy!

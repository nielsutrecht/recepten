# Giant Choc CHip Cookie

Servings: 1 | Calories: ?kcal | Protein: 30g | Carbs: 30g | Fat: 17g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**Dry ingredients**

- 30g oat flour (finely blended oats)
- 5g almond flour (ground almonds)
- 30g brown stevia (brand:
- pure via) - Sub with normal 0 cal sugar alternative) pinch of salt
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 30g vanilla whey/casein blend protein powder (I use Protein Works)
- 5-10g chocolate chips (use
- sugar free if you can find them)

**wet ingredients**

- 28g melted light butter (brand: lurpak light)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 20g unsweetened apple sauce (I just blended no added sugar canned apple slices)

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, mix together the oat flour, almond flour, brown stevia, baking soda, and a pinch of salt. Add the melted light butter, apple sauce, and vanilla extract, then stir until everything starts coming together. Fold in the vanilla whey–casein blend protein powder to form a dough, then mix through the chocolate chips. You can eat this as edible cookie dough, but for the full cookie experience, cover the bowl and chill it in the fridge for an hour. Once chilled, scoop the dough onto a lined baking tray and shape it into one big cookie. Bake at 160°C/320°F for 10 minutes. Let it cool completely on the tray for 1–2 hours. As it cools, it sets into a soft, chewy, high-protein cookie that tastes unreal. Enjoy!

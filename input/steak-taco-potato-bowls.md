# Steak taco potato bowls

Servings: 4 | Calories: 468kcal | Protein: 55g | Carbs: 44g | Fat: 8g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**crispy potatoes**

- 900 potatoes (diced into 1” cubes)
- 2 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp salt and pepper

**Taco steak bites**

- 800g lean beef steaks (I use flank)
- taco seasoning (you can also use store bought)
- 1 tbsp olive oil

**taco seasoning**

- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp cayenne
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp coriander
- 1 tsp pink salt
- 1 tsp black pepper

**Creamy chipotle sauce**

- 140g 0% greek yoghurt
- 60g lighter than light mayo
- 1 tbsp chipotle chilli paste (brand:

**Tesco)**

- ½ lime juice
- 1 tbsp honey (optional for added sweetness)

**Crunchy salad**

- 2 cups lettuce shredded
- 1 medium red onion diced two salad tomatoes diced
- ½ large cucumber diced pinch of salt juice of ½ a lime

## Instructions

1. Dice the potatoes into 1 inch cubes then add them to a bowl. Season with paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Spray them lightly with cooking oil and mix well. Air-fry at 175°C (350°F) for 20-25 minutes, shaking halfway through for even crispiness. If using an oven, bake at 180°C (356°F) for 30-35 minutes. Now season the steaks with the taco seasoning blend and add a drizzle of olive oil. Rub well till the steaks are full coated. Cook the steaks in a pan on medium high for 3 minutes on each side till they get a golden crust, then rest for 5 minutes before slicing into bite size cubes. For the creamy chipotle sauce, in a blender cup add greek yoghurt, light mayo, chipotle chilli paste, lime juice and honey. Then blend till completely smooth and creamy. finally for the salad, in a large bowl mix chopped lettuce, diced red onion, tomatoes and cucumber, lime juice and a pinch of salt. toss together. Assemble the bowls with the crispy potatoes and taco steak bites. For eating straight away, Drizzle with burger sauce and serve with the crunchy salad on the side. For storage store the salad and burger sauce separately. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat add the contents to a pan and heat through on medium heat for 3-4 minutes. Drizzle with chipotle sauce and add the salad on the side. Enjoy!

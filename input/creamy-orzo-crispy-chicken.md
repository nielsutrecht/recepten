# Creamy Orzo & Crispy Chicken

Servings: 3 | Calories: 537kcal | Protein: 55g | Carbs: 41g | Fat: 17g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**crispy chicken**

- 500g chicken breast (3 fillets)
- salt, pepper and garlic powder to taste plain flour beaten egg bread crumbs (I used
- homemade, panko work well too) cooking spray

**Creamy orzo**

- 90g dry orzo pasta (boil in

**salted water till al dente)**

- 15g light butter
- 1/2 red onion (diced)
- 80g mushroom (diced) optional pinch of salt
- 3 cloves garlic (diced)
- 150ml part-skim milk
- 60g light cream cheese
- 150ml chicken stock juice of half a lemon black pepper to taste
- 1-2 tsp plain flour to thicken sauce
- 30g low fat grated cheese

## Instructions

1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil and cook the orzo until al dente. Drain and set aside. Season the chicken fillets with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Set up a simple coating station: plain flour, beaten egg, and breadcrumbs. Coat each piece of chicken in the flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs, pressing gently so it sticks. Place the chicken on a rack, spray lightly with cooking oil, and cook until golden and crispy. Air fry at 180°C (355°F) for 12–15 minutes, or oven bake at 200°C (400°F) for 18–20 minutes, flipping halfway. While the chicken cooks, make the creamy orzo. Melt the light butter in a pan over medium heat, then add the diced red onion and mushrooms (if using) with a pinch of salt. Cook until softened and lightly golden. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant. Pour in the milk and add the light cream cheese, stirring until melted. Add the chicken stock, lemon juice, and black pepper, then stir well. Sprinkle in the flour and continue stirring until the sauce thickens. Add the cooked orzo along with the low-fat grated cheese and mix until everything is creamy and well coated. Serve the creamy orzo topped with the crispy chicken. Divide into three meal-prep containers and store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, microwave covered with a damp paper towel for 3–4 minutes, stirring halfway, until hot.

# Creamy lemon pepper chicken

Servings: 4 | Calories: 447kcal | Protein: 50g | Carbs: 46g | Fat: 7g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**seasoned chicken**

- 800g chicken breast
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp salt & pepper
- 1 tbsp olive oil

**Lemon pepper sauce**

- 15g light butter
- 2 cloves minced garlic
- 120ml chicken stock (brand: oxo)
- 180ml light single cream

**(brand: ElmLea single light)**

- 10ml Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp lemon zest juice of a lemon

**sides**

- 200g uncooked basmati
- rice (600g cooked weight)
- 200g green beans
- Garnishes lemon wedges Fresh chopped parsley

## Instructions

1. Cook the rice according to package instructions. Once done, set it aside. Dice the chicken breast into cubes and season with onion powder, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper. Drizzle with olive oil and mix until the chicken is fully coated. Heat a pan over medium-high heat. Add the seasoned chicken and cook for 8-10 minutes until golden and crispy. Once cooked, remove the chicken from the pan and set it aside. In the same pan, turn the heat to medium. Add light butter. Once melted, add minced garlic and sauté for 1-2 minutes until fragrant. To the pan, add chicken stock, light single cream, Worcestershire sauce, lemon zest, and lemon juice. Stir to combine. Allow the sauce to simmer on low heat until it becomes thick and creamy. Once the sauce has reduced, turn off the heat. Add the cooked chicken back into the pan and stir to coat it in the sauce. Serve the creamy chicken divided into four equal portions. Accompany each serving with salt and peppered seasoned green beans (boiled or steamed) and 150g of the cooked rice. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat each prep, microwave with a teaspoon of water, partially covered, for 3-4 minutes. Enjoy!

# Hot Honey Chicken Tenders

Servings: 6 | Calories: 195kcal | Protein: 23g | Carbs: 23g | Fat: 1g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**chicken**

- 600g chicken breast (2-3 chicken breasts)

**Flour station**

- 100g plain flour
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp salt

**egg station**

- 1 beaten egg
- 1 tbsp pickle juice

**crispy station**

- 100g cornflakes (lightly crushed)

**Hot Honey glaze**

- 80g sriracha
- 60g honey Juice of half a lemon

## Instructions

1. Start by setting up your breading stations. On one plate, mix the plain flour with salt, onion powder, garlic powder, smoked paprika, and black pepper. In a separate bowl, beat one egg with a splash of pickle juice for the egg station. On a third plate, lightly crush the cornflakes for your crispy coating. Next, slice each chicken breast into three even strips. Working one at a time, coat each strip in the seasoned flour, then dunk into the egg mixture, and finally press firmly into the crushed cornflakes until fully coated on all sides. Repeat for all the tenders and place on a tray. Lightly spray with cooking oil. Air-fry at 180°C/355°F for 12–15 minutes or bake at 200°C/400°F for 18–20 minutes, flipping halfway through, until golden and crispy. While the chicken cooks, melt light butter in a small pan over low heat. Add sriracha, honey, and lemon juice. Stir and simmer gently until the sauce thickens slightly. Once the tenders are cooked and crisp, toss them in the hot honey glaze until fully coated. Serve hot with your favourite sides or dipping sauce.

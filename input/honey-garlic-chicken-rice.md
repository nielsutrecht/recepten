# Honey garlic chicken & rice

Servings: 4 | Calories: 496kcal | Protein: 48g | Carbs: 47g | Fat: 12g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Honey Garlic Chicken**

- 600g (4 medium) chicken breast
- 75ml light soy sauce
- 30g honey
- 3 cloves minced garlic

**Egg fried rice**

- 600g Day-old Refrigerated cooked basmati rice
- 3 large eggs
- 20ml light soy sauce
- 25g grated parmesan chopped spring onions (green parts only) sesame seeds (garnish)

## Instructions

1. Start by preparing the marinade. In a mixing jug, combine the light soy sauce, honey, and minced garlic. Stir well to ensure everything is well mixed. Place the chicken breasts in a bowl or oven dish and pour the marinade over them. Refrigerate and let the chicken marinate for at least 30 minutes, or up to 2 hours for enhanced flavor. After marinating, cook the chicken. Heat a pan over high heat and spray it with cooking spray. Cook the marinated chicken for 12-15 minutes or until it's golden and cooked through. Once done, set the chicken aside. In the same pan, reduce the heat to low. Crack and beat three eggs, then pour them into the pan. Gently scramble the eggs, stirring every 30 seconds until they're light and fluffy. Add the day-old cooked rice to the pan with the eggs. Break up any clumps beforehand if necessary. Pour in the light soy sauce and mix thoroughly. Cook until the rice is fluffy and has taken on a golden color. Garnish the egg fried rice with chopped green onions and a sprinkle of sesame seeds. Optionally slice the cooked chicken and serve it alongside or on top of the rice. Divide into four equal portions for meal prep, then store them in the fridge for up to 5 days. When you’re ready to eat, add the contents of the prep to a pan over medium heat for 3–4 minutes until heated through. Or, reheat it in the microwave for 3–4 minutes, leaving the lid slightly cracked open. Enjoy!

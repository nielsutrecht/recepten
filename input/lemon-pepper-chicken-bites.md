# Lemon pepper Chicken bites

Servings: 4 | Calories: 527kcal | Protein: 50g | Carbs: 57g | Fat: 11g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**crispy CHICKEN bites**

- 800g raw chicken breast
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp pepper
- 2 tsp salt
- 80g potato starch/cornstarch
- 1 tbsp lemon zest lemon juice cooking oil spray

**Lemon pepper sauce**

- 50g light butter (brand: lurpak lightly salted)
- 30g honey juice of 1/2 lemon
- 1 tbsp Lemon zest salt & pepper to taste

**Garlic potatoes**

- 900g potatoes (diced)
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp pepper
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp thyme
- 1 tsp oregano cooking oil spray

**garnishes**


## Instructions

1. Preheat the air fryer to 200ºC or the oven to 220°C/425ºF. Diced your chicken breast into cubes add to a bowl with paprika, garlic powder, salt, pepper and lemon zest. Mix well to season, then add potato starch and toss until evenly coated. Lightly spray the bites with cooking oil and air fry for 10-12 minutes or bake for 15-18 minutes, flipping and respraying halfway, until golden and crispy. For the garlic potatoes. Preheat the air fryer to 180ºC/355ºF or the oven to 190ºC/375ºF. Toss the diced potatoes with salt, pepper, garlic powder, thyme, and oregano. Lightly spray with cooking oil. Air fry for 25-30 minutes or bake for 30-35 minutes, shaking or stirring halfway, until golden and crispy. Lastly for the lemon pepper sauce: In a pan over low heat, melt the butter. Stir in honey, lemon juice, lemon zest, salt, and pepper. Simmer briefly to thicken, then remove from heat.

2. chopped green onions Once the chicken bites are cooked, toss them in the lemon pepper sauce until well coated. Now just divide the lemon pepper chicken bites and garlic potatoes into 4 equal meal prep containers. Garnish with chopped green onions and enjoy!

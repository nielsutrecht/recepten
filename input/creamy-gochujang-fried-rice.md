# Creamy gochujang fried rice

Servings: 5 | Calories: 545kcal | Protein: 53g | Carbs: 48g | Fat: 15g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Korean Chicken Thighs**

- 800g boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 2 tsp garlic powder tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp white pepper (or black pepper)
- 1 tsp ginger
- 2 tsp salt
- 75g gochujang paste
- 20g brown sugar (or brown sugar alternative) cooking oil spray

**Creamy gochujang sauce**

- 200g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 60g light mayo
- 30g gochujang
- 15ml rice vinegar
- 2 tsp honey Juice of half a lemon
- 1 tsp garlic powder Salt to taste
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

**Fluffy Fried Rice**

- 750g* cooked cold white rice (250g

**dry, cook beforehand and refrigerate)**

- 150g frozen mixed beg whites of green onions
- 3 beaten eggs
- 30ml soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1-2 tsp toasted sesame oil
- green onion tips (garnish) sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Start by prepping the chicken. Add the chicken thighs to a bowl and season with garlic powder, onion powder, ginger, white pepper, salt, gochujang paste, and brown sugar. Mix well until fully coated. Bake at 200ºC/400ºF for 20–25 minutes or air-fry at 180ºC/355ºF for 15–20 minutes until golden and cooked through. To make the creamy gochujang sauce, simply add all the sauce ingredients to a blender and blitz until smooth. Transfer to a bottle or bowl and store in the fridge until ready to use. For the rice, heat a wok over medium-high heat and spray lightly with cooking oil. Add the frozen mixed veg and sauté for a few minutes until soft. Push to one side of the pan, add the beaten eggs, and scramble gently until fluffy and just cooked. Add the chilled, day-old white rice straight into the pan and stir through. Pour in the soy sauce and oyster sauce, mix again, and finish with a drizzle of toasted sesame oil. Stir everything well to combine. Divide the rice evenly into five meal prep containers. Let it cool slightly before sealing, then refrigerate. To reheat, remove the lid and place a damp paper towel over the container. Microwave for 3–4 minutes until hot, give it a quick mix, then drizzle over the creamy gochujang sauce and optionally garnish with green onions. Enjoy hot!

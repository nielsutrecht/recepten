# cHilli cheese Loaded Fries

Servings: 4 | Calories: 574kcal | Protein: 44g | Carbs: 68g | Fat: 14g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Chilli con carne**

- 600g 5% ground beef
- 500g chilli con carne sauce mix (brand: lidl)
- 1 white onion (diced)
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp salt & pepper light cooking spray

**Crispy fries**

- 900g store bought fries
- (opt for the chunkier ones as they are less cals)
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 salt & pepper to taste

**Cheese sauce**

- 60g light cheese singles (brand: lidl)
- 20ml skimmed milk (2%)
- Garnish chopped green onions (green parts) sliced red chillis garlic mayo (optional)

## Instructions

1. Start by heating a pan over medium-high heat. Spray some light cooking spray and sauté the diced white onion until it becomes translucent. Add the ground beef, breaking it apart with a spatula, and cook until it’s nicely browned. Stir in the chilli con carne sauce mix, a teaspoon each of paprika, salt, and pepper. Let it simmer on low heat for 5-10 minutes until the mixture thickens. While the chilli is cooking, prepare your fries. Spread the fries on a baking tray and cook them according to the package instructions. Once cooked, toss them in a bowl with a teaspoon of paprika, salt, and pepper for added flavor. For the cheese sauce, tear the cheese slices into a microwave-safe bowl, pour in the milk, and microwave for 30-40 seconds. Stir every 10 seconds until it’s smooth and creamy. To assemble, divide the fries into four equal portions. Top each with a quarter of the chilli con carne mixture and a generous drizzle of cheese sauce. Garnish with chopped green onions, sliced red chillies, and if you like, a drizzle of garlic mayo. To reheat, transfer the loaded fries onto a square sheet of tin foil using a large spatula. Reheat in the air fryer or oven at 200°C (400°F) for 6-8 minutes. Warm the cheese sauce separately and pour it over the fries just before serving. Enjoy!

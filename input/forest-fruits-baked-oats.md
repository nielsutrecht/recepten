# forest fruits baked oats

Servings: 6 | Calories: 383kcal | Protein: 23g | Carbs: 53g | Fat: 9g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**Dry ingredients**

- 350g rolled oats
- 60g vanilla protein powder (I use Protein Works) ⅓ cup granulated

**sweetener (brand: pure via)**

- 10g baking powder pinch of salt

**Wet ingredients**

- 480ml almond milk
- 4 large eggs
- 130g 0% Greek yoghurt
- 25g honey
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

**Filling**

- 125g frozen mixed berries

**toppings**

- 120g greek yoghurt
- 25g frozen mixed berries
- 1 tbsp honey

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 180°C (360°F) and lightly grease a 12” x 9” (30cm x 23cm) baking dish. In one bowl, mix together the oats, vanilla protein powder, granulated sweetener, baking powder and a pinch of salt. In a separate bowl, add in 0% greek yoghurt, eggs, almond milk, honey, and vanilla extract, whisking until smooth. Gradually mix the wet ingredients with the dry, stirring until combined. Gently fold the frozen mixed berries into the mixture, ensuring they are evenly distributed. Pour the mixture into your prepared baking dish, spreading it out for an even bake. Top with 25g additional frozen mixed berries then bake at 180°C (360°F) for 30-35 minutes. Allow it to cool slightly before cutting into six portions. Store in refrigerated meal prep containers for up to six days. Mix greek yoghurt with honey to make the drizzle for topping, refrigerate separately from the baked oatmeal. When you’re ready to eat, reheat it in a heatproof dish and microwave for 2 minutes. drizzle on 20g of the greek yoghurt mixed with honey and enjoy!

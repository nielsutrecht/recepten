# popeye's style Chicken sandwich

Servings: 2 | Calories: 496kcal | Protein: 62g | Carbs: 48g | Fat: 6g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**Main ingredients**

- 1 Chicken Breast (230g)
- 2 brioche buns (brand: Aldi) sliced pickles
- station 1
- flour (15g for calculating macros)
- 1 tsp salt & pepper tsp garlic powder
- station 2
- 1 egg
- 1 tbsp sriracha
- station 3
- Crushed cornflakes (30g for the macros)

**jalepeño sauce**

- 120g light cream cheese

**(brand: philadelphia lightest)**

- 30g pickled jalepños
- 10ml sriracha
- 1 tbsp pickle juice (from pickle jar)
- 1 tsp garlic powder salt & pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Flatten and halve the chicken breast. Set up three dipping stations: first with a mixture of flour, salt, pepper, and garlic powder; second with a beaten egg mixed with a tbsp of sriracha; third with crushed cornflakes and paprika. Dip chicken into each station sequentially, pressing firmly at the third for an even crispy coating. Spray with low-calorie cooking spray, bake or air-fry at 190°C (375°F) for 12-15 minutes until golden. Prepare the jalapeño burger sauce adding to a blender light cream cheese, 30g jalapeños, tsp mustard, tsp ketchup, tbsp pickle juice, a garlic clove, fresh dill, lemon juice and blending until smooth. Lightly toast the brioche bun halves then assemble the sandwich with jalapeño sauce, crispy chicken, pickles, additional jalapeño sauce, and the top bun. Serve hot and Enjoy your Popeyes style crispy chicken sandwich!

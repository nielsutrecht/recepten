# Mongolian Fried Chicken

Servings: 4 | Calories: 550kcal | Protein: 46g | Carbs: 57g | Fat: 21g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Mongolian sauce**

- 60ml reduced salt soy sauce
- 80ml water
- 40g brown stevia (sub with

**regular granulated sweetener)**

- 2 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp (10g) cornstarch
- 10g sliced ginger
- 4 cloves minced garlic
- 1 red chilli (sub with bell pepper for less heat)

**crispy chicken**

- 800g diced boneless skinless

**chicken thighs (excess fat trimmed)**

- 60g cornstarch
- 2 tsp shaoxing wine (sub with rice vinegar) cooking spray

**Rice**

- 200g uncooked rice mixed sesame seeds (to garnish)

## Instructions

1. Start by making the Mongolian sauce. In a jug, add the soy sauce, water, brown stevia, sesame oil, and cornstarch. Mix well until smooth and set aside. Dice the chicken thighs into bite-sized pieces, trimming off any excess fat. Add the Shaoxing wine and cornstarch, then mix until the chicken is fully coated. Heat a pan over high heat with some cooking spray and cook the chicken in batches until golden brown and crispy. Remove from the pan and set aside. In the same pan, add the sliced ginger and red chilli and cook until softened. Add the minced garlic and stir until fragrant. Pour in the prepared sauce and cook, stirring constantly, until it thickens and becomes glossy. Add the crispy chicken back into the pan and toss until every piece is coated in the sticky Mongolian sauce. Finish with a sprinkle of mixed sesame seeds or green onions if you like. Serve with cooked rice (200g uncooked rice split across four portions). Divide into four meal prep containers and store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, microwave covered with a damp paper towel for 3–4 minutes, stir well, and enjoy.

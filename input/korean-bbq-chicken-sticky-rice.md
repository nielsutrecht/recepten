# Korean Bbq Chicken & Sticky rice

Servings: 4 | Calories: 655kcal | Protein: 50g | Carbs: 58g | Fat: 25g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**korean BBQ CHICKEN**

- 1000g boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 60g gochujang chilli paste
- 30g honey
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp white pepper
- 1 tsp ground ginger
- 2 tsp salt cooking oil spray

**sweet Sticky rice**

- 250g dry sticky or sushi rice (washed)
- 20ml rice vinegar
- 15g 0 cal sweetener of choice (or sugar) salt to taste 1/2-1 tsp
- optional steamed pak choi teriyaki sauce (garnish) black sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 200ºC/400ºF. In a large bowl, season the chicken thighs with gochujang paste, honey, garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, white pepper, ground ginger, and salt. Mix thoroughly until the chicken is evenly coated. Spread the chicken thighs out on a lined baking tray and bake for 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway through. For extra flavour and a slight char, finish under the grill on high for 5 additional minutes. To make the sticky rice, add 250g of washed sushi or sticky rice to a saucepan with 500ml of water. Bring to a gentle bubble, then reduce the heat to low and simmer for 15–20 minutes until the water is absorbed. Once cooked, turn off the heat, cover the pot, and let it sit for 10 minutes to steam. After resting, stir in the rice vinegar, your choice of sweetener or sugar, and salt to taste. Mix well to combine. Divide the sticky rice and Korean BBQ chicken evenly into 4 meal prep containers. Garnish each with chopped green onions and sesame seeds. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, microwave for 3–4 minutes until hot. For extra flavour, finish with a drizzle of teriyaki sauce just before serving.

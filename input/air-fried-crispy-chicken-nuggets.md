# air fried crispy chicken nuggets

Servings: per | Calories: 39kcal | Protein: 6g | Carbs: 2g | Fat: 1g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**seasoned chicken**

- 500g lean ground chicken (I made mine with chicken breast in a food processor)
- 1 large egg
- 30g grated parmesan
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- ½ tsp cayenne
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper

**flour station**

- 100g plain flour (40g for tracking) same seasonings used for the chicken

**wet station**

- 1 egg (beaten)

## Instructions

1. Start by seasoning your ground chicken with garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, cayenne, salt, black pepper, Parmesan, and 1 egg. Mix it well with your hands until the flavours are well incorporated. Now, wet your hands and shape out roughly 20-22 equal nuggets, placing them on a lined baking sheet that will fit in your freezer. Once you have all the nuggets, place them in the freezer for 20-30 minutes to firm up. Meanwhile, make your coating stations. For the wet mix, simply beat one egg in a bowl. For the dry station, mix plain flour with the same seasonings used on the chicken. Once the nuggets have firmed up, dip them first in the egg, then into the flour, and place on a rack. Give them a spray with cooking oil, then air fry at 200°C for 12-15 minutes in the air fryer, or 200°C for 18-22 minutes if using the oven, flipping them over halfway and spraying once more. Serve hot with your favourite dips and enjoy!

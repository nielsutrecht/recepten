# HONEY GARLIC CHICKEN WINGS

Servings: 4 | Calories: 512kcal | Protein: 48g | Carbs: 21g | Fat: 27g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**crispy chicken wings**

- 1000g chicken wings (separated into drums and flats)
- 30ml soy sauce
- 2 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 tsp ginger
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tsp pepper
- 80g cornstarch Cooking spray (I used avocado oil)

**Honey Garlic glaze**

- 25g light butter
- 30g minced garlic
- 35ml low sodium soy sauce
- 45g honey

## Instructions

1. Add the chicken wings to a large bowl with soy sauce, minced garlic, ginger, salt, and pepper. Mix well until evenly coated. Then add the cornstarch and toss again to form a light coating on all sides. Place the wings on a wire rack or directly into your air fryer basket. Spray lightly with cooking oil. Air-fry at 180°C/355°F for 15–20 minutes, or bake at 200°C/400°F for 25–30 minutes, flipping halfway through, until the wings are crispy and golden brown. While the wings are cooking, make the honey garlic glaze. In a small saucepan over low to medium heat, melt a knob of light butter. Add honey, soy sauce, and minced garlic, and stir until combined. Bring the sauce to a gentle bubble and let it simmer for 2–3 minutes until it thickens slightly into a sticky glaze. Once the wings are fully cooked, transfer them to a large bowl and immediately pour the glaze over the top. Toss the wings until fully coated and glossy. Serve hot with a sprinkle of green onions or sesame seeds if you’re feeling fancy – and enjoy!

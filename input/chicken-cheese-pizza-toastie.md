# Chicken & Cheese Pizza Toastie

Servings: 4 | Calories: 470kcal | Protein: 52g | Carbs: 25g | Fat: 17g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients

- 2 slices low-calorie bread (Warburtons Danish Light – 65 calories each)
- 100g pre-cooked seasoned chicken breast
- 30g low-fat cheddar (I used
- 50% less fat)
- 30g grated mozzarella (reduced-fat if you’ve got it)
- 20g pizza/tomato sauce Sprinkle of parsley flakes

## Instructions

1. Start by spreading tomato or pizza sauce evenly over both slices of low-calorie bread. In a small bowl, mix the cheddar and mozzarella together. Take one slice of bread and top it with 30g of the cheese mix, followed by 50g of finely diced, pre-cooked chicken breast. You can use store-bought chicken for ease, or prep your own ahead of time. Place the second slice of bread on top — tomato side facing up. Layer on another 40g of diced chicken breast, then sprinkle over the remaining 30g of the cheese mix. Add the remaining chicken cubes on top and finish with a sprinkle of parsley. Air-fry at 200ºC/400ºF for 4–5 minutes or bake at 200ºC/400ºF for 6–8 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbling and the toastie is golden. Slice and enjoy hot!

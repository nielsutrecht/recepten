# Chocolate PB Protein shake

Servings: 1 | Calories: 459kcal | Protein: 44g | Carbs: 37g | Fat: 15g
Section: snack

## Ingredients

- 300ml unsweetened almond milk
- 1 medium banana
- 30g peanut butter
- 20g cocoa powder
- 25g vanilla protein powder (I use Protein Works)
- 25g chocolate protein powder
- ½ cup ice

## Instructions

1. To a blender add the banana, almond milk, peanut butter, cocoa powder, vanilla protein powder, chocolate protein powder, ice and blend till thick and creamy. Pour into a pint glass and enjoy this Five Guys inspired, thick, creamy and protein-packed shake!

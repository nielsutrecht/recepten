# cinnamon dough balls

Servings: 10 | Calories: 52kcal | Protein: ?g | Carbs: ?g | Fat: ?g
Section: snack

## Ingredients


**Dry ingredients**

- 120g white bread flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp salt

**wet ingredients**

- 180g 0% Greek yoghurt Cooking spray or egg wash

**Cinnamon sugar**

- 2 tbsp granulated 0 cal sweetener
- 1 tsp cinnamon

## Instructions

1. Add the bread flour, baking powder, and salt to a bowl and mix to combine. Add the Greek yoghurt and stir with a spoon until the mixture forms clumps. Bring it together with your hands and knead briefly until you have a smooth dough. Transfer the dough to a lightly floured surface and roll it into a long, even log - this makes portioning way easier. Slice the log into 10 equal pieces, then roll each piece into a small dough ball. Place the dough balls on a lined baking tray and brush lightly with egg wash or spray with a little cooking oil. Bake at 190°C (375°F) for 13–15 minutes with fan, or 20 minutes without fan, until risen and lightly golden. You can also air fry them at 180–190°C (355–375°F) for around 10 minutes, depending on your air fryer. While they bake, prepare your cinnamon sugar coating. As soon as the dough balls come out of the oven, toss them immediately in the cinnamon sugar so it sticks perfectly. Enjoy hot and fresh - soft, sweet, and only 52 calories each with over 3g of protein.

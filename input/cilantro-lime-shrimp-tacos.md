# Cilantro lime shrimp tacos

Servings: 1 | Calories: 586kcal | Protein: 48g | Carbs: 68g | Fat: 14g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**for the tacos**

- 230g raw peeled king prawns or jumbo shrimp juice of 1 lime
- 1 tbsp cilantro (finely chopped)
- 2 cloves garlic (minced)
- ½ tsp cumin salt to taste
- 30g avocado (sliced into 9 thin
- pieces - 3 slices per taco)
- 3 mini tortillas (31g each) cooking spray

**the Slaw**

- 25g shredded carrot
- 35g shredded red cabbage
- 25g sliced red onion
- 20g lighter than light mayo
- 1/2 lime juice salt to taste

**Garlic lime mayo**

- 25g lighter than light mayo (brand; bramwells)
- 10g sriracha (or any hot sauce)
- 1 garlic clove minced juice of ½ a lime

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, mix together lime juice, chopped cilantro, garlic, cumin, and salt. Add shrimp, cover with plastic wrap, and let marinate for 20 minutes in the fridge. For the slaw, wash the shredded carrot, cabbage, and sliced red onion in cold water. Add them to a mixing bowl with lighter than light mayo, the juice of half a lime, and salt to taste. Toss gently to combine. To make the garlic-lime mayo, combine lighter than light mayo, sriracha, minced garlic, and the juice of half a lime in a medium bowl. Mix and season with salt. Heat a pan on medium-high heat. Cook and sear the marinated shrimp for 2-3 minutes on each side until pink and opaque. Toast the tortillas for 1 minute on each side until crispy but flexible. To build the tacos, add 25g of slaw, a few shrimp, 3 slices (10g) of avocado, and a drizzle of the garlic-lime mayo to each tortilla. Garnish each taco with cilantro, then serve and enjoy!

# Creamy Garlic Chicken & Potatoes

Servings: 4 | Calories: 546kcal | Protein: 55g | Carbs: 41g | Fat: 17g
Section: dinner

## Ingredients


**Crispy steak bites**

- 900g boneless skinless chicken thighs (fat trimmed)
- 1.5 tsp salt
- 1 tsp oregano
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp black pepper Cooking spray

**crispy potatoes**

- 1000g diced potatoes
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp dried parsley
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1.5 tsp salt cooking spray

**Creamy garlic sauce**

- 1 tbsp (15g) light butter
- 1/2 red onion (diced)
- 4 garlic cloves (diced)
- 150ml chicken bone broth
- 100g light cream cheese
- 10g Parmesan
- 80g low fat cheese (I used 50% less fat cheddar)

## Instructions

1. Dice the potatoes into bite-sized cubes, spray lightly with cooking oil, and season with smoked paprika, dried parsley, black pepper, and salt. Toss well, then air fry at 180°C (355°F) for 25–30 minutes or oven bake at 200°C (400°F) for 30–35 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and crispy. While the potatoes cook, dice the chicken thighs and season with salt, oregano, smoked paprika, onion powder, and black pepper. Heat a pan over high heat with some cooking spray and cook the chicken until it gets a deep golden sear and is cooked through. Remove from the pan and set aside. Lower the heat to medium and melt the light butter in the same pan. Add the diced red onion and cook until softened, then add the garlic and cook until fragrant. Pour in the chicken bone broth, add the light cream cheese and parmesan, and stir gently over low heat until the sauce thickens and turns creamy. Add the cooked chicken and crispy potatoes back into the pan and toss until everything is coated in the garlic sauce. Sprinkle over the low-fat cheese, cover with a lid, and let it melt into the sauce. Finish with a sprinkle of fresh parsley, then divide into four meal prep containers. Store in the fridge for up to 5 days. To reheat, microwave covered with a damp paper towel for 3–4 minutes, stir, and enjoy.

# garlic Bread cheeseburgers

Servings: 3 | Calories: 515kcal | Protein: 52g | Carbs: 30g | Fat: 21g
Section: lunch

## Ingredients


**beef patties**

- 600g lean beef mince (5% or less)
- salt & black pepper to taste
- 1 egg cooking spray

**garlic bread buns**

- 3 Aldi brioche burger buns
- 40g light butter
- 2 cloves garlic (finely diced) chopped parsley

**Burger sauce**

- 60g light mayo
- 40g reduced sugar ketchup
- 8g American mustard
- 15g chopped pickles
- 10g chopped onion black pepper, paprika to taste

**other ingredients**

- 3 light cheese singles (brand:

**Tesco) shredded lettuce**

- Begin by seasoning your lean ground beef with salt and pepper to taste.

## Instructions

1. Add one egg, then mix it well with your hands to incorporate, and divide the beef into six equal balls (roughly 110g each). For the burger sauce, in a bowl, combine light mayo, reduced sugar ketchup, American mustard, chopped pickles, chopped onion, black pepper, and paprika to taste. In a small bowl, melt some light butter in the microwave, then add diced garlic and chopped parsley, mixing well. Spread the garlic butter evenly on all the buns. Add them to a pan on medium heat, pressing down with a spatula or burger press, and toast for 1-2 minutes. Set the buns aside, then turn the pan up to high heat. Add some cooking spray and place two burger balls in the pan. Smash them down with a burger press and sear for 2-3 minutes on each side until golden and crispy. Place a light cheese single slice on one of the patties and layer the other patty on top. To assemble the burgers, add some shredded lettuce to the bottom bun, followed by the double patty, a tablespoon of burger sauce, and the top bun. Repeat for the other two burgers and enjoy!

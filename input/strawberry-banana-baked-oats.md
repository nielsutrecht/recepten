# Strawberry & banana baked oats

Servings: 6 | Calories: 376kcal | Protein: 21g | Carbs: 58g | Fat: 9g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**Dry ingredients**

- 350g rolled oats
- 60g vanilla protein powder (I use Protein Works)
- ⅓ cup granulated sweetener (brand: pure via)
- 10g baking powder
- 1 tsp cinnamon pinch of salt

**Wet ingredients**

- 480ml almond milk
- 4 large eggs
- 2 medium bananas
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

**Filling**

- 50 fresh strawberries
- 100g frozen strawberries

**toppings sliced banana (optional)**


**sliced strawberries (optional)**


## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 180°C (360°F) and lightly grease a 12” x 9” (30cm x 23cm) baking dish. In one bowl, mix together the oats, vanilla protein powder, Granulated sweetener, baking powder, cinnamon, and a pinch of salt. In a separate bowl, mash the bananas. Add in eggs, almond milk, honey, and vanilla extract, whisking until smooth. Gradually mix the wet ingredients with the dry, stirring until well combined. Dice 50g of fresh strawberries and fold them into the mixture along with 100g of frozen strawberries, ensuring they are evenly distributed. Pour the oatmeal mixture into the prepared baking dish, spreading it out for an even bake. Optionally top with additional sliced banana and strawberries then bake at 180°C (360°F) for 30-35 minutes. Allow it to cool slightly before cutting into six portions. Store in refrigerated meal prep containers for up to six days. When you’re ready to eat, reheat it in a heatproof dish and microwave for 2 minutes Enjoy your high protein strawberry and banana baked oatmeal! BONUS: Some reduced-fat custard on top takes this to another level! ((50g on each prep - adds roughly 40 cals)

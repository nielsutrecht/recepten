# Blueberry muffin baked oats

Servings: 6 | Calories: 380kcal | Protein: 21g | Carbs: 57g | Fat: 9g
Section: breakfast

## Ingredients


**Dry ingredients**

- 350g rolled oats
- 60g vanilla protein powder (I use Protein Works)
- ⅓ cup granulated sweetener (brand: pure via)
- 10g baking powder
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp nutmeg pinch of salt

**Wet ingredients**

- 480ml almond milk
- 4 large eggs
- 2 medium bananas
- 1 tbsp honey

**Filling**

- 150g frozen blueberries

**toppings**

- 25g fresh blueberries

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 180°C (360°F) and lightly grease a 12” x 9” (30cm x 23cm) baking dish. In one bowl, mix together the oats, vanilla protein powder, granulated sweetener, baking powder, cinnamon, nutmeg and a pinch of salt. In a separate bowl, mash the bananas. Add in eggs, almond milk, honey, and vanilla extract, whisking until smooth. Gradually mix the wet ingredients with the dry, stirring until combined. Gently fold the frozen blueberries into the mixture, ensuring they are evenly distributed. Pour the mixture into your prepared baking dish, spreading it out for an even bake. Top with 25g additional blueberries then bake at 180°C (360°F)for 30-35 minutes. Allow it to cool slightly before cutting into six portions. Store in refrigerated meal prep containers for up to six days. When you’re ready to eat, reheat it in a heatproof dish and microwave for 2 minutes. Enjoy! BONUS: This also works perfectly with some reduced-fat custard on top! (50g on each prep - adds roughly 40 cals)
